## Excluded classes (owner decisions D-002, D-003, D-004; 2026-10-23)

**Why these classes are out (D-002):** each processes a canonical mineral only as a high-volume secondary or downstream commodity (scrap copper, ferro-silicon for steel, antimonial lead, phosphoric acid for fertiliser) and numbers in the hundreds of small units. Including them would bury the strategic midstream projects the map exists to show; their registry counts are kept below so the scale stays visible.
