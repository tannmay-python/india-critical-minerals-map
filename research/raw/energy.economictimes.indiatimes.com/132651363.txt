IREL’s 4k cr rare earth JV in TN to generate 4k jobs
The project will produce rare earth oxides, a critical raw material for manufacturing permanent magnets used in EVs, wind turbines, aerospace, defence and other strategic sectors.

IREL (India) Ltd’s proposed integrated 
rare earth mining
 and processing facility at Tuticorin will entail an investment of about 4,000 crore, generate around 4,000 direct and indirect jobs, CMD S B Mohanty told TOI. It would also lay the foundation for a domestic rare earth 
permanent magnet supply chain
, Mohanty said.
The project will produce 
rare earth oxides
, a critical raw material for manufacturing permanent magnets used in 
EVs
, wind turbines, aerospace, defence and other strategic sectors. It is also expected to have a significant clustering effect by attracting major midstream and downstream industries to locate close to the source of raw materials and technology.
Advt
“The corridor will supply the foundational materials needed to develop both the oxide-to-magnet ecosystem, which is critical for EVs and wind turbines, and the titanium value chain for aerospace and defence. Beyond mineral extraction, the state stands to benefit from substantial economic gains driven by industrial clustering,” he said.
Mohanty said the project would supply rare earth oxides, complementing the country’s efforts to build a domestic permanent magnet supply chain.
