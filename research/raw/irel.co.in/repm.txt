 REPM – Rare Earth Permanent Magnet (VIZAG) 
IREL in the process of setting up a plant producing Rare Earth Permanent Magnet (REPM) at Visakhapatnam based on Indigenous Technology.
The proposed REPM production facilities shall be of strategic importance to the Nation and is a Make in India initiative.
