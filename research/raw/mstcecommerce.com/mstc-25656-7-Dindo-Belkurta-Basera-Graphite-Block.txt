        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station is Renukut (86 km) on the Howrah–Delhi line
                          under the East-Central Railway Zone; Ambikapur Railway Station (92 km)
                          under the Southeast Central Railway Zone also provides rail connectivity.
                                                                                  
          Road            The area is well connected by metalled roads and is accessible from
                          Wadraf Nagar (45 km), Ramanujganj (52 km), and Ambikapur.
          Airport         The nearest major airport is Swami Vivekananda Airport, located at an
                          approximate distance of 330 km (Approx).                
        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface The area shows a general easterly slope, drained by small nalas including
          Drainage Pattern, Kursa Nadi and Lothia Nala flowing towards the northeast and southeast
          Rivers, etc     through open scrub jungle.                              
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall Average rainfall of about 150 cm.                  
          Temperature     The highest temperature reaches up to 45°C in the month of May and
                          lowest temperature goes to 5° in winter.                
                                                                                  
        9 Topography                                                              
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 64M/05                   
          Morphology of the The terrain is predominantly hilly and undulating, with shallow valley
          area            plains around village areas. In the central part, the hills trend nearly E–W,
                          attaining a maximum elevation of about 550 m above mean sea level near
                                                                                  
                          Basera village in the southeastern part. The average reduced level (RL) of
                          the flat areas is around 415 m above mean sea level.
