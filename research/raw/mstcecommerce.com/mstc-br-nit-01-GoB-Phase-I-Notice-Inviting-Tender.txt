                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                    
            “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone and Iron Ore (Magnetite)
                                                                                    
                                       minerals”                                    
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development and
          Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, as
          amended from time to time, the Government of Bihar has identified:        
                                                                                    
              03 mineral blocks (1 block of Limestone, and 2 blocks of Iron Ore (Magnetite))for electronic
               auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.

          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in digital
          and physical format from eligible bidders.                                
                                                                                    
          Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms, and conditions etc. for participating in the electronic auction
          are provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications,
          updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form
          only and can  be downloaded from the e-auction portal of MSTC Limited     
          (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).               
                                                                                    
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above e-auction portal. On successful
                                                                                    
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction
          process. Interested and eligible bidders can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the
          Tender Document on payment of the Tender fee from the above e-auction portal. Any revision,
