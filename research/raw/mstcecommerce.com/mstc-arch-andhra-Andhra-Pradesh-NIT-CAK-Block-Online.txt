          document. The model tender document and mineral block summaries are available free of cost and can

              Interested and eligible participants can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the
          tender document from the website of MSTC Ltd. Bidder shall be able to make payment of tender
          document fee through the payment gateway of e-auction platform. After purchase of the tender
          document, eligible bidders can register themselves on MSTC’s website. On successful registration, bidders
          will obtain Login ID and Password necessary for participation in e-auction.
              Last day for online purchase of tender document is 15:00 Hrs IST on 01.10.2018 and the last date
          for submission of the bid is 15:00 Hrs IST on 04.10.2018. Any further communication, amendments etc.
          shall be published on website of MSTC.
