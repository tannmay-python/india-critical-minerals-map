                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone mineral and for grant of Composite
          Licence for Copper mineral, Gold associated with Silver, Lead & Copper, Copper & associated
                                                                                  
          mineral, and Iron Ore minerals”                                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B and Section 11 of the Mines and Minerals
          (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015
                                                                                  
          notified thereunder, the Government of Maharashtra has identified:      
          a)  02 (two) blocks of Limestone mineral for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids for the
              purpose of grant of Mining Lease.                                   
                                                                                  
          b)  07 (seven) mineral blocks [1 (one) block of Copper, 1 (one) block of Copper & associated
              mineral, 1 (one) block of Gold associated with Silver, Lead & Copper) and 4 (four) blocks of
                                                                                  
              Iron ore] for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids for the purpose of grant of Composite
              Licence.                                                            
                                                                                  
          The list of mineral blocks is as under:                                 
          S. No. Name of Block                Mineral        Concession Type      
            1.   Matna Iron ore Block         Iron ore      Composite Licence     
                                                                                  
            2.   Ambadgaon Iron ore Block     Iron ore      Composite Licence     
            3.   Vazare Iron ore Block        Iron ore      Composite Licence     
            4.   Tale Iron ore Block          Iron ore      Composite Licence     
                                                                                  
            5.   Lal Heti Copper Block        Copper        Composite Licence     
                                        Gold associated with Silver,              
            6.   Bhimsain Killa Gold Block*                 Composite Licence     
                                            Lead & Copper                         
            7.   Miregaon Copper Block Copper and Associated Mineral Composite Licence
            8.   Bori Limestone Block        Limestone        Mining Lease        
            9.   Javra Limestone Block       Limestone        Mining Lease        
          Note: The abovementioned block marked with “*” is under “second” attempt.
          The price of Tender Document for each mineral block is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) exclusive of
          GST. The GST shall be payable by reverse charge mechanism.              
                                                                                  
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in digital
          and physical format from eligible bidders.
