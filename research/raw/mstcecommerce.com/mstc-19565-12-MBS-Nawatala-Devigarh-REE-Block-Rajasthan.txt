         4   Exploration Details                                                  
         i.  Regional Geology        The EL block in and around Nawatala, Devigarh areas of Barmer
                                     and Jodhpur districts, Rajasthan is close to Siwana Ring
                                     Complex with similar geological setup of Malani Volcanics.
         ii. Geochemical Investigation The ground water samples collected from the proposed
                                      Nawatala, Devigarh areas show encouraging values of REE
                                      (La+Ce), which may indicate presence of peralkaline rocks of
                                      Malani Igneous Suite. The value of Mg in ground water sample
                                      about 317 ppb and high value of copperupto551.36ppb indicate
                                      presence of basic rocks and presence of concealed source of
                                      copper below the aeolian sand respectively. 
         5   Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                    
         i.  Mineral Commodity        REE and associated mineralization           
         ii  Exploration Potential of Block The EL block in and around Nawatala, Devigarh areas of Barmer
                                     and Jodhpur districts, Rajasthan is close to Siwana Ring
                                     Complex with similar geological setup, known for REE
                                                                                  
                                     mineralization associated with Malani Igneous Suit. The
                                     regional trend of rocks of Malani Volcanic suite reported from
                                     the adjacent areas indicates possible extension of these rocks
                                     below the aeolian soil cover within the block.
                                          The ground water samples collected from the Nawatala,
                                     Devigarh areas show encouraging values of REE (La+Ce) which
                                     may indicate presence of peralkaline rocks of Malani Igneous
                                     Suite. The high Mg value of 317 ppb in ground water samples
                                     indicates that country rock might be basic in nature. Similarly,
                                     high value of copperupto551.36 ppbin ground water samples
                                     indicates the presence of Malani volcanics.  
                                           Aeromagnetic map of the area is showing NW-SE
                                      (south-eastern part of the block) and N-S (in the central portion
                                      of the block) trending two high magnetic linears. Similar
                                      aeromagnetic responses have been also observed from the
                                      nearby Siwana Ring Complex, which is well known for its
                                                                                  
                                      REE and RM potential. The NGPMMagnetic (TF) anomaly
                                      contour map is well corroborating with the aeromagnetic
                                      anomaly. The proposed EL Block lies over a gravity gradient
                                      in NGPM Bouguer Gravity anomaly map.
