         4  Exploration Details                                                   
                                                                                  
         i. Regional Geology         The major rock units of the area are composed of various types
                                     of granites intruded at different periods which constitute a
                                     composite batholith with xenolithic inclusions and inliers of
                                     metasediments and metabasites which belong to the Sakoli
                                     Group. Dykes and sills of granophyre, quartz-porphyry and
                                     quartz-plagioclase porphyry, which possibly represent the
                                     hypabyssal and volcanic equivalents of the granite pluton, occur
                                     intimately associated with the granites and gneisses in a
                                     sporadic manner. Uralite-gabbro and metadolerite dykes, quartz
                                     pegmatite veins and quartz veins, occur as intrusive in the older
                                     rocks.

         5  Quantity of Minerals (Grade-                                          
            wise)                                                                 
         i. Mineral Commodity        Diamond                                      
                                                                                  
         ii Exploration Potential of Block  The historical significance of diamond mining near
                                        Wairagarh, supported by extensive old workings and debris
                                        piles, strongly indicates the potential of the area for
                                        diamond exploration.                      
                                       Previous investigations have confirmed the presence of
                                        kimberlite indicator minerals in the region, indicating
                                        favourable potential for diamond exploration. Stream
                                        sediment studies have recorded the occurrence of chrome
                                        diopside and pyrope garnet, along with chrome spinel and
                                        G5 garnets within the Deorighat Nala block. Notably,
                                        spinels exhibiting Cr₂ O₃ concentrations exceeding 52%,
                                        corresponding to S4 and S5 compositional fields, suggest
                                        the presence of kimberlite-affinity rocks in the upstream
                                        areas. These findings collectively reinforce the diamond
                                        exploration potential of the region.      
                                       Furthermore, exploration activities have demonstrated
                                        significant potential in alluvial, colluvial, and weathered
                                        bedrock deposits, as well as within laterite profiles suggest
                                        the potential presence of Kimberlite affinity rocks, as source

                                     located approximately 180 km from Nagpur and 80 km
                                     from Chandrapur, with district roads linking it to adjoining
                                                                                  
                                     districts such as Bhandara, Chandrapur, and Nagpur.

                                     roads facilitates smooth travel for residents and visitors
