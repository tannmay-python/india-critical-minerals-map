       The mineral concession of the blocks will be granted in accordance with the Honourable Supreme Court's order dated
       May 9, 2024, in Writ Petition (Civil) No. 4677/1985, M.C Mehta vs Union of India & ORS with writ petition (s) (civil)
       no. 202/1985, T N Godaverman v/s Union of India & ORS or any subsequent order issued in these petitions, if
       applicable.                                                                
          The  interested and eligible bidders, upon registration on MSTC website 
       <http://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp> will obtain login ID and Password. After successful
       registration, the eligible bidder can purchase the Tender Document online on payment of the tender fee of INR Rs.
       2,00,000/- (Indian Rupees Two Lakh) and can participate in the e-auction process.
       If a bidder had already purchased the tender document during the first attempt of auction for a specific block, then in
       the second attempt of auction, the tender document will be available for download to that same bidder only for the
       same block, upon payment of a non-refundable Tender Document fee of INR 25,000/- (Indian Rupees Twenty-Five
       Thousand Only).                                                            
       The Parewar (SN-IV) Block, District Jaisalmer (mentioned at serial no. 9), which was earlier notified for auction vide
       NIB dated 16.04.2025 and subsequently cancelled, is now being put up for auction afresh. All prospective bidders are
       required to obtain the tender document afresh as per the terms and conditions. However, bidders who had already
       purchased the tender document for the said block during the earlier auction process shall be exempted from paying the
       tender document fee again.                                                 
       The GST at the rate of 18% is applicable on the purchase of tender document. The GST applicable is to be submitted
       by the prospective bidder itself and the copy of receipt is to be attached with the technical bid. The prospective bidder
       shall be solely responsible for remitting the GST as per applicable law and statutory liability in this regard will be
       responsibility of prospective bidder and no liability whatsoever shall be borne by the DMGR/MSTC.
