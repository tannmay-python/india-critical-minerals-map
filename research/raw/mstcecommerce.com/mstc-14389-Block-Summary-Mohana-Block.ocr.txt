6  ACCESSIBILLITY
FEATURES  DETAILS
NEAREST RAIL HEAD  Gwalior (approximately 60 km north from the block)
ROAD  The area is well connected by roads. The nearby areas  are
intersected by the NH-3, NH-25 and NH-75, connecting
Gwalior to Shivpuri, Gwalior to Jhansi and Jhansi to Shivpuri,
respectively. NH-3 (also called A-B road/ Agra-Mumbai Road)
is passing through the western part of the toposheet no. 54G/13
and 54G/14. Gwalior and Shivpuri are the two big cities located
60 km north and south of Mohana respectively. The area is
accessible by metalled and unmetalled roads, cart and foot
tracks which are either originated from or linked to NH-3 and
Mohana-Aron-Harsi-Narwar Road.
AIRPORT  The nearest airport is Gwalior, which is 72 km north from
Mohana.

9  TOPOGRAPHY
TOPOSHEET NUMBER  54G/13 and 54G/14
MORPHOLOGY OF THE  Geologically the Eastern and southeastern part of the area is
AREA  underlain by Bundelkhand Granite Complex which forms a
peneplained terrain with a few isolated hillocks and ridges
mostly made up of quartz reefs. On the west, Bundelkhand
Granite is overlain by the rocks of the Vindhyan Supergroup,
which forms steep escarpments. At places, they form flat and
slightly undulatory plateaus over the granites. The elevation of
the area ranges from slightly below 240 m. to above 428 m.
from mean sea level. Geomorphologically, the topography of the
study area has been described as structural origin, denudation
origin (pediment and pediplain complex) and anthropogenic
origin.
Structurally area is divided in to three units
I). Moderately dissected hills and valleys (towards north and
northeast of the ParbatiRiver.
II). Low dissected hills and valleys (towards north-west of the
study area).
IIl).Moderately dissected lower plateau (towards west-southwest
of the area)
