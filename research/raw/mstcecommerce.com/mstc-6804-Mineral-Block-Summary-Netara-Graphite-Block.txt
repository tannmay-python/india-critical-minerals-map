                 Highlights of Ground In the Netara-Khattali-Bari-Phata area the induced
                 Geophysical Survey Polarization survey have successfully demarcated two
                                   high chargeability zones, one is linear chargeability zone
                                   following strike of geological unit from traverse line 400
                                   to -900 and another one is U shape chargeability from
                                   traverse line -1100 to -1600. The high chargeability zone
                                   with chargeability varying from 22mv/v to 26mV/V and
                                   24 mv/v to26 mv/v respectively. SP value ranging from -
                                   60 Mv to 40Mv in Northern zone and 60Mv to 140Mv in
                                   southern zone and apparent resistivity of 00ohm-m to
                                   200ohm-m in both zones together with low magnetic
                                   anomaly gives confirmatory indication of the presence of
                                   graphite on this zone.                           
                                   The overlay of IP/chargeability, magnetic and apparent
                                   resistivity contour maps with geology has indicated the
                                   lithological control in the area. The integration of
                                   IP/chargeability contour map with geological map reveals
                                   that the Netara graphite schist band demarcated by
                                   geological mapping is lying well within the high

                 Highlights of  the The graphite mineralisation in the area is mainly
                 Petrographical/   associated with carbon phyllite belonging to Kalinjara
                 Mineragraphic Studies Formation of Meghnagar Group of Aravalli Supergroup,
                                   which often grades into graphite schist at places. The
                                   graphite schist / carbonaceous phyllite occur as lensoidal
                                   bodies, concordant linear bands and sometimes as a
                                   pocket in the area of investigation. The thickness of the
                                   graphite schist band ranges from <1m to 25m along the
                                   general foliation direction of the lithounits. The graphite
                                   mostly occurs as flaky and amorphous variety in the area.
                                   The flaky variety of graphite mainly comprises of flakes
                                   which are silver grey to greyish black in color. In hand
                                   specimen its appears greyish black to silver grey in color
                                   and usually soils hand with black to silver grey color. The
                                   amorphous variety of graphite has a dull lusture,
                                   comparatively hard, massive.
