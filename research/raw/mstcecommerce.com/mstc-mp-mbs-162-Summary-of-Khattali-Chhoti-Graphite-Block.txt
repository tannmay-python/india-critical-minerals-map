               Highlights of     A total of 5 nos. of BRS samples were collected from the
               Geochemical Survey Khattali Chhoti block and analysed for Fixed Carbon in
                                 which graphite yielded fixed carbon ranges from 1.99 to
                                 6.50 %.                                          
               Highlights of Ground Details Ground Geophysical Survey carried out in Netara-
               Geophysical Survey area the induced Polarization survey and demarcated two
                                 high chargeability zones, one is linear chargeability zone
                                 following strike of geological unit and another one is U
                                 shape chargeability. The high chargeability zone with
                                 chargeability varying from 22mv/v to 26mV/V and 24
                                 mv/v  to26 mv/v respectively. The overlay of     
                                                                                  
                                 IP/chargeability, magnetic and apparent resistivity
                                 contour maps with geology has indicated the lithological
                                 control in the area. The integration of IP/chargeability
                                 contour map with geological map reveals that the Netara
                                 graphite schist band demarcated by geological mapping is
                                 lying well within the high chargeability zone of 20mv/v to
                                 24mv/v demarcated by the geophysical IP survey in the
                                 northwestern part of Netara graphite schist band. The
                                 demarcated chargeability axis is almost parallel to the
                                 graphite schist band in N20°W direction. It indicates that
                                 the graphite schist band may extend further in northwest
                                 direction but in southeast direction chargeability zone take
                                 U shaped turn which may be attributed to the folding in
                                 graphite schist band at south of Netara village.

               Highlights of  the The graphite mineralisation in the area is mainly
               Petrographical/   associated with carbon phyllite belonging to Kalinjara
               Mineragraphic Studies Formation of Meghnagar Group of Aravalli Supergroup,
                                 which often grades into graphite schist at places. The
                                 graphite schist / carbonaceous phyllite occur as lensoidal
                                 bodies, concordant linear bands and sometimes as a
                                 pocket in the area of investigation. The thickness of the
                                 graphite schist band ranges from <1m to 25m along the
                                                                                  
                                 general foliation direction of the lithounits. The graphite
                                 mostly occurs as flaky and amorphous variety in the area.
                                 The flaky variety of graphite mainly comprises of flakes
                                 which are silver grey to greyish black in color. In hand
                                 specimen its appears greyish black to silver grey in color
                                 and usually soils hand with black to silver grey color. The
                                 amorphous variety of graphite has a dull lusture,
                                 comparatively hard, massive.
