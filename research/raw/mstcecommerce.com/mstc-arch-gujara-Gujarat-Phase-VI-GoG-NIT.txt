                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                 “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone and Marl Mineral”
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development and
          Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, the
          Government of Gujarat has identified 3 (three) mineral blocks containing Limestone mineral and 1 (one)
          mineral block containing Limestone and Marl minerals for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders
                                                                                    
          for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.                                
                                                                                    
          Accordingly, Financial bids are invited in digital format only and Technical bids are invited both in
          digital and physical format from Eligible bidders.                        
                                                                                    
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender
          Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and other details for
          the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only and can be downloaded
                                                                                    
          from the website of MSTC Limited (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).
                                                                                    
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on MSTC website. On successful registration,
          eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction process.
          Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summary are available free of cost on the website of MSTC
          Limited (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).       
                                                                                    
          Last date for purchase of Tender Document, after payment of a tender fee of INR 2,00,000 [INR Two
                                                                                    
          Lakh] excluding GST (GST is payable under Reverse Charge Mechanism), on website of e-auction portal
          provider is April 23, 2020 [on or before 17:00 hours (IST)] and the last date for submission of the bids is
          April 29, 2020 [on or before 17:00 hours (IST)].                          
                                                                                    
          It may be noted that the Bidders who have already participated in the fifth phase of auction (Reference to
          NIT dated February 25, 2019) that was held for Dhank Sub-Block - A, Dhank Sub-Block - C, Mudhvay
          Sub-Block - A and Sutrapada Block (i.e. the Bidders who have submitted their technical bids and bid
          security pursuant to the fifth phase of auction held for these four blocks), shall not be required to pay
          Tender Document fee in respect of purchase of the respective Tender Document and may download the
          Tender Document for Dhank Sub-Block - A, Dhank Sub-Block - C, Mudhvay Sub-Block - A and
          Sutrapada Block free of cost from the e-auction portal.
