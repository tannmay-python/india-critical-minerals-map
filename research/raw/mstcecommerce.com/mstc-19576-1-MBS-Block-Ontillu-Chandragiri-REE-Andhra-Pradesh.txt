         4  Exploration Details                                                   
         i. Regional Geology            Regionally the proposed block for Exploration
                                     Licence lies in the southern part of the Eastern Dharwar
                                     Craton (EDC). The area is part of Chittoor terrane in the
                                     vicinity of SGT in the south. The major lithounits
                                     exposed in the area include Meso- to Neo-Archean
                                                                                  
                                     TTGs, migmatites and granitoids belonging to the
                                     Peninsular Gneissic Complex – II (PGC-II) and
                                     Closepet Granite affinity. Smaller enclaves of Older
                                     Metamorphics comprising hornblende schist, mica
                                     schist and talc schist occur within the granitoids. The
                                     PGC-II comprising biotite-hornblende granite gneiss
                                                                                  
                                     occupies almost the entire area. The rocks of PGC-II
                                     have been classified into “porphyritic hornblende
                                     granite", “biotite granite gneiss” and nebulitic to
                                     homophanous granite'' under the "Migmatite Group'’
         ii. Geochemical Investigation NGCM (2017 and 2019) reported high TREE of 4507
                                      ppm, 3124 ppm, 2209 ppm, 1720 ppm, 1557 ppm from
                                      the area. Ce values up to 2036 ppm, La up to 1047 ppm,
                                      Nd up to 767 ppm and Pr up to 221 ppm are observed in
                                      the area. The south-central part of the block shows good
                                                                                  
                                      values for Zr, ranging from 665-1371ppm.

         ii Exploration Potential of Block  The mineral potentiality of the block is
                                       indicated from the favorable geological set-up and
                                       NGCM  data of composite samples for REE    
                                       mineralization.                            
                                                                                  
                                     • The NGCM  data reveals elevated concentration of
                                       TREE (max. 4507 ppm) NGCM data of composite
                                       samples indicating potential for REE mineralization.
                                                                                  
                                     • NGPM  survey indicates a gravity gradient zone, and
                                       possible deeper sources of high-density rocks in sub-
                                                                                  
                                       surface, which may be related to deep seated
                                       mineralization.                            
                                                                                  
                                     • NAGMP surveys over the study area shows anomalous
                                       spectrometric signatures in U, Th and U/Th maps
                                       along with prominent structures.           
                                                                                  
                                     • Samples from pegmatites from a G4 stage exploration
                                       carried out in TS 57O/7, south of the proposed area,
                                       yielded more than 1000 ppm TREE, of which two
                                       samples indicated presence of 2282ppm and 2323 ppm
                                                                                  
                                       TREE.
