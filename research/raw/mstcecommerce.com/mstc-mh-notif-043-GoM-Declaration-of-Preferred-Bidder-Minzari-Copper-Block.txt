                                   Important Notice                               
                                                                                  
                     Declaration of Preferred Bidder for Minzari Copper Block     
                                                                                  
         1. Pursuant to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone and
            Bauxite minerals and for grant of Composite Licence for Bauxite, Copper, Base
                                                                                  
            Metal, and Manganese Ore minerals” vide NIT dated September 07, 2023, issued
            by the Government of Maharashtra for auction of 6 major mineral blocks, the forward
            e-auction of Minzari Copper Block was held on November 21, 2023 (Tuesday) on MSTC’s
                                                                                  
            e-auction portal.                                                     
         2. We are pleased to inform that Hindalco Industries Limited (MSTC Registration No. 70748)
                                                                                  
            has submitted the highest Final Price Offer of 7.25 % (seven point two five per cent) and
            is hereby declared as the Preferred Bidder for grant of composite licence of Minzari
            Copper Block.
