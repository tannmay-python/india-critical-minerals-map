                               Notice Inviting Tender                             
           “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Zinc, Iron
           ore and Manganeseminerals  and for grant of Composite Licence          
           forAluminous Laterite,Basemetal (Lead, Zinc, Copper and Silver), and   
           Manganese Ore”                                                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
           In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B and Section 11 of the
           Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in       
           accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, as
           amended from time to time, the Government of Madhya Pradesh has        
           identified:                                                            
             a. 9 mineral blocks (7 blocks of Limestone, 1 block of Iron ore and Manganese, and 1
               block of Zinc)for Electronic Auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of
               grant of Mining Lease.                                             
                                                                                  
             b. 3 mineral blocks (1 block of Aluminous Laterite, 1 block of Basemetal (Lead, Zinc,
               Copper and Silver), and 1 block of Manganese Ore) for electronic auction and
               hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Composite Licence.
                                                                                  
           Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical
           bids are invited both in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.
                                                                                  
           Eligibility conditions, date and time,terms, and conditions etc.for    
           participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender Document.
           Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and
           other details for the e-auction processfor the mineral blocksareavailable in
           electronic form only and can be downloaded from the e-auction portal of
           MSTC                                                  Limited          
           [https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp].           
                                                                                  
           Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above e-
           auction portal. On successful registration, eligible bidders will obtain login
           ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction process.  
           Interested and eligible bidders can participate in the bidding only after
           online purchase of the Tender Document on payment of the Tender fee from
           the above e-auction portal. The Tender Fee shall be waived for bidders who
           have previously purchased the Tender Document for any specific block in
           prior auction attempts.Any revision, clarifications, addendum, corrigendum,
           time extensions, etc. to the tender document will be hosted on the above e-
           auction portal only and no separate notifications shall be issued in the press.
