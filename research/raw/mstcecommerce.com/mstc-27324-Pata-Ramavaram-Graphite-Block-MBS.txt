          Geochemical Studies Geochemical analyses yielded F.C. values of 0.06–4.28% in bedrock
                           samples and 0.02–8.74% in trench samples. Highest FC values were
                           recorded from calc-silicate rubble and khondalite boulders near the old
                           working, indicating localized high-grade graphite mineralization.
                                                                                  
          Petrographic, SEM and Petrographic and SEM studies confirm the presence of graphite flakes
          Raman Spectroscopic and carbonaceous materials in both calc silicate and khondalite, along
          Studies          with associated minerals such as monazite, barite, and anglesite. Raman
                           spectroscopy of five samples further supports the identification of
                           graphite and its flaky morphology, with distinct Raman peaks
                           characteristic of graphite.

                           silicate body, from which 12 trench (PTS) samples were collected and

        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones Graphite occurrences in the investigated block is associated with calc-
                           silicate granulite which has a strike length of 50 m and 20 m to 25m in
                           width located at top of Pata Ramavaram hill as a float within Khondalite.

                           The graphite mineralization is flaky.                  
                                                                                  
          Trend            The graphite occurrences in the study area are aligned along a NE-SW
          (Dip and Strike) trend, which corresponds with the regional strike of foliation and the
                           Narsipatnam shear zone, located to the east of the area.
          Justification    Based on the G4 level of exploration carried out in the area, Graphite
                           mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer the details
                           of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence block
                           area is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.

                           Railways.

                           is located at an approximate distance of 45 km by road.
          Airport          Nearest airport is Rajahmundry Airport, Madhurapudi, situated
