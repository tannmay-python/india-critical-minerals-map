                  Corrigendum 1 – Additional Time Window for Submission of Queries  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            1. This is with reference to “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of
               Critical and Strategic Minerals” dated February 29, 2024, issued by the
               Government of India.                                                 
                                                                                    
            2. During the Pre-Bid Conference held on March 27, 2024, certain potential bidders
               requested that they be allowed an additional time window to submit queries
               related to the Tender Documents.                                     
            3. Accordingly, a fixed time frame from 10:00 hours of March 29, 2024 (Friday) to
               17:00 hours of April 04, 2024 (Thursday) is given for submission of queries by
               potential bidders. Queries may be sent on the email id: do-mom@gov.in as
               specified in Clause 7.2 of the Tender Document. The email should clearly bear the
                                                                                    
               following subject line: “Queries/Request for Additional Information: Tender
               Document for “Insert Name of Mineral Block” clearly specifying the name of the
               mineral block for which the query is being raised.                   
            4. Potential bidders who have submitted their queries after 17:00 hours of Thursday,
               March 21, 2024 (last date of receiving queries from Potential Bidders as per the
               Tender Documents) are requested to kindly resubmit their queries within the
                                                                                    
               above-specified timeframe.                                           
            5. Kindly note that all other terms and conditions remain as mentioned in the Tender
               Documents.
