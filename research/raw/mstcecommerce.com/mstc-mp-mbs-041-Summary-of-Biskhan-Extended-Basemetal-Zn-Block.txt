           5  MINERALISED ZONES                                                     
                                                                                    
              NUMBER OF MINERAL ZONES A total three mineralized zones have been delineated in the
                                     Biskhan block. The Surface indications of mineralization in the
                                     area is scanty and are evident by visible specks, minute veins
                                     of sphalerite along with disseminated chalcopyrite, pyrites and
                                     gahnite are hosted in garnetiferous-qtz-biot-mus-schist and
                                     associated foliated rhyolites/garnetiferous rhyolites. The
                                     mineralization is parallel to dominant foliations and thus the
                                     mineralization is structurally controlled. The average length
                                     and width of Biskhan area mineralization is about 1000m x
                                     800m.
