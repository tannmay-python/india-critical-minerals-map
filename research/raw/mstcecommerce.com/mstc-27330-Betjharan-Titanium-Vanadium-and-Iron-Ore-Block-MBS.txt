          Geochemical Studies GSI reported vanadium bearing magnetite ore bodies occur as magmatic
                           layering in the gabbro-anorthosite suite of rocks in the area. The ore
                           patches occur in the eastern flank of Kanapat Hill range, associated with
                           fine grained gabbro. The total Fe content of the ore varies mostly
                                                                                  
                           between 55% to 60% and TiO2 content varies mostly between 10% to
                           15% and V content from trace to 1.24%. A reserve of 0.75 million tonnes
                           of ore with 1% V2O5 has been estimated.                
          Drilling         51 boreholes with total drilling meterage of 1517.90 m 
          Borehole Density 20 m grid intervals                                    
          Trench and Pit   57 pits and trenches with 401.14 cubic meter were carried out in the block
        4 Quantity of Minerals (Grade wise)                                       
                                                                                  
          Minerals         Titanium, Vanadium and Iron Ore (Magnetite)            
          The block has been explored in G-4 stage and no UNFC-compliant mineral resource estimation has
          been carried out.                                                       
                                                                                  
        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones A total of 16 small and large vanadium bearing titaniferous –magnetite
                           bodies occur as segregated pockets and layers within gabbroic rocks
                           varying in area from 100 sq m to 2500 sq m were delineated.
          Trend            Igneous layering in gabbro has orientation varying from E-W to NE-SW
          (Dip and Strike) with moderate dips towards north direction in northern part of
                                                                                  
                           Kakuriruma village and near the foothills of Kumardubi village and
                           Betjharan village.

          Justification    Based on the G4 level of exploration carried out in the area, Titanium,
                           Vanadium and Iron ore (magnetite) mineralisation has been identified in
                           the block area. To infer the details of the mining feasibility, detailed
                           exploration is required. Hence block area is recommended for grant of
                           Composite Licence.                                     
        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head Gorumahisani Railway Station on the South Eastern Railway is the
                           nearest railhead, while Rairangpur Railway Station is situated at a
                           distance of approximately 22 km from the area.         
          Road             The block area comprising Luhashila, Kakuriruma and Balarampur
                           villages falls in Gorumahisani Tahasil of Mayurbhanj District, Odisha. The
                           area is approachable from Rairangpur town and is connected by a
                           network of district and village roads.                 
                                                                                  
          Airport          The nearest operational airport is Jamshedpur (Sonari) Airport,
                           Jharkhand. Rairangpur Airstrip is the nearest airstrip to the block area.
