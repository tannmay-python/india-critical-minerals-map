                  Corrigendum 3 – Additional Time Window for Submission of Queries  
                                                                                    
          1. This is with reference to “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of Critical and
            Strategic Minerals” dated November 29, 2023, issued by the Government of India.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          2. During the Pre-Bid Conference held on December 22, 2023, certain potential bidders
            requested that they be allowed additional time window to submit queries related to the
            Tender Documents.                                                       
                                                                                    
          3. Accordingly, Government of India has allowed a fixed time frame from 10:00 hours of
            December 23, 2023 (Saturday) to 17:00 hours of December 26, 2023 (Tuesday) for submission
            of queries by potential bidders. Queries may be sent on the email id: do-mom@gov.in as
                                                                                    
            specified in Clause 7.2 of the Tender Document. The email should clearly bear the following
            subject line: “Queries/Request for Additional Information: Tender Document for “Insert
            Name of Mineral Block”. clearly specifying the name of the mineral block for which the query
            is being raised.                                                        
                                                                                    
          4. Potential bidders who have submitted their queries after 17:00 hours of Tuesday, December
            19, 2023 (last date of receiving queries from Potential Bidders as per the Tender Documents)
            are requested to kindly resubmit their queries within the above specified timeframe.
                                                                                    
          5. Kindly note that all other terms and conditions remain as mentioned in the Tender
            Documents.
