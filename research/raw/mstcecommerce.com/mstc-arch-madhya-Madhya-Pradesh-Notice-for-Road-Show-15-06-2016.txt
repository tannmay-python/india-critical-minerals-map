          FICCI along with the Mineral Resources Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, is
          organizing a Road Show Summit titled “Madhya Pradesh: An Investment Destination for
          Mining”.                                                                
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          All the interested bidders and mining companiesrelated to Limestone and Diamond sector
          may attend the conference at the time and venue mentioned below:        
                                                                                  
          Date & Time  June 20, 2016 (Monday), 1030 hours                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Venue        The Orchid Hotel, Adjacent to Domestic Airport, 70-C, Nehru Rd, Vile
                       Parle East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400099                     
                                                                                  
          The following will be the broad topics for discussion:                  
                                                                                  
              Features of the blocks being auctioned (size, location, land details etc.)
              Brief outline of the auction process being followed                
              Bidders’ expectations regarding the auction process                
              Views on the bid parameters                                        
                                                                                  
              Any other matter relevant to the auction
