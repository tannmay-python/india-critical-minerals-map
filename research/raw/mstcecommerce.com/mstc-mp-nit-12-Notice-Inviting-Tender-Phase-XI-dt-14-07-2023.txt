                                  Notice Inviting Tender                          
                                                                                  
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Iron Ore, Bauxite and Manganese Ore
                                                                                  
          and for grant of Composite Licence for Manganese Ore, Iron Ore, Iron Ore and Manganese Ore
          Phosphorite, Graphite, Graphite and Vanadium, Limestone, Diamond, Platinum Group Elements
          (PGE), Copper, Basemetal, Gold and Basemetal”                           
                                                                                  
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10A(2)(d), Section 10B and Section 11 of the Mines and
          Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules,
          2015 notified there under, as amended from time to time, the Government of Madhya Pradesh has
          identified:                                                             
                                                                                  
            a) 13 mineral blocks (2 blocks of Bauxite, 2 blocks of Iron Ore, 8 blocks of Limestone and 1 block
              of Manganese Ore)for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of
              Mining Lease.                                                       
            b) 38 mineral blocks (3 blocks of Basemetal, 1 block of Copper, 4 blocks of Diamond, 2 blocks of
              Gold & Basemetal, 6 blocks of Graphite, 1 block of Graphite & Vanadium, 1 block of Iron Ore, 1
              block of Iron Ore & Manganese Ore, 5 blocks of Limestone, 10 blocks of Manganese Ore,3 block
              of Phosphorite and 1 block of PGE) for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the
              purposes of grant of Composite Licence.                             
                                                                                  
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in digital
                                                                                  
          and physical format from eligible bidders.                              
                                                                                  
          Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms, and conditions etc. for participating in the electronic auction
          are provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications,
          updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form
          only and can be  downloaded from the e-auction portal of MSTC Limited   
          (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).             
                                                                                  
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above e-auction portal. On successful
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction
          process. Interested and eligible bidders can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the
          Tender Document on payment of the Tender fee from the above e-auction portal. Any revision,
          clarifications, addendum, corrigendum, time extensions, etc. to the tender document will be hosted on the
          above e-auction portal only and no separate notifications shall be issued in the press.
