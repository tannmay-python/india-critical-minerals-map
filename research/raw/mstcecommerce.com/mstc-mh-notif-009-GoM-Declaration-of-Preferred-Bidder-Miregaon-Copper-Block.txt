                    Declaration of Preferred Bidder for Miregaon Copper Block     
                                                                                  
             1. Pursuant to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone mineral
                and for grant of Composite Licence for Copper mineral, Gold associated with Silver,
                Lead & Copper, Copper & associated mineral, and Iron Ore minerals” vide NIT
                                                                                  
                dated July 25, 2025, issued by the Government of Maharashtra for auction of major
                mineral blocks, the forward e-auction of Miregaon Copper Block was held on
                December 12, 2025 (Friday) on MSTC’s e-auction portal.            
                                                                                  
             2. We are pleased to inform that Sagar Stone Industries (MSTC Registration No.
                                                                                  
                102007) has submitted the highest Final Price Offer of 46.00 % (forty six point zero
                zero per cent) and is hereby declared as the Preferred Bidder for grant of Composite
                Licence of Miregaon Copper Block.
