          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, REE and
                          RM mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer the
                          details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence
                          block area is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.
        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head Ramtek station                                        
          Road            The block area is well approachable from Nagpur by Nagpur-Jabalpur
                          Road via NH44 (former NH 7) and from where an approx. of 05 km
                          metalled road leads to Kawalapur block. The block area is located 06km,
                          south of Ramtek town and is accessible via NH 753 and SH 253 from
                          Ramtek.                                                 
          Airport         Nagpur Airport                                          
                                                                                  
        7 Hydrography                                                             
          Local     Surface The area is drained by a major nala in the center of the area. Number
          Drainage Pattern, of small nala joins this nalas which forms a sub dendritic to sub parallel
          Rivers, etc     drainage pattern in the area.                           
        8 Climate                                                                 
                                                                                  
          Mean Annual Rainfall The average annual rainfall of the area is 1064.1 mm.
          Temperature     The area experiences a tropical climate with maximum temperature up
                          to 45°C during summer and minimum upto 4°C during winter.
        9 Topography                                                              
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 55O/7                    
                                                                                  
          Morphology of the The southern and eastern part of the area is undulating. The strike
          area            ridges in the area trend in E-W. The highest elevation of the area is
                          309m above MSL is located on the isolated hillock in eastern part of the
                          study area and the lowest elevation is 295m above MSL located in the
                          catchment area of the major nala draining the study area.
