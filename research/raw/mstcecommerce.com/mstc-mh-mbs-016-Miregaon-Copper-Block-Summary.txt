          ii. Exploration Agency             MECL                                 
         iii. Local Geology                  The Miregaon block area in the east central
                                             part of Sakoli Fold Belt (SFB) which is
                                             prominent mineral bearing belt known for
                                             polymetal (Cu, Au, Ag) mineralization. The
                                             lithounits exposed in the block area mainly
                                             belongs to Mesoproterozoic Sakoli Group
                                             followed by Cainozoic (Laterite) and 
                                             Quarternery sediments. The lithounits
                                             include mainly Granite gneiss and Quartzites
                                             of Archean Amagoan Gneissic Complex  
                                             (AGC) and Phyllite and Micaschist of 
                                             Mesoproterozoic Sakoli Group. Besides,
                                                                                  
                                             basic intrusive such as Metabasalt & Dolerite
                                             dyke, Quartz Reefs and quartz veins are
                                             younger intrusive of Mesoproterozoic age.
                                             The Laterite of Cainozoic age is present as
                                             encrustation above the micashist. Alluvial
                                             Soil is the youngest and covers most of the
                                             parts of block area.
