         6 Accessibility                                                          
                                                                                  
           Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station to the study area is Balotra, located
                           approximately 20 kilometers east of the study area along the Barmer-
                           Jodhpur-Degana-Jaipur Section of the North-Western Railway.
           Road            The study area is located about 20 km southwest of Balotra town,
                           which serves as the district headquarters. Road connectivity is
                           provided by NH-25 and NH-58, linking Balotra with Jaipur via Jodhpur,
                           Beawar, and Kishangarh.                                
                                                                                  
           Airport         The nearest airport is Jodhpur Airport, located about 95–105 km from
                           Balotra district, providing regular air connectivity to major cities.
