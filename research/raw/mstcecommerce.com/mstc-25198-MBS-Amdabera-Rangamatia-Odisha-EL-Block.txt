         i   Regional geology            The area forms part of the Archean Singhbhum
                                                                                  
                                         Craton and comprises a diverse assemblage of
                                         litho-tectonic units. The principal geological
                                         components include the Older Metamorphic 
                                         Group, Older Metamorphic Tonalite Gneiss,
                                         Badampahar–Gorumahisani Schist  Belt,    
                                         Singhbhum Granite Phase-III, gabbro–anorthosite
                                         suite, Mayurbhanj Granite, Dhanjori Group, and
                                         younger dolerite dykes and sills. The Badampahar–
                                         Gorumahisani belt represents a typical greenstone
                                         belt developed in the northeastern part of the
                                         craton. It occurs close to the northern fringe of the
                                         Similipal complex and acts as a lithotectonic
                                         boundary separating the western granite terrain of
                                         the Singhbhum granitoids from the Mayurbhanj
                                         Granite in the east. The belt forms a contiguous,
                                         curvilinear hill range and is dominantly composed
                                                                                  
                                         of supracrustal sequences belonging to the Iron Ore
                                         Supergroup. These supracrustals are intermittently
                                         intruded by a variety of plutono-magmatic bodies
                                         ranging in composition from gabbro to granite.
         ii  Geochemical Investigation   NGCM   brought out high concentration of 
                                         vanadium, titanium and iron in stream sediments;
                                         Fe O (>18%, n=3), TiO2 (>2.2%, n=1) and V

                                         (>400 ppm, n=1), which reflects locally higher
                                         values influenced by the presence of meta-
                                         sedimentary rocks of the Gorumahisani Group and
                                         associated intrusive gabbro- anorthosite complex.
