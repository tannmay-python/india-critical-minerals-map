                Highlights of The samples for PGE-Au, Ni, Cr, Co, Cu and V analysis were collected
                Geochemical  from the sulphide rich zone, oxide rich zones, contact zones of two
                Prospecting  magma types and zones of compositional layering in meta-gabbro. The
                             analytical results of 100 bed rock samples (BRS) revealed that-
                             The total PGE (Pt, Pd. Ir, Ru and Rh) was recorded maximum up to 55
                             ppb in a coarse-grained amphibolite located 500 m north east of Piparia
                             village.                                               
                             The Ni value varies from <10 to 570 ppm. The maximum value of Ni
                             analysed in sample from meta-gabbro dyke located 1 km north-west of
                             Belkhera vilage.                                       
                             The Cr value varies from <10 to 820 ppm. The maximum value of Cr
                             analysed in a sample from gabbro containing sulphide bearing quartz vein
                             near Jamtara village.                                  
                             The Co value varies from <10 to 102 ppm. The maximum value of Co
                             analysed in sample from meta-gabbro dyke 1 km north-west of Belkhera
                             vilage.                                                
                             The Cu value varies from <10 to 1700 ppm. The maximum values of Cu
                             analysed in sample from meta-gabbro near Jamtara village.

                             analysed in a sample of meta-basalt enclave within meta-gabbro located
                             100 m west of Tandi village.                           
                             The Au values is <25ppb in all samples.                
                             The maximum value for Pt is 19ppb and for Pd is 33 ppb. The highest
                             value for Ir is 11 ppb, for Ru 09 ppb and for Rh is 04 ppb. Encouraging
                             high values of Ni, Cr and Cu are recorded in various rock types present
                             in the study area. The highest value for Ni is 570 ppm, for Cr 820 ppm

                             gabbro. High values of 1050 ppm, 1450 ppm and 1700 ppm of Cu are
                             recorded in metabasalt enclave, coarse grained amphibolite and meta
                             gabbro respectively.                                   
                Highlights of A total of 20 cubic metre trenching was carried out in four trenches in
                Pitting/trenching different locations in the study area. In TR-01 total PGE (Pt, Pd. Ir, Ru
                             and Rh) value varies from <5 ppb to 13 ppb, Ni varies from 62 to 291
                             ppm; Cr varies from 18 to 194 ppm; Co varies from 25 to 54 ppm, Cu
                             varies from 25 to 99 ppm and V varies from 203 to 240 ppm.
                             In the TR-02 total PGE (Pt, Pd. Ir, Ru and Rh) value varies from <5 ppb
                             to 7 ppb, Ni values vary from 120 to 195 ppm; Cr values vary from 171
                             to 189 ppm; Co values vary from 18 to 39 ppm, Cu values are below
                             detection limit; V values vary from 63 to 109 ppm.     
                             In the TR-03, total PGE (Pt, Pd. Ir, Ru and Rh) value varies from <5 ppb
                             to 6 ppb, Ni values vary from 38 to 430 ppm; Cr values vary from 29 to
                             72 ppm; Co values vary from 49 to 82 ppm, Cu values vary from <10 to
                             298 ppm; V values shows consistency >400 ppm in 3 samples out of 4
                             samples.                                               
                             In the TR-04, total PGE (Pt, Pd. Ir, Ru and Rh) value varies from <5 ppb
                             to 7 ppb, Ni values vary from 85 to 295 ppm; Cr values vary from 96 to
                             480 ppm; Co values vary from 18 to 70 ppm, Cu values vary from <10 to
                             15 ppm; V values vary from 96 to 137 ppm.

              6 Accessibility                                                       
                Features                                                            
                Nearest rail head The nearest railway station is Parasia, located on the Betul-Amla-
                             Chhindwara narrow gauge railway track of Central Railway. The other
                             nearby railway stations are Nawegaon and Chhindwara located on the
                             same track.                                            
                Road          The area lies about 40 km west of Chhindwara town and well connected
                                                                                    
                              by metal roads. The area can be approached from Bhopal-Betul via
                              Ghoradongri-Sarni road or via Multai road. It can also be approached
                              from Bhopal-Chhindwara via- Hoshangabad-Piparia-Tamia road.
                                                                                    
                Airport      Nagpur                                                 
              7 Hydrography                                                         
                Local surface The area mainly exhibits undulatory topography marked by linear
                drainage pattern mounds dissected by sub parallel to dendritic drainage pattern.
                (Channels)                                                          
                Rivers/Stream Area is drained mainly by Kanhan River and its tributaries viz., Bel nadi,
                             Karawoh nala, Batiyadev nala etc. in the south and middle part of the
                             area and Pench River in the north
