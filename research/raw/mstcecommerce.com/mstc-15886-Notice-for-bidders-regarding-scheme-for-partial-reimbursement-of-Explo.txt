          Notice regarding scheme for partial reimbursement of Exploration Expenses for holders
          of Exploration Licence                                                    
                                                                                    
          Reference:Invitation of bids for grant of Exploration Licence, Notice Inviting Tender (NIT)
          dated 13 March 2024                                                       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Bidders are hereby notified that Ministry of Mines; Government of India has notified“Scheme
          for partial reimbursement of Exploration Expenses for holders of Exploration Licence”.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The objective of the scheme is to provide partial reimbursement of exploration expenses for
          exploration licence holders which are granted through auction.            
                                                                                    
          Exploration Licence holders granted under section 10BA of the MMDR Act shall be eligible to
          apply for reimbursement of exploration expense under the present scheme.  
                                                                                    
          The Bidders are encouraged to acquaint themselves with provisions of said scheme. Scheme is
          attached along with this notification.
