                          Cassiterite shows presence of tantalum.

        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head The study area is well connected by the South-Eastern Railway's main line
                          which is located in the northern portion of the study area at Tumsar Road
                          station.                                                
          Road            The study area is located about 33 km north of Bhandara District
                          Headquarters, about 7 km from Tumsar and approximately 90 km from
                          Nagpur. The area is well connected by road network from Nagpur via NH-
                          53 along the Nagpur–Bhandara Road, followed by Spur Road 543K
                                                                                  
                          towards Tumsar and Balaghat.                            
          Airport         Nearest airport is Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur.

          Mean Annual Rainfall The average annual rainfall of the area is 1064.1 mm.
          Temperature     The area experiences a tropical climate with maximum temperature up to
                          45°C during summer and minimum up to 4°C during winter.
