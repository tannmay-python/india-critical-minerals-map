                       Notice for Return of Bid Security for 02 Mineral Blocks      
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          1. This is in reference to “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of Critical and
          Strategic Minerals” dated 24 June 2024, issued by Designated Officer, Ministry of Mines,
          Government of India.                                                      
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          2. The E-auction process for 02 blocks was concluded and Preferred Bidder has been declared
          vide notification dated 26 November 2024, issued by Designated Officer.   
                                                                                    
          3. Pursuant to Clause 14.3 of the Tender Document, the Bid Security of the bidders other than the
                                                                                    
          Preferred Bidder and the Qualified Bidder who has submitted the second highest Final Price Offer
          pertaining to the following blocks will be returned by the Designated Officer for the 02 critical
          mineral blocks as follows:                                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                   No.                   Block Name                                 
                                                                                    
                    1. Ponchi Graphite Block                                        
                                                                                    
                    2. Vadakhol-Asoli Ni, Cr, Co and Associated Mineral Block

          second highest Final Price Offer will be retained until the earlier of the following:
          (i) the issuance of the Letter of Intent to Qualified Bidder who has been declared as the Preferred
          Bidder pursuant to Clause 8.l(B)(c), or                                   
          (ii) the provision of the Performance Security by such second highest Bidder pursuant to it having
          been declared as the Preferred Bidder inters of Clause 10.1.3,or          
                                                                                    
          (iii) annulment of the tender process as per the provisions of the Tender Document.
                                                                                    
          5. In accordance with para 3 above, the bidders other than the Preferred Bidder and the Qualified
          Bidder who has submitted the second highest Final Price Offer will be intimated for the return of
