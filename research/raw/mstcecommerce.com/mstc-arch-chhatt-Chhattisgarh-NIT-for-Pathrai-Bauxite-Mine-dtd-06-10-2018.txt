                                    Brief Description                               
          CMDC  invites online offer from the experienced, reputed and financially sound parties for
          Mining and Marketing of Bauxite of Distt. - Surguja (Chhattisgarh).       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         1.   Details of Mining Lease Government of Chhattisgarh vide order No. F 3-4/2007/12 Naya
                                 Raipur dated 07.01.2017 has sanctioned Mining lease for mineral
                                 Bauxite to Chhattisgarh Mineral Development Corporation Ltd.
                                 in the village Pathrai over an area 99.350 Ha. in Tehsil - Mainpat,
                                 District- Surguja Chhattisgarh. For which CMDC has executed
                                 lease deed with Government of Chhattisgarh on 09.01.2017.
                                                                                    
         2.   Scope of Work          CMDC will allow the contractor (selected bidder) to
                                 operate the mine for Mining & Marketing of Bauxite as per the
                                 Bid conditions under the prevailing mining laws. The mineral
                                 concession will be in the name of CMDC throughout the life of
                                 mine.                                              
                                                                                    
                                     The selected Bidder (Contractor) will be responsible for the
                                                                                    
                                 execution of following activities at their own cost-
                                                                                    
                                     Mining and Marketing of Bauxite                
                                                                                    
                                     (i) Deployment of Mining Machineries and blasting.
                                     (ii) Employment of qualified persons           
                                     (iii) Maintenance of records as per requirement
                                     (iv) Maintenance of statutory provisions as per
                                        requirement of mining act & rules           
                                    (v) Meet all other expenses related to Mining and
                                        Marketing of Bauxite.                       
                                     (vi) Maintenance of environmental conditions at lease
                                        area as per provision of law                
                                     (vii) Renewal of air/water consent and approval of mining
                                        scheme preparation as and when required.    
                                        All the cost involved in the above activities shall be
                                        borne by the contractor.                    
                                                                                    
                                    (viii) If incase of failure of the contractor these activities are
                                                                                    
                                       undertaken by CMDC than under such circumstances
                                                                                    
                                      the cost will be recovered from the contractor.
                                                                                    
                                     (For details please refer to this Bid document )

               1.   The bidder should be a company incorporated under the Companies Act,
               1956/2013 or ownership/ partnership firm registered under Indian Partnership Act,
               1932/LLP under the LLP Act, 2008

               mineral (Schedule IV mineral) during the preceding 5 years anywhere in India.

          /Calcinations.
