                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                    
                      “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone and
                                     Marl Mineral”                                  
                                                                                    
         In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development and
         Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, the
         Government of Gujarat has identified 6 (six) mineral blocks containing Limestone and Marl mineral for
         electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.
                                                                                    
         Accordingly, Financial bids are invited in digital format only and Technical bids are invited both in digital and
                                                                                    
         physical format from Eligible bidders.                                     
                                                                                    
         Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender
         Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the
         e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only and can be downloaded from the
         website of MSTC Limited (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).
                                                                                    
         Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on MSTC website. On successful registration, eligible
         bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction process. Model Tender
         Document and Mineral Block Summary are available free of cost on the website of MSTC Limited
         (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).                
                                                                                    
         Last date for purchase of Tender Document on website of e-auction platform provider is February 09, 2024
                                                                                    
         [on or before 17:00 hours (IST)] and the last date for submission of the bids is February 16, 2024 [on or before
         17:00 hours (IST)]. The Price of Tender Document per mineral block is Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rupees Two Lakh)
         excluding GST and bidders are required to pay applicable GST under Reverse Charge Mechanism. Moreover,
         Bidders who have already participated in the previous phase of auction (Reference to NIT dated February 17,
         2022 i.e. the Bidders who have submitted their technical bids pursuant to the eighth phase of auction held for
         Shepa Limestone & Marl Block, Varvada Limestone & Marl Block and Shantipura Limestone Block), shall
         not be required to pay Tender Document fee in respect of purchase of the Tender Document and may download
         the Tender Document for the said block free of cost from the e-auction portal. Out of these Six (6) blocks,
                                                                                    
         Shantipura Limestone Block is an expired mine and remaining Five (5) blocks are virgin Blocks
