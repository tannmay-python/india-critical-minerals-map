                     Revised notice for Submission of Bids and Opening of Technical Bids
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            1. This is with reference to the “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of Critical and
               Strategic Minerals” dated February 29, 2024 and notice for Submission of Bids and Opening of
               Technical Bids dated May 20, 2024, issued by Designated Officer, Ministry of Mines, Government
               of India, the Technical Bid Opening will be conducted at 10:30 hours on Tuesday, June 18, 2024.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            2. All bidders are advised to adhere to the timelines mentioned in the Tender Document (and
               reproduced below) for timely submission of the Technical Bid (Technical + initial price offer) as
               follows:                                                             
                                                                                    
              S. No.         Event Description           Estimated Date             
                                                                                    
                1. Bid Due Date (Technical + initial price offer) [on Friday, June 14, 2024
                   or before 17:00 hours (IST)]                                     
                2. Opening of the Technical Bids       Tuesday, June 18, 2024       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            3. All bidders are advised to note that the Technical Bid shall be submitted on the e-auction portal
               and the duly executed original physical copies must be hand delivered in an envelope to the below
               stated address so that they are received on or prior to the Bid Due Date, failing which the
               Technical Bid shall be considered as not received.                   
                                                                                    
               O/O Designated Officer                                               
                                                                                    
               Ministry of Mines, Government of India                               
               Room no. 307, ‘D’ Wing                                               
               Shastri Bhawan                                                       
               New Delhi- 110001                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
               It is expressly clarified that bidders are required to submit the Initial Price Offer (IPO) electronically
               only, as provided in Schedule III of the Tender Document.

               Designated Officer and the same will be uploaded on MSTC website.

               allowed to participate in the Technical Bid opening on production of duly issued letter of
