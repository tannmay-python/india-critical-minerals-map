                                  Notice Inviting Tender                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
              “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone and Marl Mineral”
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development
          and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified
          thereunder, the Government of Gujarat has identified 3 (three) mineral blocks containing
                                                                                    
          Limestone mineral and 1 (one) mineral block containing Limestone and Marl minerals for
          electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.
                                                                                    
          Accordingly, Financial bids are invited in digital format only and Technical bids are invited both
                                                                                    
          in digital and physical format from Eligible bidders.                     
                                                                                    
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the
          Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and
          other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only
                                                                                    
          and   can  be   downloaded from  the   website of   MSTC   Limited        
          (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).               
                                                                                    
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on MSTC website. On successful
                                                                                    
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the
          e-auction process. Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summary are available free of
          cost on the website of MSTC Limited                                       
          (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).               
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Last date for purchase of Tender Document, after payment of a tender fee of INR 2,00,000 [INR
          Two Lakh] plus applicable GST, on website of e-auction platform provider is April 02, 2019 [on
          or before 17:00 hours (IST)] and the last date for submission of the bids is April 08, 2019 [on or
          before 17:00 hours (IST)].                                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          It may be noted that the Bidders who have already participated in the third phase of auction
          (Reference to NIT Dated August 11, 2017) that was held for Mudhvay Sub-Block - A (i.e. the
          Bidders who have submitted their technical bids and bid security pursuant to the third phase of
