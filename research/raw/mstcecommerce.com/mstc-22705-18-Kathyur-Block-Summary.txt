         6 Accessibility                                                          
           Nearest Rail Head Dehradun                                             
           Road           The area is accessible via rough and poorly maintained metaled roads,
                                                                                  
                          with the Maldevta-Dubra metaled road passing near the block, serving
                          as a crucial route for transportation and connectivity. This road, along
                          with other motorable and metaled roads in the area, ensures that the
                          block remains accessible throughout the year, facilitating the
                          movement of people and goods.                           
           Airport        Jolly Grant Airport of Dehradun

         8 Climate                                                                
           Mean     Annual                                                        
                          The mean annual rainfall is about 250 cm.               
           Rainfall                                                               
           Temperature    The Summer is warm but not excessively hot, with average
                          temperatures ranging from 20°C to 35°C (68°F to 95°F). Winters are
                          cool, with temperatures typically ranging from 3°C to 20°C (37°F to
                          68°F). The rains are generally heavy and continue upto early
                          September.
