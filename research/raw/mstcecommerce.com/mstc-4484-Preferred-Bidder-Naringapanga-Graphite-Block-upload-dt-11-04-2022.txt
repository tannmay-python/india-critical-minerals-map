                               Declaration of Preferred Bidder                    
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          With reference to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Iron Ore & Manganese,
          Limestone and Graphite” dated December 29, 2021 issued by the Government of Odisha for grant
          of Mining Lease, forward e-auction of Naringapanga Graphite Block was held on March 29, 2022
          on the MSTC e-auction portal.                                           
                                                                                  
          We are pleased to inform that Maa Kudargarhi Steels Private Limited (108239) has submitted
          the highest Final Price Offer of 7.65% (Seven point Six Five percent) and is hereby declared the
          Preferred Bidder for grant of Mining Lease of Naringapanga Graphite Block.
