MSTC Limited is pleased to announce Launch of its new Mineral Block Portal on 05.02.2024. The new portal is available at https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln and can be accessed through Google Chrome ,Mozilla Firefox and Microsoft Edge Browsers. The events with bid start date before 05.02.2024 will be available for bidding, opening and subsequent processes on the old portal only. While the events with bid start date on or after 05.02.2024 will be available for bidding on the new portal. Your existing username and password will work on the new portal. The new portal shall require Class 3 signing and encryption type Digital Signature . In case of any issue please contact MSTC Helpdesk.
