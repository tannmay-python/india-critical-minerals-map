        4   Exploration Details                                                   
        i.  Regional Geology             The rocks exposed in Kothi, Vishamundi and
                                                                                  
                                      Jinjgaon areas belong to Bengpal and Surjagarh
                                      Group and are characterized by diopside-quartzite,
                                                                                  
                                      sericite-fuchsite-garnet-quartzite/ quartz schist,
                                      banded magnetite quartzite, quartz-kyanite rock,
                                                                                  
                                      amphibolite, hornblende schist, talc-tremolite
                                                                                  
                                      actinolite schist occurring as bands and enclaves in
                                      gneissic and migmatitic rocks. Homogenization in
                                                                                  
                                      migmatite is so over-whelming that at times it is
                                      difficult to establish their inter-relationship in the
                                                                                  
                                      area. These rocks are later intruded by dolerite,
                                                                                  
                                      ultramafite and gabbro dykes. The Bengpal Group is
                                      overlain by green-stone suite of rocks i.e. 
                                                                                  
                                      characterized by metaultramafite-serpentinite
                                      association (Alpine type) with ferruginous chert,
                                                                                  
                                      Banded haematite quartzite and basic meta volcanic.
                                                                                  
                                      These rocks are later intruded by dolerite and quartz
                                      veins. The pyritiferous cherty quartzite which was
                                                                                  
                                      previously included in Bengpal Group (Saxena et.al
                                      1978-79) is now considered to form part of Surjagarh
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                      Group only as it is intimately associated with
                                                                                  
                                      ultramafite and ferruginous cherty sequence. The
                                      Surjagarh Group in this area occurs in a long linear
                                                                                  
                                      NE-SW trending belt which takes an E-W swing
                                      further south west and then swerve to NW-SE 
                                                                                  
                                      forming a limb of antiformal structure parallel to main
                                      Surjagarh antiformal closure.               
                                                                                  
        ii. Geochemical Investigation     No NGCM work has been carried out. Bed rock
                                                                                  
                                      analysis given some encouraging results of Pb which
                                      is about 2% whereas in few samples, Zn analyzed
                                                                                  
                                      upto 1.5%.                                  
        5   Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                     
                                                                                  
        i.  Mineral Commodity       Lead & Zinc                                   
        ii  Exploration Potential of Block The occurrences of base metal mineralization
                                                                                  
                                      have been located near Jhirmal nala section, Amipalli
                                      nala section, at the confluence of these two nala
                                                                                  
                                      sections, 3 km NE of Mangutha village and to the
                                                                                  
                                      north of Pakhur village. The one in the Jhirmal nala
                                      section is located about 600m north of Kandodi-
                                                                                  
                                      Bhamragarh road crossing the nala. Fairly good
                                      incidences of galena, sphalerite, pyrite and
                                                                                  
                                      chalcopyrite have been observed in a band of cherty
                                                                                  
                                      quartzite in association with the metabasics rock. The
                                      zone strikes N40°W-S40°E and dips vary steeply. As
                                                                                  
                                      surficial observation, the zone continues in the north-
                                      west for about 3-4 km but with very sparse incidence
                                                                                  
                                      mostly as occasional specks. In the south-west the
                                                                                  
                                      band is concealed under soil cover. A parallel band
                                      with incidence of galena, chalcopyrite is located about
                                                                                  
                                      550m south of the above band and about 50-60
                                      meters, north of the road crossing. This band is
                                                                                  
                                      exposed in the nala section for a strike length of about
                                      10 meters and width of one meter. The chemical
                                                                                  
                                      analysis has given some encouraging results of Pb
                                                                                  
                                      which is about 2% whereas in a few samples Zn is
                                      about 1.5%.                                 
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                         A zone of incidence of chalcopyrite is located in
                                                                                  
                                      the part of Ampalli nala section, about 1.5 km, NW of
                                      Partampalli village consists of a band of garnetiferous
                                                                                  
                                      amphibolite striking N60°W-S60°E with a very steep
                                      dip towards SW. It consists of quartz vein striking
                                                                                  
                                      N25°E-S25°W running for over 200 meters with an
                                      average width of about 20 meters. The quartz vein
                                                                                  
                                      exhibits box work after pyrite and is very much
                                                                                  
                                      sheared and silicified. Shearing trend is in N25°E
                                      direction. In-situ dark reddish brown and lemon-
                                                                                  
                                      yellow oxidized material is found in the boxwork. In
                                      the south-west part of the vein, incidence of
                                                                                  
                                      chalcopyrite is fairly good. Besides, pyrite is also
                                                                                  
                                      noted. In the SW, the vein is under soil cover,
                                      chalcopyrite occurs as disseminated specks. The
                                                                                  
                                      quartz vein contains angular inclusion of the epidote-
                                      chlorite rock.
