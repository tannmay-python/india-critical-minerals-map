          Geochemical Studies The glauconitic sandstone/shale horizon shows a thickness variation of
                          14.4 m to 33.0 m and occurs at depths ranging from 4.7 m to 48.8 m. The
                          K₂O content in core samples varies from 1.42% to 7.61%, averaging 5.44%
                          across 17 boreholes.                                    
                                                                                  
          Drilling        17 numbers of vertical boreholes with total drilling meterage of 793.75m.
          Borehole Density The boreholes are drilled in grid pattern at an interval of 400m X 400m.
          Trench and Pit  Not carried out.

         of 5.45% K₂O at 4% K₂O cutoff grade.                                     
        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones Glauconite bearing horizons were demarcated at Khairmal, Kasalwa and
                                                                                  
                          Gohirapali, villages along a NE-SW trending sandstone band. Glauconitic
                          Sandstone occurs as detrital grains in various sizes and shapes within
                          sandstone.                                              
          Trend           The trend of the glauconitic sandstone is NE-SW with varying dip of 5-10°
          (Dip and Strike) towards SE.                                            
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, glauconite
                          mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer the details of
                                                                                  
                          the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence block area
                          is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.
