                                NOTICE INVITING TENDER                              
                                                                                    
                “Grant of Exploration Licence for the Minerals specified in the Seventh Schedule”
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10BA of the Mines and Minerals (Development and
          Regulation) Act, 1957 (“Act”) (and pursuant to order dated October 21, 2024 bearing ref. no. M.VI-
          1/3/2023-Mines VI issued by the Ministry of Mines, Government of India under Section 20A of the Act) to
          grant Exploration Licence for minerals specified in the Seventh Schedule to Act, and in accordance with
          the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, and as amended from time to time, Government of
          India, through Designated Officer, Ministry of Mines, has identified 11 blocks for grant of Exploration
          Licence through electronic auction, and hereby invites, in respect thereof, financial bids in digital format
                                                                                    
          only and technical bids both in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.
          Out of these 11 blocks, 02 exploration blocks are being notified under ‘second attempt’ as per Rule 19E(13)
          of Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015.

          The eligibility conditions, manner of conduct of the bidding process, and date and time for participating in
          the electronic auction are provided in the tender document (the “Tender Document”), which (along with
          timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process for the blocks) is available in
          electronic form only and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC Limited:
          https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp.                
          Interested and eligible bidders may register themselves on the above website. Upon successful registration,
          eligible bidders will obtain log-in ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction process.
                                                                                    
          The model Tender Document and a summary of the blocks are available in the free section on the MSTC
          e-auction portal.                                                         
          The price of the Tender Document is Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One Lakh only) plus applicable Goods &
                                                                                    
          Services Tax (GST). GST is payable under the reverse charge mechanism.    
          Further, for the blocks put up for re-auction in this tranche, bidders who have purchased the tender
          document and/or submitted the bids in Tranche I (NIT dated March 13, 2025) are exempted from paying
          the Tender Document Fee for the respective exploration blocks.            
                                                                                    
          The Tender Document may be purchased by making payment of the price of the Tender Document on the
          above website commencing from Tuesday, February 17, 2026. The last date for purchasing the Tender
          Document is Monday, April 06, 2026, on or before 17:00 hours (Indian Standard Time).
