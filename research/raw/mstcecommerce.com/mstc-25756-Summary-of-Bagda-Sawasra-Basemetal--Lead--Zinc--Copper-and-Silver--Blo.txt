             NUMBER OF MINERAL ZONES      In Bagda Sawarsa basemetal block, mineralization is
                                          mainly associated with quartz veins intruded within
                                          Harda Granitoids. NW-SE trending 2-5m wide quartz
                                          vein is discontinuously exposed over a strike length of
                                          approximately 2 km in the form of bouldary outcrops.
                                          Galena mineralization is seen over a strike length of
                                          approximately 500 meters. Preliminary test drilling
                                          carried out in 1991-92 in six boreholes (1105.25m)

             TREND (DIP AND STRIKE)       Bedding-                                  
                                          Shear Zones: No                           
                                          Joints and fractures: Granite gneisses show ENE-
                                          WSW foliation with sub vertical to vertical dip. Minor
                                          isoclinal folds with E-W axis are exhibited by the
                                          quartz- amphibole- garnet- carbonate rock. These are
                                          refolded again showing N-S axis and plunging 70° N.
                                          The granite and granite gneiss are showing 7 to 8 sets
                                          of joints whereas the quartz veins exhibit 5 set of joints.
