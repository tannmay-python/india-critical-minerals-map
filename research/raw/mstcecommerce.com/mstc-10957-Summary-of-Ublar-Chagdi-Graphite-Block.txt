                 Highlights of   the The graphite mineralisation in the area is mainly
                 Petrographical/     associated with carbon phyllite belonging to Kalinjara
                 Petrochemical Studies Formation of Meghnagar Group of Aravalli     
                                     Supergroup, which often grades into graphite schist at
                                     places. The mineralisation mostly occurs along the
                                     foliation planes as thin layers, small patches, pockets
                                     and lumps within the carbon phyllite/graphite  
                                     schist/carbonaceous marble. The graphite mostly
                                     occurs as coarse to small flakes (microcrystalline
                                     variety) which is intermittently mixed with mica flakes
                                     and grey powdery material. It is dark grey to steel grey
                                     in colour, soils hand and greasy in nature. The presence
                                     of graphite in the graphite bearing mineralization zones
                                     has been confirmed by study of thin polished sections
                                     by the Raman spectroscopy.

                 MORPHOLOGY  OF THE  Major part of the Ublar-Chagdi block is occupied by
                 AREA                small isolated hills/mounds and intervening valleys.
                                     The area is characterized by hilly and rugged  
                                     topography. Maximum elevation is 372m near to  
                                     Chagdi village. The regional slope of the area is
                                     towards south and southwest.
