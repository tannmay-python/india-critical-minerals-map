         6 Accessibility                                                          
           Nearest Rail Head The nearest railhead is Kantabanji of East Coast Railways, which is
                           about 40 km SSW of the block area on Titlagarh-Raipur broad gauge
                           section.                                               
           Road            The block area is about 7 km SW of Patnagarh and about 45 km west
                           of district headquarter Balangir contiguous to the state highway
                                                                                  
                           connecting Balangir-Patnagarh to Belapara-Kantabanji. The area is
                           connected by fair weather road from the Patnagarh Tehsil.
           Airport         Swami Vivekanand Airport, Raipur is the nearest airport at a distance
                           of about 220 km away from the block in WNW direction.
