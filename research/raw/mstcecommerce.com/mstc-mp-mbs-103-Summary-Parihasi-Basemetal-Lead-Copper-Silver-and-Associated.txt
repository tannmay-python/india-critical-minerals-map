           5  MINERALISED ZONES                                                     
              NUMBER OF MINERAL ZONES Basemetal mineralization are noticed near Parihasi and
                                     east of Bhalua villages. Sulphide mineralization is mainly
                                     in the form of disseminated grains and specks in white
                                     quartz veins Near Parihasi village within white quartz
                                     vein (about 180m in length & trending- N70°E) stains of
                                     malachite, grains of galena and few grains of  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    

                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                     arsenopyrite is observed. Malachite Stain is also
                                     observed in quartz vein in East of Bhalua in road section.
              TREND (DIP AND STRIKE) The area has undergone three phases of deformation
                                     represented by ENE-WSW (D1), ESE-WSW (D-2) and 
                                     NNE-SSW (D-3). The geomorphology of the area is
                                     controlled by D-2 deformation which is represented by
                                     tight isoclinal and overturned folds plunging easterly,
                                     while signature of D-3 is present in from of Joint planes
                                     and broad warps. General trend of lithologies in area is
                                     ESE-WNW and dipping southerly. Metamorphism of low
                                     green schist facies observed. The quartz vein at Parihasi
                                     trending N60°E having dimensions 1m X 50m is   
                                     showing very good value of Cu and Pb.

              MORPHOLOGY OF THE AREA Physiographically the area is represented by hilly and
                                     rugged terrain with a series of ENE-WSW trending hills.
                                     The central part of the area is covered with high hill
                                     terrains with mix dense forest where as low laying area in
                                     southern and northern part is under cultivation. The GM
                                     area is mostly under forest.
