                                  Notice Inviting Tender                            
                     Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Leasefor Bauxite Blocks 
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B of the Mines and Minerals (Development and
          Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015(as amended from time
          to time) notified thereunder, Mineral Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh has identified
          the following Bauxite Blocksfor electronic auction* and hereby invites bids for the purpose of grant of
          Mining Lease(ML):                                                         
           Sl No        Name of the Block    District Area(Ha) Tender Fees)(INR)    
                                                             Excluding GST          
           1.   Sapnadar (Block A,B & C) Bauxite Block Surguja 171.136 2,00,000/-   
           2.   Kandraja Bauxite Block       Surguja 135.223  2,00,000/-            
          *Second attempt of auction according to subrule 12 of rule 9 of Mineral Auction Rules, 2015
               Accordingly, Financial bids are invited in digital format only and Technical bids are invited both in
          digital and physical format from Eligible bidders.                        
               Terms and conditions, deadlines etc. for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the
          Tender Document. The Model Tender Document for the mineral blocks and mineral block summary are
          available free of cost in electronic form and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC Ltd.
          (http://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp) and websites of Mineral Resources
          Department, Government of Chhattisgarh (www.chhattisgarhmines.gov.in)for the purpose of information
          only. Timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process are available on the
          website of MSTC Ltd.                                                      
               Those interested and eligible for bidding can participate in the bidding only after online purchase
          of the Tender Document on payment of the non-refundable tender fee as mentioned above (Goods and
          Service Tax(GST) shall be payable at 18% of tender fee), from the website of e-auction platform provider
          MSTC Ltd. Bidder shall be able to make payment of the Tender Document fee through RTGS/ net
          banking (through the payment gateway of e-auction platform). After registration at MSTC website,
          interested & eligible bidders can purchase the Tender Document. On successful registration, eligible
          bidders will obtain Login ID and password necessary for participation in the e- auction process.
               Last date for online purchase of Tender Document is Tuesday, July 12, 2022and the last date for
          submission of the bid is 15:00 Hours IST on Wednesday, July 13, 2022. Any further communications,
          amendments etc. shall be available on the above website of MSTC and the websites of Mineral
          Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh.
