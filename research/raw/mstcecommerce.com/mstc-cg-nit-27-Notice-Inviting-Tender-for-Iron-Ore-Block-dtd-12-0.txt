                                Notice Inviting Tender                            
                                                                                  
                   Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Iron Ore Mineral blocks
         In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act,
         1957, its subsequent amendments and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 and its subsequent
         amendments notified thereunder, Mineral Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh has identified the
         following Mineral Blocks for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease (ML) through electronic auction and hereby
         invites bids in digital format only from eligible bidders.               
                                                                                  
          Sr   Name of the Block            Mineral District Area (Ha.) Tender Fees
          No                                                          (INR)*      
          1    North Bailadila (Block A) Iron Ore Block Iron ore Dantewada 78.00 5,00,000
          2    North Bailadila (Block B) Iron Ore Block Iron ore Dantewada 452.00 5,00,000
                                                                                  
          3    Kalwar Iron Ore Block        Iron ore Kanker   23.72    5,00,000   
          4    Lohattar (Sonadehi) Iron Ore Block Iron ore Kanker 150.00 5,00,000 
         *Excluding GST                                                           
         Terms and conditions, deadlines etc. for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender
         Document. The Model Tender Document for the mineral blocks and mineral block summary are available free of
         cost in electronic form and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC Ltd.
         (http://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp) and websites of Mineral Resources Department,
         Government of Chhattisgarh (www.chhattisgarhmines.gov.in) for the purpose of information only. Timelines,
         notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process are available on the website of MSTC Ltd.
                                                                                  
         Those interested and eligible for bidding can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the Tender
         Document on payment of the non-refundable tender fee as mentioned above excluding Goods and Service Tax
         (GST shall be payable at 18% of tender fee), from the website of e-auction platform provider MSTC Ltd. Bidder
         shall be able to make payment of the Tender Document fee through RTGS/ net banking (through the payment
         gateway of e-auction platform). After registration at MSTC website, interested & eligible bidders can purchase the
         Tender Document. On successful registration, eligible bidders will obtain Login ID and password necessary for
         participation in the e- auction process.                                 
         Last date for online purchase of Tender Document is 29-09-2021 and the last date for submission of the bid is
         15:00 Hours IST on 30-09-2021. Any further communications, amendments etc. shall be available on the above
         website of MSTC and the websites of Mineral Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh.
