       1957, its subsequent amendments and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 as amended from time to
       time notified there under, the Government of Rajasthan has notified the following major mineral block for the
       purpose of grant of Mining lease & hereby invites bids in digital format only from eligible bidders through electronic
       auction. These blocks are being proposed for auction with pre-embedded clearances.
