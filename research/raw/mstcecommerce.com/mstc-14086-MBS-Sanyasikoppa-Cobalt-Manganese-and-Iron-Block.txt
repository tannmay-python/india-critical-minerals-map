             Justification Since GSI’s G4 stage work was carried out in larger area and indicated
                           anomalous zone of Co-Mn-Fe values in three pockets and scattered
                           values for Pb, Zn and associated elements. In order to bring out the
                           proper disposition of the potential zones of poly-metal, the area needs
                           to be assessed through LSM, systematic sampling, detailed geophysics
                           survey and scout drilling. The block has been recommended for putting
                           for auction as composite license (CL) for up-gradation to assess the full
                           economic potentiality of the Cobalt, Manganese and Iron in the block.
