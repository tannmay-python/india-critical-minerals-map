          4  Exploration Details                                                  
                                                                                  
          i. Regional Geology         The study area is predominantly occupied by granitoids of the
                                      PGC-II, mainly hornblende–biotite granite, containing
                                      enclaves of migmatitic gneiss and Yerraballi schist.
                                      Regionally, the granitoids have been classified into two
                                      phases, namely grey granite and pink granite. The older
                                      metamorphic rocks occur as enclaves within the granites and
                                      represent sedimentary and volcanic cycles. Basic dykes
                                      intrude both pre- and post-pink granite phases, while acid
                                      intrusives are represented by quartz reefs and epidote-bearing
                                      quartz veins.                               
         ii. Geochemical Investigation National Geochemical Mapping (NGCM) was carried out
                                      during FS 2021–22 in toposheet No. 56J/16. The analytical
                                      results indicate TREE values ranging from 442.72 ppm to
                                      1118.50 ppm, with a median value of 735.24 ppm. Within the
                                      proposed EL block, one sample recorded TREE values
                                                                                  
                                      exceeding 1000 ppm, two samples yielded values in the range
                                      of 800–1000 ppm, and eighteen samples recorded values
                                      between 500–800 ppm, indicating moderate to strong REE
                                      enrichment. Fourteen samples yielded values above the
                                      threshold value of 590 ppm for the toposheet.
                                                                                  
                                      Two soil samples (C-horizon) yielded TREE values exceeding
                                      1000 ppm, of which one sample recorded values above 2500
                                      ppm, suggesting significant near-surface REE enrichment.
                                      Petrographic observations indicate the presence of allanite and
                                      titanite within pegmatite veins intruding grey biotite granite in
                                      the northern part of the area and K-feldspar granite in the
                                      southern part, indicating REE-bearing accessory mineral
                                      phases.                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                      The concentration of yttrium varies from 15 ppm to 101 ppm
                                      within pink granite of PGC-II, while hafnium values range
                                      from 10.91 ppm to 183.05 ppm, with several samples
                                      exceeding the threshold value of 90.67 ppm, further
                                      supporting the rare-earth and associated rare-metal potential of
                                      the block.

          ii Exploration Potential of Block  National geochemical mapping (NGCM) carried out in the
                                                                                  
                                        area during field season 2021-22 show encouraging values
                                        of REE in stream sediment and soil samples indicating
                                        presence of possible REE in the area.     
                                       The high magnetic zones in the northeastern, central and
                                                                                  
                                        southeastern portions of the proposed EL block may
                                        possibly indicate extension of Migmatite gneiss/granitoids
                                        of PGC-II in toposheet 56J/16 near Chinna Kodur,
                                        Siddipet and Velakatur villages respectively.
                                                                                  
                                       Exploration in this block will help in identification of the
                                        mineral commodity and classification of the mineral
                                        system in the area.                       
                                       Titanite and allanite occur in pegmatite veins intruding
                                        grey biotite granite in the northern part of the area, while
                                        allanite-bearing pegmatite veins intrude K-feldspar granite
                                        in the southern part, indicating REE-bearing accessory
                                        mineralization.
