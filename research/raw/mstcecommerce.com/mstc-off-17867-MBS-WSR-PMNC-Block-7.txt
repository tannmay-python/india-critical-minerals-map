            Geology and Geomorphology of the exploration area                     
        6   Offshore Geomorphology West Sewell Ridge (WSR) is located in between the Forearc and
                                 Inner Volcanic arc in the southern part Andaman Sea. WSR is a
                                 N-S trending ridge of rugged topography with inferred
                                 lineaments, faults and having steep and gentle flanks in the east
                                 and west respectively. The ridge top is characterized by small
                                 fracture zone presently filled with ooze sediments. Gullies
                                 /erosional channels are more at the eastern flank of the mount
                                 rather than the western flank. WSR comprises mainly Volcanic
                                                                                  
                                 rocks (Basalt, Spilite, Dacite), ooze sediments, terrestrial influx,
                                 volcanic arc sediments and sporadic occurrence of polymetallic
                                 nodules and crusts. The area is cut across by major North-South
                                 trending faults and lineaments such as West Andaman Fault and
                                 Sumatra Fault System.                            
            Seabed Morphology    Seabed Morphology - Seabed is characterised by a N-S
                                 trending ridge of rugged topography with inferred lineaments,
                                 faults and having steep and gentle flanks in the east and west
                                 respectively. The ridge top is characterized by small fracture
                                 zone presently filled with ooze sediments. Most of the area has
                                 having slope less than 5°, whereas a higher slope of more than
                                 20° occurs in the eastern and western flanks.    
            ROV videography details The ROV was deployed at southern part of WSR between water
                                                                                  
                                 depths 1145 to 1571 m. North western part of the block is
                                 covered by sediment and western part occupied by boulders and
                                 cobbles. Polymetallic nodules and crusts were observed over the
                                 sediment covered areas at water depths ranging from 922 to 1505
                                 m.
