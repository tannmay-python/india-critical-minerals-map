          Geochemical Studies The analytical data of Mo in the quartzo feldspathic veins ranges from
                           0.01 to 2.40 %. The value of Mo in the grid samples (10 m x 10 m) of the
                           host granite ranges from 0.25 ppm to 3833.67 ppm.      
          Petrographic, SEM and Molybdenite mineralization is mainly associated with quartzo feldspathic
                                                                                  
          Raman Spectroscopic veins hosted within syenogranite to monzogranite trending E-W and NW-
          Studies          SE in north of Lanjabanda area. Apart from these veins there are several
                           smaller arrays of veins with similar character hosting molybdenite. The
                           host granite also contains specks and clusters of molybdenite, mainly
                           proximal to the mineralized quartzo-feldspathic, quartz and pegmatite
                           veins. Molybdenite is identified as bluish-grey coloured, very soft mineral
                           having a metallic luster and grey streak. It is flaky in nature and easily
                           peels off with pocket knife. Microscopic study shows euhedral to
                                                                                  
                           subhedral flaky aggregates with high reflectivity and bluish anisotropism.
                           They are widely spread in 4 locations in the form of specks and stringers
                           of molybdenite along with chalcopyrite ± bornite ± covellite ± pyrite ±
                           magnetite as associated ore minerals.

        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones Molybdenite mineralization is mainly associated with quartzo-feldspathic
                           veins hosted within syenogranite to monzogranite. The molybdenite-
                           bearing quartzo-feldspathic veins and associated host granite occupy an
                           exposed mineralized zone of approximately 150 m × 100 m.
                                                                                  
          Trend            The trend of the veins are E-W and NW-SE.              
          (Dip and Strike)                                                        
          Justification    Based on the G4 level of exploration carried out in the area, Molybdenum
                           mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer the details
                           of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence block
                           area is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.    
        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station is Veldurthi Railway Station located at a
                           distance of about 8 km-10 km from Lanjabanda Village.  
          Road             The Lanjabanda GM Block is well connected by village and district roads.
                           The block is accessible via NH-44 passing west of the area near Veldurthy,
                           providing connectivity to Kurnool, Dhone, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru.
          Airport          The nearest airport is Kurnool Airport (Uyyalawada Narasimha Reddy
                           Airport) at Orvakal, Kurnool District, located about 25–35 km from the
                                                                                  
                           block area and providing domestic air connectivity to major cities.

          Mean Annual Rainfall The average rainfall is 624.00 mm.                 
          Temperature      The climate of Lanjabanda-Govardhanagiri area is characterized by hot
                           summers with may being the hottest month with mean daily
                           temperature of 28.20⁰ C (min) and 40.30⁰ C (max). December is the
                           coldest month when the mean daily temperature is 16.10⁰ C (min) and
                           30.30⁰ C (max).
