                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Basemetal (Zinc), Iron ore and
          Manganese, and Manganese minerals and for grant of Composite Licence for Basemetal (Zinc),
          Limestone, Aluminous Laterite, Basemetal (Lead, Zinc, Copper and Silver), Manganese Ore, and
          Iron ore and Manganese”                                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B and Section 11 of the Mines and Minerals
          (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015
          notified thereunder, as amended from time to time, the Government of Madhya Pradesh has identified:
            a) 11 mineral blocks (8 blocks of Limestone, 1 block of Basemetal (Zinc), 1 block of Iron ore and
               Manganese, and 1 block of Manganese) for Electronic Auction and hereby invites tenders for the
                                                                                    
               purposes of grant of Mining Lease.                                   
            b) 6 mineral blocks (1 block of Basemetal (Zinc), 1 block of Limestone, 1 block of Aluminous
               Laterite, 1 block of Basemetal (Lead, Zinc, Copper and Silver), 1 block of Manganese Ore, and 1
               block of Iron ore and Manganese) for Electronic Auction and hereby invites tenders for the
               purposes of grant of Composite Licence.                              
                                                                                    
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in digital
          and physical format from eligible bidders.                                
                                                                                    
          Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms, and conditions etc. for participating in the electronic auction
          are provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications,
                                                                                    
          updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form
          only and can  be downloaded from the e-auction portal of MSTC Limited     
          [https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp].              
                                                                                    
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above e-auction portal. On successful
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction
          process. Interested and eligible bidders can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the
          Tender Document on payment of the Tender fee from the above e-auction portal. The Tender Fee shall be
          waived, for those mineral blocks only which have been put up for auction under “second attempt” as per
          Mineral Auction Rule vide this Notice Inviting Tender, for such bidders only who have previously
                                                                                    
          purchased the Tender Document in prior auction attempts of the said mineral block. Any revision,
          clarifications, addendum, corrigendum, time extensions, etc. to the tender document will be hosted on the
          above e-auction portal only and no separate notifications shall be issued in the press.
