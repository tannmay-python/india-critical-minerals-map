          Highlights   of A total of 05 borehole samples were subjected to 34 element analysis
          Geochemical Survey by ICPMS method. Most of the samples were collected from the
                          graphite mineralization from borehole intersections. Analytical results
                          show vanadium (V) is associated with graphite and show up to
                          486.24ppm.                                              
          Geophysical Survey On the basis of combined study of magnetic and SP methods, targeted
                                                                                  
                          mineralized zones are successfully identified with different lithological
                          units and their contacts. The large amplitude and the narrow shape of
                          the anomaly on profile plots indicate that the body is at shallow depth
                          and steeply dipping and almost near vertical in the area. Hence, the
                          indicated zones may be checked by drilling for the presence of
                          mineralization.                                         
          Drilling        13 nos of core boreholes (inclined) with total drilling meterage of 755
                          m carried out.                                          
                                                                                  
          Borehole Density Nearly 100m strike interval                            
          Trench and Pit  90 cubic meters of trenching (5 nos) carried out.

        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones                                                       
                                                                                  
          Trend  (Dip and General trend of the mineralization is NNE-SSW dipping towards NW
          Strike)         with an amount 50º to 80º.                              
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area,
                          Manganese and Graphite mineralisation has been identified in the
                          block area.                                             
                                                                                  
                          To infer the details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is
                          required. Hence block area is recommended for grant of Composite
                          Licence.                                                
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railhead is Balangir which is 50km from the block in the
                          southeast which is part of the Jharsuguda–Vizianagaram line.
                                                                                  
          Road            The block is well connected with the State Highway 42 which connects
                          Balangir –Patnagarh-Kantabanji and passes 10km south of the
                          Bharatbahal block. The block is located around 50km from the district
                          head quarter Balangir.                                  
          Airport         The Jharsuguda Airport at Jharsuguda is the nearest airport which is
                          210km in the northeast while Swami Vivekananda Airport at Raipur,
                          Chhattisgarh is 220 km away in the west.                
        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface The block area falls in between the catchment area of Suktel River.
          Drainage Pattern, Suktel river flows near to the southwestern corner point of the block.
          Rivers, etc     The drainage pattern is dendritic.                      
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The average rainfall of the district is 1423.5 mm. 
          Temperature     In summer the temperature rises up to high of 44°C, and in winter it
                                                                                  
                          falls as low as 7°C.
