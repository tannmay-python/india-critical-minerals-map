              5  MINERALISED                                                        
                 ZONES                                                              
                 NUMBER OF         Based on borehole data three mineralised zones i.e. zone-I, II
                 MINERAL ZONES     and III has been delineated. Mineralized zone-II is the main
                                   graphite bearing zone occurring as lensoidal body over a strike
                                   length of 300m and intersected in all boreholes. It is likely to
                                   continue in eastern part in reserve forest and therefore could
                                   not be explored. The mineralized zone-I is intersected only at
                                   first level in a single borehole as a small lense at shallow depth
                                   over a strike length of 50m. Mineralized zone-III also
                                   intersected only in one first level borehole as a thin bands at
                                   deeper depth than mineralized zone-II.           
                 Nature of Mineralized Graphite bearing mineralized zone occurs as narrow, lensoidal

                 LOCAL SURFACE     The drainage is mostly dendritic in nature. Most of the streams
                 DRAINAGE PATTERN  originated at the northern and southern highlands are merged with
                 (CHANNELS)        each other in the central part of the study area and drained through
                                                                                    
                                   valley part. The drainage in the area appears to be topographically
                                   controlled along with variation in lithological competence and
                                   locally structural controlled as well.           
                 RIVERS/ STREAMS   The general slope of the area is from ENE to WSW and major
                                   nalas are flowing in this direction. The Makra area in and around
                                   is drained by Betul River, which is a tributaries of Tapti River.
                                   Betul River originating from the area near Garha 4km east of
                                   Junawani and flows in WSW direction and finally joins Tapti
                                   River in further west at western margin of Toposheet no. 55G/09
                                   near Jamu village.

              9  TOPOGRAPHY                                                         
                 TOPOSHEET NUMBER  55G/09                                           
                 MORPHOLOGY  OF    The area around Makra is characterised by peneplain-pediplain
                 THE AREA          topography with presence of isolated hills in the form of
                                   monadnocks and residual mounds which comprises of quartzites,
                                   porphyroblastic gneiss, granite and granite gneiss with small
                                   intervening valleys. The valley portion mainly comprises of
                                   metasediments in granite/granite gneiss as small enclaves. The
                                   highest elevation of 597m is recorded in eastern part of the Makra
                                   block over a quartzite hill while lowest elevation of 511m is
                                   recorded in western part of the block in the south of Makra. In
                                   general, the area in Makra block mostly represented by rugged
                                   and undulated terrain with lithologically and structurally
                                   controlled small hillocks, mounds and valleys trending in ENE-
                                   WSW to NE-SW direction.
