        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones Surface indications of bauxite in the area are manifested in form of
                          pisolitic, nodular and concretionary grains of gibbsite within aluminous
                          laterite. Bauxite occurs as patches, irregular and lensoidal form within
                          aluminous laterite. The aluminous laterite zone within bauxite patches are
                          also be considered as the mineralized zone. A total of 390 m of drilling were
                          carried out in 28 boreholes. All the boreholes have intersected the
                                                                                  
                          mineralized zone of aluminous laterite and bauxite.     
          Trend           Mineralisation occurs as irregular, patchy and lensoidal bodies within
          (Dip and Strike) aluminous laterite.                                    
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, Vanadium,
                          Gallium, Titanium, Bauxite and Aluminous Laterite mineralisation has
                          been identified in the block area. To infer the details of the mining
                          feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence block area is
                          recommended for grant of Composite Licence.             
                                                                                  
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station is at Kankavli (On Konkan Railway).
          Road            The study area is located about 25 km northeast of Devgad Tehsil
                          Headquarters in Sindhudurg District, Maharashtra. The area is well
                          connected by MDR-61, which further joins NH-66 (Mumbai–Goa Highway)
                          near Rajapur, about 40 km to the north, providing road connectivity to the
                                                                                  
                          study area.                                             
          Airport         The nearest airport is Sindhudurg Airport, which is located at Chipi which
                          is about 95 km south of the study area.

        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The annual rainfall is around 4500 mm.             
          Temperature     The area experiences a typical Konkan coastal–inland climate,
                          characterized by hot and humid summers with temperatures reaching up
                          to 40°C. During the rainy season, temperatures generally range between
                                                                                  
                          17°C and 30°C, while winters remain mild.               
        9 Topography                                                              
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 47H/07                   
          Morphology of the The area forms a small part of the Konkan plain that is located due west
          area            of the Western Ghat escarpment and due east of the Arabian Sea. The
                          area consists of gently undulating topography covered dominantly by
                                                                                  
                          laterite. The general elevation of the area varies from 10 MSL to 150 MSL.
