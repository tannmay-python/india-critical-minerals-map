             Highlights  of It is inferred that the higher values of Al2O3 (%) and Li (> 200ppm) in
             Geochemical Survey BRS are mainly associated with rocks of bauxite column (pisolitic
                           bauxite, non-pisolitic bauxite and claystone. Li and Al2O3 (%) scatter
                           plot for the bedrock samples clearly depicts positive correlation among
                           these two elements.                                      
                           The streams samples were collected in a random manner, good values
                           of Al2O3 (%) avg. 22.8% and Li avg. 299ppm were detected in analysed
                           stream sediment samples. These considerably high values of Al2O3 (%)
                           and Li (ppm) indicates the flux of sediments derived from the northerly
                           dipping bauxite column. The high values and positive correlation of
                           Al2O3 (%) and Li (ppm) analysis in BRS and stream sediment samples
                           favors the potentiality of bauxite and it helped in borehole planning over
                           bauxite to explore for third dimension.
