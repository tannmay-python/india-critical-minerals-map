                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
        “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Iron Ore and Limestone mineral and Composite License
                                                                                    
                                   for Limestone mineral”                           
        In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) and Section 11 of the Mines and Minerals (Development
                                                                                    
        and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, as
        amended from time to time, the Government of Assam has identified:          
                                                                                    
          a) 07 (seven) mineral blocks (1 block of iron ore and 6 blocks of limestone) for electronic auction and
             hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.      
                                                                                    
          b) 01 (one) mineral block of limestone mineral for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the
                                                                                    
             purposes of grant of Composite License.                                
                                                                                    
        Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in digital and
        physical format from eligible bidders.                                      
                                                                                    
        Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms and conditions etc. for participating in the electronic auction are
                                                                                    
        provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and
        other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only and can be
                                                                                    
        downloaded    from     the      website    of      MSTC       Limited:      
        (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).                 
                                                                                    
        Interested bidders can register themselves on the above website. On successful registration, bidders will obtain
        login ID and password necessary for participation in the e- auction process.
                                                                                    
        Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summaries are available free of cost on the website of MSTC
                                                                                    
        Limited.                                                                    
                                                                                    
        Last date for purchase of Tender Document after payment of a tender fee on the website of MSTC Limited is
        Wednesday, January 24, 2024 and the last date for submission of the bid is Wednesday, January 24, 2024 on or
                                                                                    
        before 17:00 hours (Indian Standard Time).                                  
                                                                                    
        The tender fee is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupee five lakh) for each mineral block. The price of Tender Document is
        exclusive of GST and the GST is payable under Reverse Charge Mechanism.
