                                            (NSMB) which are reported to abut against each
                                            other along a crustal-scale ductile shear zone
                                            named South Purulia Shear Zone (SPSZ) or
                                            Tamar-Porapahar Shear Zone (TPSZ). The
                                            Volcano-sedimentary Sequence between  
                                            Chotanagpur Gneissic Complex and Dalma
                                                                                  
                                            Volcanics.                            
         ii. Geochemical Investigation      In NGCM samples Au value ranges from 0 to 49
                                            with an average of 2.53 ppb. High Au values
                                            dominants in and around Chapodih area. The
                                            total REE concentration in stream sediments
                                            varies from 98.626 ppm to 451.61ppm.  
         5  Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                     
         i. Mineral Commodity               Gold and Copper.                      
         ii Exploration Potential of Block  Chandra et al., 1997 reported gold in quartz veins
                                            from the Taramba area up to 12.22 ppm.
                                            Anomalous Cu 0.19% values are restricted to the
                                            Taramba area. Sharan et al., reported 70 ppb
                                            from stream sediment samples and 50 ppb and
                                            300 ppb of gold from BRS within vein quartz in
                                            the Susri sector.
