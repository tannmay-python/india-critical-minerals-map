              HIGHLIGHTS OF          Detailed geophysical investigations (IP, Resistivity,
              GEOPHYSICALSTUDY       Magnetic and SP methods) was carried out for gold in
                                     Sudda area (Sonkurwa and Bagadha-Byodhihar blocks). In
                                     Sonkurwa block, high chargeability values have been
                                     obtained along a belt extending in the strike direction over a
                                     length of 1.8 km indicating a sulphide system in the
                                     phyllites intruded by parallel sheared quartz veins associated
                                     with gold mineralisation in the Southwest of the proposed
                                     Byodhihar block                                
              HIGHLIGHTS OF          Analytical results reveal that the positive encouraging
              GEOCHEMICAL STUDY      results of Au, Pb and As correspond to mineralized zone-I
                                     have been found. Analytical results of bed rock samples
                                     (BRS) show Au values up to 1.76 ppm, Ag up to 3 ppm, Pb
                                     up to 3900 ppm and As up to 3600 ppm.          
              HIGHLIGHTS OF          Under microscope, meta-quartz arenite is medium grained,
              PETROGRAPHIC AND       moderately sorted rock dominated by quartz (97 vol%) with
              MINEROGRAPHIC WORK     minor muscovite/sericite, biotite and accessory zircons and
                                     opaques. Petrological examination of quartz vein reveals
                                     extensive recrystallisation of quartz grains with sutured
                                     grain-grain margins (lobate contact) and sweepy or shadowy
                                     extinction (undulose extinction).

           5  MINERALISED ZONES                                                     
              NUMBER OF MINERAL ZONES Byodhar gold and basemetal block lies in western extension
                                     of the Chakariya gold mineralised zone in Amalihwa-
                                     Hathipathar-Kapurdei areas. The detailed studies and
                                     prospecting work have been carried out in Byodhihar,
                                     Bagdha, Sonkurwa, Amalihwa, Senduriya, Tisihwa,
                                     Jamalihwa, Khokhawa and Harrahwa villages to find out
                                     possible occurrences of gold mineralisation. The sulphide
                                     mineralisation is mainly associated with quartz veins and is
                                     structurally controlled. The vein type indicates that the
                                     mineralisation is epigenetic in nature.        
              TREND (DIP AND STRIKE) Two main events of deformation have been noticed which
                                     are responsible for the regional WNW-ESE trend of the
                                     Mahakoshal rocks and the third deformation is less
                                     pronounced in intensity and developed at high angle to the
                                     first two deformations.                        
                                     Overall trend of pyllite is N280° dipping subvertical to
                                     vertical in either side i.. NE-SW.

           9  TOPOGRAPHY                                                            
              TOPOSHEET NUMBER       63L/11                                         
              MORPHOLOGY OF THE AREA The topography of the area (63L/11) is rugged, with low to
                                     moderately high strike ridges and elongated narrow valleys.
                                     The maximum elevation is 420 metres above M.S.L. in
                                     south western part of the area and minimum elevation is 290
                                     metres above M.S.L. in the eastern part of the area. The area
                                     is drained by Gotan and Bijulnadi and its tributaries.
