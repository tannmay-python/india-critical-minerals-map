           Features                             Details                           
           Road            The area under investigation is situated 106km south-west of Raipur
                           and it is well connected by NH-53 up to Rajnandgaon and then
                           connected by Rajnandgaon-Dalli Rajhara Road.           
           Airport         The nearest airport is Raipur.                         
                                                                                  
         7 Hydrography                                                            
           Local     Surface The study area is mainly drained by many different first and second
           Drainage Pattern, order streams. Major water supply source in the study area is
           Rivers, etc     borewell, nala and ponds. The Kharkhara canal flowing in the
                           southern part of the block supplies water to meet the agricultural
                           demand.                                                
         8 Climate                                                                
                                                                                  
           Mean Annual Rainfall The annual average rainfall in the area is about 1500mm.
           Temperature     The area experiences severe dry, hot climate during summer, when
                           temperature rises about 46°C and cold climate during winter when
                           the temperature lower down to 8°C.                     
         9 Topography                                                             
           Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 64H/01                  
                                                                                  
           Morphology of the Most of the area is covered by soil and utilized for agricultural
           area            purpose and characterized by plain and flat ground with highest
                           elevation 340m above MSL in the western part and lowest elevation
                           is 327m above MSL at the eastern part.
