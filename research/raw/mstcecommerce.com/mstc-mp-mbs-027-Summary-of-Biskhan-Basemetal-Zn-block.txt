           5  MINERALISED ZONES                                                     
              NUMBER OF MINERAL ZONES A total three mineralized zones have been delineated in the
                                      Biskhan block. The Surface indications of mineralization in the
                                      area is scanty and are evident by visible specks, minute veins of
                                      sphalerite along with disseminated chalcopyrite, pyrites and
                                      gahnite are hosted in garnetiferous-qtz-biot-mus-schist and
                                      associated foliated rhyolites/garnetiferous rhyolites. The
                                      mineralization is parallel to dominant foliations and thus the
                                      mineralization is structurally controlled. The average length and
                                      width of Biskhan area mineralization is about 1000m x 800m.
              TREND (DIP AND STRIKE)  N65°E - S65°W dip 65° due southeast.          
                                                                                    
              TOTAL THICKNESS         2.58m to 39.40m average thickness 22.94m

           7  HYDROGRAPHY                                                           
                                                                                    
              LOCAL SURFACE DRAINAGE  The block area is drained by first and second order dry streams
              PATTERN (CHANNELS)      joining the Kharparanala which joins Bel River. The overall
                                      drainage pattern is coarse dendritic to sub-dendritic.
              RIVERS/ STREAMS         No major river in the block
