                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Iron Ore and Bauxite minerals, for
                                                                                  
          grant of Composite Licence for Bauxite minerals”                        
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B and 11 of the Mines and Minerals (Development
          and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified
                                                                                  
          thereunder, the Government of Maharashtra has identified:               
          a)  4 (four) mineral blocks [2 (two) blocks of Limestone, 1 (one block of Iron Ore and 1 (one) block
              of Bauxite] for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids for the purpose of grant of Mining
                                                                                  
              Lease.                                                              
          b)  4 (four) mineral blocks [4 (four) blocks of Bauxite] for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids
                                                                                  
              for the purpose of grant of Composite Licence.                      
          The list of mineral blocks is as under:                                 
          S. No. Name of Block                  Mineral     Concession Type       
                                                                                  
            1.   Khatweshwar-II Bauxite Block   Bauxite     Composite Licence     
            2.   Sadewadi-II Bauxite Block      Bauxite     Composite Licence     
            3.   Dinganwadi Bauxite Block       Bauxite     Composite Licence     
                                                                                  
            4.   Sandve Bauxite Block           Bauxite     Composite Licence     
            5.   Borgaon-Khandla Limestone Block Limestone    Mining Lease        
            6.   Dhunaki-Lakhamapur Limestone Block Limestone Mining Lease        
                                                                                  
            7.   Advali (Kudopi) Bauxite Block  Bauxite       Mining Lease        
            8.   Kalne Iron Ore Block          Iron Ore       Mining Lease        
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          The price of Tender Document for each mineral block is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) exclusive of
          GST. The GST shall be payable by reverse charge mechanism.              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in digital
                                                                                  
          and physical format from eligible bidders.                              
                                                                                  
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender
                                                                                  
          Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and other details

          Bidders may note that for composite licences the Mineral (Auction) Amendment Rules, 2022 have
                                                                                  
          dispensed with the requirement of indicating the division of the land into forest land, land owned by the
          State Government, and land not owned by the State Government and has provided the flexibility of
