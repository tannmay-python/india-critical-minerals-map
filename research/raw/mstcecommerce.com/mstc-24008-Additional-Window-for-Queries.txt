                               Additional Window for Queries                      
                                                                                  
            1. With reference to the “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of Critical and
               Strategic Minerals” dated 16th September 2025, issued by the Designated Officer,
               Ministry of Mines, Government of India, it is hereby notified that an additional window has
               been opened for submission of queries related to changes in the Tender Document arising
               from the second and third amendments in the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015.
                                                                                  
            2. Potential bidders may submit their queries on or before 17:00 hours on Sunday, 23rd
               November 2025.                                                     
                                                                                  
            3. Queries may be submitted by email to do-mom@gov.in, as specified in clause 7.2 of the
               Tender Document.                                                   
                                                                                  
            4. Queries unrelated to these amendments will not be entertained.
