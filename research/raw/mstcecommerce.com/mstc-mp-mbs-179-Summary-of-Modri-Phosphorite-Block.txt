          5  MINERALISED ZONES                                                      
             NUMBER OF MINERAL  One band of phosphorite dolomite and ferruginous chert.
             ZONES                                                                  
             NATURE OF          Phosphate mineralization in the dolomitic rocks is weak and is confined
             MINERALIZATION     to lenses and is highly irregular irregular, without any distinct control.
                                Richer phosphorite is mostly associated with ferruginous chert having
                                specularite veins, suggesting that the phosphate in the Modri area could
                                be of epigenetic origin in nature as it is associated with the iron fillings.
             TREND (DIP AND     The general strike of the dolomite here is varying between to N60°E-
             STRIKE)            S60°W with dips 70° due SSE and exhibits tight folding. The out crop
                                pattern is controlled by a doubly plunging F2 fold which can be

                                45° due N20°W and the other end is plunging at 40° due SE

          6  ACCESSIBILLITY                                                         
             FEATURES           DETAILS                                             
                                                                                    
             NEAREST RAIL HEAD  Indore is the major nearest railheads connecting the area. beside this
                                Khandwa-Ratlam-Ajmer section (meter gauge) of the western - railway
                                passes through the Barwaha tehsil.                  
             ROAD               Modri is situated 12 km east of Barwaha and Barwaha is connected with
                                the Indore Khandwa state highway. motorable roads-namely Barwah-
                                Katkut road connects this area. the interior parts of the area can be
                                approached by cart and foot tracks by fair weather roads.
                                                                                    
             AIRPORT            the nearest airport at Indore is 70 km              
          7  HYDROGRAPHY                                                            
             LOCAL SURFACE      Dendritic to sub-dendritic                          
             DRAINAGE PATTERN                                                       
             (CHANNELS)                                                             
             RIVERS/ STREAMS    Narmada river, which flows from ENE to WSW in extreme southern part
                                of the area of investigation, is the main drainage. it forms an island
                                named Omkarmandhata. the other important river draining the area is the
                                                                                    
                                Choral river which originates from the Malwa plateau and flows
                                southerly to meet the Narmada river. the Odhali nala, a tributary of choral
                                river also flows southerly to meet the choral river near Barwah.
                                Kariakund nala is another important drainage in the area.
          8  CLIMATE            The Block experiences semi-arid to dry climatic conditions with very hot
                                summers from April to June and mild winters from December to
                                February. the temperature rises up to 45°c in summer. during winter
                                season the temperature drops to around 10°c. dust storms and small wind
                                                                                    
                                vortices are quite common in summer. rains normally commence from
                                end of June and continue till September.            
             MEAN ANNUAL        The average annual rainfall is about 100 cm.        
             RAINFALL
