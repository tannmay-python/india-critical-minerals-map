                     Notice for Submission of Bids and Opening of Technical Bids    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            1. This is with reference to the “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of
               Critical and Strategic Minerals” dated November 29, 2023 issued by Ministry of Mines,
               Government of India. The Technical Bid Opening will be conducted at 11:00 hours on
                                                                                    
               Wednesday, February 21, 2024.                                        
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            2. All bidders are advised to adhere to the timelines mentioned in the Tender Document (and
               reproduced below) for timely submission of the Technical Bid (Technical + initial price
               offer) as follows:                                                   
                                                                                    
               S. No.      Event Description           Estimated Date               
                                                                                    
                 1.  Bid Due Date (Technical + initial price offer) Tuesday, February 20, 2024
                     [on or before 17:00 hours (IST)]                               
                                                                                    
                 2.  Opening of the Technical Bids   Wednesday, February 21, 2024   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            3. All bidders are advised to note that the Technical Bid shall be submitted on the e-auction
               portal and the duly executed original physical copies must be hand-delivered in a sealed
               envelope to the below-stated address so that they are received on or prior to the Bid Due
               Date, failing which the Technical Bid shall be considered as not received.
