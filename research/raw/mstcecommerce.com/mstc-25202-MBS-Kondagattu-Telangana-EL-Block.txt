          i.  Regional Geology         The proposed area forms part of the Karimnagar Granulite Terrain
                                       (KGT) within the Eastern Dharwar Craton. The Karimnagar
                                       Granulite Terrain, also referred to as the Karimnagar Granulite Belt
                                       (KGB), is bounded by the Pakhal–Sullavai Group (Middle to Late
                                       Proterozoic) and Gondwana formations (Palaeozoic to Mesozoic)
                                       of the Pranhita–Godavari Valley to the northeast, and by the
                                       Archaean low-grade rocks of the Eastern Dharwar Craton to the
                                       southwest.                                 
                                       Entire region has undergone four phases of deformation, the
                                                                                  
                                       first of which is shown by isoclinal folds in banded magnetite
                                       quartzite (BMQ). In relation to the D2 deformation, this
                                       demonstrates the pre- to syn-tectonic emplacement of
                                       younger granitoids (GMS suite). NE-SW trending basic
                                       dykes, which are post-tectonic to D3 deformation along the
                                       axial planar surface of F3 folds, are used to infer the presence
                                       of the D3. There are brittle-ductile shear zones as a result of
                                       D4.                                        
          ii. Geochemical Investigation National geochemical mapping (NGCM) was carried out
                                       during FS 2022–23 in toposheet No. 56J/14. The analytical
                                       results indicate TREE values in the EL block ranging from
                                       808.21 ppm to 1247.45 ppm. The threshold value of TREE is
                                       782.80 ppm, and the median value is 444.67 ppm. Ten
                                       samples yielded TREE values above 800 ppm, of which four
                                       samples recorded values exceeding 1000 ppm.
