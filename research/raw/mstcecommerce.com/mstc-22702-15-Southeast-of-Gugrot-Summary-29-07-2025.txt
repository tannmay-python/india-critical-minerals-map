        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Nearest railway station is Mokalsar Railway station which is 15 km from
                          the study area.                                         
                                                                                  
          Road            The area is well connected with Jaipur by NH 58, NH 162 and SH-68. The
                          study area is well traversed with metalled, semi metalled and gravel
                          roads.                                                  
          Airport         The nearest airport is at Jodhpur located 129 km from study area.
        7 Hydrography                                                             
          Local     Surface Drainage pattern of the Gugrot area is dendritic to sub-dendritic
                                                                                  
          Drainage Pattern, controlled by lithology and joint pattern present in the area. Drainage
          Rivers, etc     density is higher in hilly outcrop and established dunes present in
                          southern part of the study area. No major stream flows in the area.
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall Average rainfall 277mm per year.                   
          Temperature     During summer (March to June), the maximum temperature generally
                                                                                  
                          varies between 46° and 51° C. Night temperatures decrease considerably
                          to 20°–29° C. January is the coldest month. During winter (December to
                          February), minimum temperatures may fall down to 0° C at night.
        9 Topography                                                              
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 45C/06                   
          Morphology of the The study area forms a part of Thar Desert and displays aeolian sand and
                                                                                  
          area            dunes along with high hills with steep slope. The topography is mainly
                          controlled by geological features such lithology, major joints traversing
                          the lithology, fault and aeolian activity in the area. The elevation observed
                          in the area varies from 230 m in north to 440m in south. The low lying
                          areas along the foothill are covered with windblown sand.
