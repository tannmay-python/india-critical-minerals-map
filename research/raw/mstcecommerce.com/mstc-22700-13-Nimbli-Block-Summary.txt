        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones The average phosphorite thickness in the block is 4.91m at >5% P2O5.
                                                                                  
          Trend           The phosphorite rock unit has NE-SW strike, dip 35o-80o due
          (Dip and Strike) westwards/eastward due to folding.                     
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, rock
                          phosphate mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To
                          infer the details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is
                          required. Hence block area is recommended for grant of Composite
                          Licence.

        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Fatehgarh (FGR) Railway Station                       
          Road            Nimbli block is well connected with sub-district headquarter Fatehgarh
                                                                                  
                          (Tehsildar office) which is situated 55km away in northeastern
                          direction from the block and falls on NH 68. District headquarter
                          Jaisalmer is lying in northern direction from the block which at a
                          distance of 1115 km, can be approached by two national highways
                          connecting Kohra and Jaisalmer viz., NH-11 and NH68.    
          Airport         Jodhpur Airport                                         
        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface Localized ephemeral streams and water courses are present in the
          Drainage Pattern, Nimbli and adjacent areas, apart from local streams. Regionally the
          Rivers, etc     area is drained by Luni River which flows from Samdari, passes through
                          Balotra.                                                
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall 280.14mm                                           
          Temperature     The temperature varies from 48° C in summer to 2° C in winter.
                                                                                  
        9 Topography                                                              
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 40J/16                   
          Morphology of the Area is covered mostly by aeolian sand, widespread sand dunes
          area            morphology is broken by steeply rising isolated hills and flat rocky
                          areas. The hill ridges of Randha and Birsingha are east-west trending
                          and rise abruptly to about 150 meters from the ground level, these hill
                                                                                  
                          ranges are located in southeast direction from the block.
