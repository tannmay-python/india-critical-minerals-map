          Studies        roundness, from sub-angular to sub-rounded. It contains quartz,

                         rounded with variable sizes. The rock is both mineralogically and
                         texturally matured.                                      
                         The glauconite mineralization is associated with coarse grained
                         glauconitic sandstone, thinly laminated fine to medium grained
                         sandstone and intercalation of glauconitic sandstone and shale of

          Trend (Dip and The major target zone is the NE-SW trending glauconite bearing
          Strike)        sandstone of Bhalukona Formation present towards northern part of the
                         block. The glauconitic sandstone unit being drilled is mainly horizontally
                         bedded. The dip varies from 5° to 15° towards southerly to SE.
          Justification  Based on achievement of encouraging results in conformity with the
                         established views, mechanization of mining and processing techniques
                         could be adopted and usage of local glauconite should be popularized.
                         This will effectively provide an indigenous, workable alternative to the
                         huge quantum of imported potash fertilizer to a great extent. To infer
                         the details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required.
                         Hence, block area is recommended for grant of Composite License.
