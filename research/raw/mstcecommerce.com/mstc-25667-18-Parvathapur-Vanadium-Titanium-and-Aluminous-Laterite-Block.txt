        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
                                                                                  
          Mineralised Zones Mineralized zones identified for Vanadium, Titanium and Aluminous
                          Laterite.                                               
          Trend           The eastern and northern parts of the block are characterized by gentle
          (Dip and Strike) slopes and flat terrain, whereas the southern and western sections
                          exhibit scarps and steep slopes.                        
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, Vanadium,
                          Titanium & Aluminous Laterite mineralisation has been identified in the
                                                                                  
                          block area. To infer the details of the mining feasibility, detailed
                          exploration is required. Hence block area is recommended for grant of
                          Composite Licence.                                      
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest rail facilities are available in Zaheerabad, linking it to the
                          broader region.                                         
                                                                                  
          Road            Surrounded by Chincholi Mandal on the west, Kohir and Jharasangam
                          Mandals on the east, and Peddemul Mandal to the south, Parvathapur
                          enjoys a strategic location. Despite its proximity to Zaheerabad, which
                          is the nearest town at 15 kilometers away and provides road
                          connectivity                                            
          Airport         Rajiv Gandhi International Airport in Hyderabad         
        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface The study area is extensively dissected, with a complex network of
          Drainage Pattern, ephemeral nalas (streams) that carve through the landscape, forming a
          Rivers, etc     dendritic to subparallel drainage pattern. The major stream, Madhura
                          Nala, is a perennial watercourse, playing a pivotal role in the area’s
                          drainage system.                                        
        8 Climate                                                                 
                                                                                  
          Mean Annual Rainfall Annual rainfall ranging from 800 to 1300 mm.       
          Temperature     Temperature variations are marked, with winter minimums and
                          maximums ranges between 18°C and 25°C, and summer temperatures
                          fluctuating between 32°C and 38°C.
