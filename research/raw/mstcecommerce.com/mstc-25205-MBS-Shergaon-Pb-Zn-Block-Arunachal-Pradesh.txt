         4  Exploration Details                                                   
                                                                                  
         i. Regional Geology          The area forms a part of the Lesser Himalayan
                                      metasedimentary sequence of Arunachal Pradesh. An
                                      assemblage of unfossiliferous low grade meta-sediments
                                      of arenaceous, argillaceous and calcareous composition
                                      thrust over by the Bomdila gneisses. The litho-
                                      assemblages form a NE-SW trending belt extending from
                                      Rupa to Shergaon. The area is located between two major
                                      thrusts i.e. MCT towards NW and MBT towards SE in the
                                      Lesser Himalayan sequence of Arunachal Himalaya
         ii. Geochemical Investigation Based on NGCM analysis of stream sediment samples
                                     maximum value of Pb is 359 ppm and maximum Zn is 836
                                     ppm.                                         
         5  Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                     
         i. Mineral Commodity         Lead-Zinc                                   
         ii Exploration Potential of Block Bhushan et al., reported anomalous values in the soil
                                     samples having Pb content up to 1. 12% and Zn up to
                                     0.79% while during FS 1989- 90, soil samples show Pb
                                     values ranges from 20 to 5900 ppm and Zn ranged from 20
                                     to 6600 ppm. Sar et al. reported a promising stretch of 150
                                     meters in the western scarps of the Amritganga stream
                                     having a cumulative thickness of ore zone of 3 to 12m with
                                     Pb & Zn concentration ranging from 1% to 10%. Wanchoo
                                                                                  
                                     et al. reported the mineralized dolomite of the Shergaon
                                     area from 204 samples, with 14 samples having Pb ranging
                                     between 1% and 4.1% while four samples have analyzed
                                     Zn between 1% and 3.12%; 33 samples analyzed Zn
                                     between 0.1% to 0.37%. Wanchoo et al., 1993-94 reported
                                     from drilling in the Mukatung and Vasundhara - Amrit
                                     Ganga blocks Shergaon area, mineralization in dolomite
                                     ranging from 0.75% to 5% Pb over a thickness of 13.45m
                                     at shallow depths established by BH - SAV-1. From
                                     NGCM  stream sediment samples maximum value of Pb up
                                     to 359 ppm, Zn values in the range of 41 to 836 ppm, Ba
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     20-1148 ppm, Rb 25-390 ppm, Sr 24-154 ppm, Zr 10-703
                                     ppm, Li 4-56 ppm, Bi <0.1- 21 ppm.
