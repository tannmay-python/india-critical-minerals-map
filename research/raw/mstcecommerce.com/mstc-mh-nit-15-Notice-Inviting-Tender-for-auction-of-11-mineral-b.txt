                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Graphite, Iron ore, Bauxite,
          Kyanite & Sillimanite and Manganese Ore Minerals and for grant of Composite Licence
                                                                                  
          for Copper, Copper & Gold and Manganese Ore Minerals”                   
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development
                                                                                  
          and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified
          thereunder, the Government of Maharashtra has identified:               
                                                                                  
          a)  8 (eight) mineral blocks for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids for the purpose of
                                                                                  
              grant of Mining Lease (details as per table below).                 
          b)  3 (three) mineral blocks for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids for the purpose of
                                                                                  
              grant of Composite Licence (details as per table below).            
          The list of mineral blocks is as under:                                 
                                                                                  
            S.       Name of Block           Mineral      Type of Concession      
           No.                                                                    
           1.  Dongargaon Block         Limestone           Mining Lease          
                                                                                  
           2.  Kurai Block              Limestone           Mining Lease          
           3.  Pardi (Kinwat) Block     Limestone           Mining Lease          
           4.  Bidwadi Block            Graphite            Mining Lease          
                                                                                  
           5.  Bhisi Block              Iron Ore            Mining Lease          
           6.  Girola (Jamdi) Block     Kyanite and Sillimanite Mining Lease      
           7.  Chikhalgaon (West) Block Bauxite             Mining Lease          
           8.  Hivara Block             Manganese Ore       Mining Lease          
                                                                                  
           9.  Mandri Panchala Block    Manganese Ore     Composite Licence       
           10. Minzari Block            Copper            Composite Licence       
           11. Ghanpur Mudholi West Block Copper and Gold Composite Licence       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both
                                                                                  
          in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.
