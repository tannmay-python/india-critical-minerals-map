         4   Exploration Details                                                  
         i.  Regional Geology         The area is part of the Eastern Dharwar Craton (EDC),
                                      characterized by a reactivated and remobilized terrain. It
                                      features composite batholiths composed of granodiorite,
                                                                                  
                                      granite, adamellite, and quartz monzodiorite.
                                      Paleoproterozoic granitic plutons in the region are emplaced
                                      within an older gneissic terrain, as evidenced by the presence
                                      of amphibolite, migmatite, and gneiss in the form of bands
                                      and patches, along with mafic enclaves within the granitoids.
                                      These rock formations are regarded as the northern extensions
                                                                                  
                                      of the Peninsular Gneissic Complex (PGC).   
         ii. Geochemical Investigation Only 50% of the area is covered under the National
                                      Geochemical Mapping (NGCM) program, specifically in
                                      parts of Toposheet No. 56/09. The coverage is limited to the
                                      southwestern (SW) and northwestern (NW) portions of the
                                                                                  
                                      area, where elevated values for tantalum (Ta) is recorded.
         5   Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                    
         i.  Mineral Commodity        Tantalum                                    
         ii  Exploration Potential of Block Tantalum concentrations of 110 ppm, 120 ppm, and 130 ppm
                                      have been reported in pink granite, sheared granite, altered
                                                                                  
                                      granite, pegmatite, and quartz veins located 1.5 km north of
                                      Kondalwadi. These values are 50 to 60 times higher than the
                                      crustal abundance of 1–2 ppm, strongly suggesting that the
                                      block has significant potential for tantalum mineralization.

         ii. Road                     The area is well-connected by an extensive network of
                                      roadways. State Highway-204 links Naigaon, Narsi, Biloli,
                                      and Diglur to Nanded, the district headquarters, and further
                                                                                  
                                      extends to Nagpur. National Highway No. 7 traverses the
                                      eastern part of the study area, providing additional
                                      connectivity. State Highway-225 passes through Narsi and
                                      Biloli, joining NH No. 7 at Dichpally. Additionally, the area
                                      is served by numerous fair-weather metaled roads, cart tracks,
                                                                                  
                                      and footpaths, ensuring accessibility even to remote locations.
