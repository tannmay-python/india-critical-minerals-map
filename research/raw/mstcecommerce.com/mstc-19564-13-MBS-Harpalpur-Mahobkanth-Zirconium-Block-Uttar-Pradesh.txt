         4  Exploration Details      --                                           
         i. Regional Geology           The area comprises rocks of Older Metamorphic Group
                                       of Bundelkhand Granitoid Complex (BGC) of  
                                       Archaean to Palaeo-Proterozoic age and Quaternary
                                       sediments of Early - Middle Pleistocene to Holocene
                                       age. The rocks of Older Metamorphic Group are
                                                                                  
                                       represented by metasediments, quartzite, quartz-biotite
                                       schist and amphibolites. It occurs as enclaves in BGC.
                                       BGC  is represented by undifferentiated granite,
                                       granodiorite, gneisses with quartz reef and dolerite
                                       dykes. The Quaternary sediments are represented by
                                       Older Alluvium and Newer Alluvium. The Older
                                       Alluvium is represented by Banda Alluvium which are
                                                                                  
                                       residual cum fluvial outwash deposits comprising
                                       polycyclic sequences of red-brown sand, silt and
                                       quartzo-feldpathic material with kankar and grit.
         ii. Geochemical Investigation Analysis of composite sample show Zr values from 229
                                       ppm to 1125 ppm. The spatial distribution of Zr in the
                                       area shows that the higher values (above   
                                       threshold=771.62) are observed near Lahchuraghat and
                                       Kohnia in Dhasan river basin, Naipur in Keolari river
                                       basin and near Pipri in Birma river basin.
