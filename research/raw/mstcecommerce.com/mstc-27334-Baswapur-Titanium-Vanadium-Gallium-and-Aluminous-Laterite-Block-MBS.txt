        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The area is accessible via Tandur and Vikarabad railway stations on the
                                                                                  
                           South-Central Railway network.                         
          Road             The block is accessible from Hyderabad via NH-65 and the Hyderabad–
                           Vikarabad–Bantwaram road network. It is situated approximately 90 km
                           northwest of Hyderabad.                                
          Airport          The nearest airport is Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA),
                           Hyderabad.                                             
        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface The drainage is predominantly dendritic to sub-dendritic and forms part
          Drainage Pattern, of the Kagna River sub-basin. No major perennial rivers or reservoirs
          Rivers, etc      occur within the block.                                
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The area experiences a tropical wet-and-dry climate with annual rainfall
                           of 800–1300 mm.                                        
                                                                                  
          Temperature      Summer temperatures range from 32–45°C, while winter temperatures
                           range between 18–25°C.
