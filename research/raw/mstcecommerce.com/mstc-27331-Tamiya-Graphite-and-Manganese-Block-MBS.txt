        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The average rainfall of the district is 1423.5 mm. 
          Temperature     The climate of the district is extreme. It is hot and dry in summer, hot
                          and humid in rainy season and cold and dry in winter. In summer the
                          temperature rises up to high of 44°C, and in winter it falls as low as
                          7°C.
