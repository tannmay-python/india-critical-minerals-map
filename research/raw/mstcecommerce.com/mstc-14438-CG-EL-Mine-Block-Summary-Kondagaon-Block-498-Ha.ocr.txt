The Bastar craton of the central Indian shield is bounded by the
Eastern Ghats, the Mahanadi and the Godavari rifts and the Son-
Narmada lineament. It consists predominantly of gneisses and
granitoids with supracrustal rocks. As the area contains basement
crystalline rocks and lots of mafic dyke swarms which are favorable
zone for kimberlitic intrusion and other rare minerals.
Major structural trends suggest that the study area may be proximal to
Bastar Craton and Eastern Ghat Mobile Belt (EGMB) weak zone,
10  Brief Description  hence, favorable for kimberlite emplacements.
Several geological investigations by GSI has suspected presence of
Kimberlite indicator minerals in the area.On the basis of information
available, further detailed exploration and petrographic studies may be
carried out in the proposed area for kimberlitic indicators and other
minerals.
Rare earth minerals occur in association with rare metals mineral or
independently that are hosted both in pegmatites and their parent
granites also reported in Bastar Craton region
