                     GENERAL  INFORMATION  ABOUT MINERAL BLOCK                      
                                                                                    
                      Features                         Details                      
          1 Location                                                                
            Mineral Block              Bagwari-Khirkhori Copper (Cu) and associated 
                                       minerals block                               
          i Location                   -                                            
          ii Corner Points (Latitude, Longitude) Latitude: 24°20'55.74"N to 24°20'39.89"N
                                       Longitude: 81°47'9.41"E to 81°49'17.10"E     
            Villages                   Khirkhori, Chhirauhi & Bagwari               
            Tehsil/ Taluka             Gopadbanas                                   
            District                   Sidhi                                        
                                                                                    
            State                      Madhya Pradesh                               
          2 Area (hectares)                                                         
            Total Area of Block for Auction 299.341 Ha.                             
          3 Exploration                                                             
            Status (G2/ G3/ G4 etc.)   G-4 was carried out in 141.75 sq. km (out of which
                                       2.99 sq. km was recommended for G3 exploration
                                       which is now raised for composite license auction).
            Exploration Agency         Mineral Exploration and Consultancy Ltd.     
                                       (Formerly Mineral Exploration Corporation Limited)
            Surface sampling/Pitting/ Trenching Surface Sampling Results            
            Details                    A total of five surface samples were collected,
                                                                                    
                                       identifying four copper-rich zones: MSB-BR27 (6218
                                       ppm Cu), MSB-BR12 (4710 ppm Cu), MSB-BR47    
                                       (3020 ppm Cu), and MSB-BR31 (1755 ppm Cu).   
                                       Additionally, MSB-BR09 (6218 ppm Cu) exhibited
                                       both copper and gold mineralization.         
                                                                                    
                                       Pitting/Trenching Results                    
                                       A total of seven samples were collected, out of which
                                       two confirmed copper mineralization. Trenching
                                       revealed copper values in MSB-T3 (6576 ppm Cu,
                                       Bagwari Village), MSB-T5 (5120 ppm Cu, northeast
                                       of Kushmahar), and MSB-T2 (4601 ppm Cu,      
                                       Khirkhori Village). MSB-T1 (1440 ppm Cu, Madwa
                                       Tola, Kushmahar) and MSB-T4 (1429 ppm Cu, south
                                       of Kushmahar) also showed significant copper 
                                       values. Notably, MSB-BR27 and MSB-BR47       
                                       correspond to MSB-T3 and MSB-T2, respectively,
                                                                                    
                                       confirming the continuity of mineralization. 
            Total Number of Boreholes with Borehole MSB-01 and MSB-03 were drilled at trench
            meterage                   location T3 and T2  respectively. Copper

                                       during the G-4 stage: MSB-01 (1.44% Cu over 0.50m
                                       at 32.00m depth) and MSB-03 (0.76% Cu over 1.0m
                                       at 48.85m, 1.49% Cu over 0.50m at 70.50m), with

          4 Quantity of Minerals (Grade wise)                                       
            Minerals                   Copper (Cu) and Associated Mineral           
            Total Geological Resources Not Estimated                                
            Average quality            The estimated average copper grade in the Block
                                       based on previous exploration data is approximately
                                       0.50% to 1.50% Cu in mineralized zones.      
                                                                                    
          5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
            Number of Mineral Zones    Two                                          
            Mineralization             In  the present exploration block, copper    
                                       mineralization is seen in the form of specks, veins
                                       and lenses of azurite, pyrite, malachite stains in
                                       surface, trench and as well as in Boreholes. 
                                       Mineralization in the block is structurally controlled,
                                       primarily occurring within shear zones, quartz veins,
                                       and lithological contacts, predominantly in schistose
                                       rocks and granite gneiss. Chalcopyrite was observed
                                       in borehole cores.                           
            Trend (Dip and Strike)     The Block area exhibits a NE-SW to ENE-WSW   
                                       strike (N50°E-S50°W to N80°E - S80°W) with a 
                                       moderate to steep dip of 40° – 60° towards SSE.
                                                                                    
            Average Thickness          0.50m to 1.00m                               
          6 Accessibility                                                           
            Nearest Rail Head          Beohari (55 Km in SW direction), Rewa (60 Km in
                                       NW direction)                                
            Road                       Connected by Sidhi-Majhauli state highway ,located
                                       12 km SSW of Sidhi                           
            Airport                    Varanasi (160 km in NE direction)            
          7 Hydrography                                                             
            Local Surface Drainage Pattern Drainage pattern in the block is dendriticwith general
            (Channels)                 slope towards north. Streams flow towards north
                                       converting in Tendumand DeonarNala.          
                                                                                    
            Rivers/ Streams            Tendum Nala, Deonar Nala.

                                       340m AMSL.
