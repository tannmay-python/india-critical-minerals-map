          5  Mineralised Zones                                                      
             Number of Mineral Zones The vein quartz is the main host rock for copper
                                 mineralization. To some extent, mineralization has also been
                                 noticed in granites. Vein quartz bodies present in the block
                                 are confined to the sheared zones and fractures which form
                                 main channels for the entry of mineralizing solution. The
                                 main control of mineralization is structural and intensity of
                                 mineralization is controlled by degree of fracturing.
                                 Geochemical sampling has indicated two anomalous zones,
                                 one in the north and other in the southern part of the block.
                                 The overall results of geophysical surveys with IP, SP,
                                 magnetic, resistivity and VES have also indicated anomalous
                                 zone between S-4 and N3 profile. Thus the results are in
                                 corroboration with the results of geochemical sampling.
                                 Test drilling carried out has indicated presence of copper
                                                                                    
                                 mineralisation in the area.                        
                                 On the basis of integrated exploration, it can be concluded
                                 that mineralisation is found to occur over a strike length of
                                 about 600 m.                                       
             Trend (Dip and Strike) The trend of gneissosity is N10° E-S10° W to almost N-S
                                 with mostly westerly dip of 75° with few exceptions dipping
                                 due east.                                          
             Justification       Block area is the extension of Malanjkhand Copper Deposit.
                                 Geological data obtained from Geological, Geophysical,
                                 Geochemical, Trenching and Drilling during G4 level of
                                 exploration indicate the presence of copper mineralisation in
                                 the block area. It also indicated total Reconnaissance copper
                                 ore resource of 0.46 million tonnes (Net resources 0.36 million
                                 tonnes) with an average grade of 0.37% Cu over a strike
                                 length of 400m in the block. Hence, as the occurrence of
                                                                                    
                                 Copper ore mineralization is established and ‘Reconnaissance
                                 Resource’ (334) of copper is estimated, the block may be
                                 recommended for putting for auction of composite license
                                 (CL)for up-gradation of level of exploration to assess the full
                                 economic potentiality of the copper mineralization in the
                                 block.

          9  Topography                                                             
             Toposheet Number    64B/12                                             
             Morphology of the area The topography around the Shitalpani is almost flat to gently
                                 sloping. In general, about 80% area is sloping towards
                                                                                    
                                 east/north-east except a small south-western part of the block
                                 which slopes towards west. The maximum elevation recorded
                                 in the block is 668 m. The minimum elevation is 576 m. to the
                                 south of Shitalpani village.
