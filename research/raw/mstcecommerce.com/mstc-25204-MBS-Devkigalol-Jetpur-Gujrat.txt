         4   Exploration Details                                                  
         i.  Regional Geology         The area is dominantly occupied by basaltic rocks belonging to
                                      Deccan volcanics (Cretaceous to Eocene). The area, except the
                                      southwestern part, is almost a flat terrain with small, isolated
                                      hillocks and mounds. The Girnar Hill forms a part of the
                                      southwestern part of the study area (Eastern part of Junagadh).
                                      The lava flows of the area are intruded by the Girnar Igneous
                                      Massif.                                     
         ii. Geochemical Investigation NGCM data available                        
         5   Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                    
         i.  Mineral Commodity        Vanadium and associated mineralization.     
         ii  Exploration Potential of Block The analytical results of stream sediment samples carried out
                                                                                  
                                      during NGCM shows threshold values of vanadium (~ 264
                                      ppm) above crustal abundance (190 ppm) with maximum
                                      concentration of Vanadium up to 277 ppm in the proposed
                                      block. The high concentration zones of V occur around
                                      Devkigalol area and at the periphery of Girnar reserve forest,
                                                                                  
                                      where litho units are amygdular basalt and dykes of basalt and
                                      dolerite of Deccan volcanics. The acidic dyke near the Chanaka
                                      village, which is highly oxidised, brecciated and limonitized
                                      may be studied further in detail. The analytical value of gold in
                                                                                  
                                      12 nos of samples and copper in 3 nos samples ranges from 2
                                      to 5 ppb and 100-338 ppm respectively in the stream sediment
                                      samples which may be studied further in detail. Based on these
                                      analytical results of the stream sediments samples, the area may
                                      be further be explored for vanadium and associated
                                                                                  
                                      mineralization in the toposheet no. 41K/10.
