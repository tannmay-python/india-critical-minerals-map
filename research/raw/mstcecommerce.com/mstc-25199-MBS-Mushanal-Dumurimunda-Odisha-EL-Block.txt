         4   Exploration Details                                                  
             Regional geology           The proposed EL block constitutes a part of the
                                        Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt of Archean to Proterozoic
                                        age and comprises of strongly metamorphosed,
                                        deformed sequence of metasedimentary and gneissic
                                        rocks belonging to the Khondalite Group of Eastern
                                        Ghat Mobile Belt (EGMB). This is the oldest
                                        metamorphosed sediments and is represented by the
                                        rocks of Khondalite Group which include   
                                        garnetiferous quartzo-feldspathic sillimanite
                                        schist/gneiss, quartzite and the associated calc silicate
                                        rocks. Garnetiferous granite-gneiss and megacrystic
                                                                                  
                                        garnetiferous granite-gneiss are intrusive bodies
                                        within this group. The various rock types exposed in
                                        the block are quartzite, quartz-garnet-sillimanite-
                                        graphite schist, calc- granulites, charnockites,
                                        pyroxene granulite and                    
                                        granite gneiss                            
             Geochemical Investigation  Higher concentration of REEs has been reported in
                                        the stream sediments. A total of 62 nos. of stream
                                        sediment composite samples have been collected
                                        from the proposed block. The tREE values in these
                                        samples range from                        
                                        386.3 to 7046.6 ppm with an average of 1257.84
                                        ppm.

         ii    Exploration potential of the block  The proposed block exposes mostly the rocks of
                                          the Eastern Ghats granulite belt viz. quartzite,
                                          quartz-garnet-sillimanite-graphite schist, calc
                                          granulites, charnockite, pyroxene granulite and
                                          granite gneiss. Several anomalous zones for
                                          REEs have been identified from the stream
                                          sediment geochemical data.              
                                         The granulitic rocks of the Eastern Ghats Mobile
                                          Belt are the source of REEs within the stream
                                          sediments. Several authors have reported
                                                                                  
                                          unmappable pegmatite bodies in toposheet no.
                                          65M/05.                                 
                                          These rocks are the suitable lithologies to host
                                          REE mineralization. Also, the potentially of REE
                                          in soil horizons overlying granites or pegmatites
                                          may  be checked. Further, the faults and
                                          lineaments present in the block are the favorable
                                          locales for hosting REE and associated  
                                          mineralization.
