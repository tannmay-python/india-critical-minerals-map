          Highlights  of One sqkm area has been covered under ground magnetic survey with
          Geophysical Survey 10m x 100m grid pattern.Qualitative interpretation of magnetic data is
                         analyzed through various maps. For example anomalies corresponding
                         to highs, lows, contour patterns etc. indicate the signatures of geology.
                         Minimum anomaly of -95 nT (W370/N100) over weathered material
                         around western boundary of survey area and maximum anomaly +15144
                                                                                  
                         nT (E250/S50) over sulphide zone. Magnetic anomaly contour map has
                         been prepared. Based on qualitative interpretation, five zones have
                         been delineated. These are named as Zone I, Zone II, Zone III, Band I and
                         Band II as seen over the map. Occurrences of rocks having
                         iron/sulphides have been reported by geologists in that particular zone
                         during DM survey.

          Strike)                                                                 
          Justification  On the basis of the investigation carried out at Depo area, the strike
                         extension of the Band-2 i.e., southern band may be explored on both
                         sides and deeper (2nd level) boreholes may be drilled for establishing
                         sub-surface continuity of the lode and for resource estimation. The
                         boreholes have analysed mineralised zones average grade of 0.04% to
                         0.48% V2O5 and 5.33% to 11.91% fixed carbon. Block area needs further
                         detailed exploration for the feasibility of mining and to ascertain the
                         measured mineral resources.
