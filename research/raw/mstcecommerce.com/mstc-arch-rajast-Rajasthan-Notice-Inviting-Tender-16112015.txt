                                  NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT)                      
                                            FOR                                     
                              GRANT OF MINING LEASE FOR LIME STONE                  
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957, its
          subsequent amendments including Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2015 and in
                                                                                    
          accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified there under, the Government of Rajasthan has notified mineral
          blocks for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease for mineral Lime Stone through electronic auction and hereby invites bids
                                                                                    
          in digital format only from eligible bidders.                             
                                                                                    
          The interested and eligible bidders can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the Tender Document on
                                                                                    
          payment of  the  tender fee  of  Rs.  1,14,500/- from the MSTC   website  
          <http://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp>. Upon registration on the above mentioned MSTC website
          eligible bidder can purchase tender document. On sucessfull registration , eligible bidders will obtain login ID and
                                                                                    
          Pasword necessarily for participation in the e-auction process.
