         5 Mineralised Zones                                                      
                                                                                  
           Mineralised Zones In Bastar region, tin mineralization occur as discrete crystals and
                          disseminations mostly in pegmatites emplaced in the basic rocks and
                          to a less extent in those traversing the sericite quartzite and schists.
                          Along with cassiterite, tantalite, Columbite, Lepidolite, amblygonite,
                          beryl and rubidium, caesium bearing micas are present.  
                                                                                  
           Trend          The amount and direction of dip varies widely from 22° to 35° and
           (Dip and Strike) from N to ENE.                                        
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
           Justification  Based on the G4 level of exploration carried out in the area, Tin ore
                          mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer the
                          details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required.
                          Hence block area is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.

         9 Topography                                                             
           Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 65 F/14                 
           Morphology of the The block area has a gently undulating topography with the general
           area           slope towards south. The north-eastern part of the area is marked by
                          a prominent NW-SE trending hill whose maximum elevation is about
                          270m above MSL. The hill gradually slopes down to a plain having a
                          general elevation varying from 200 to 225 m above MSL.
