                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                    
              “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Graphite Mineral and for grant of
                                                                                    
                            Composite Licence for Limestone Mineral”                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B and Section 11 of the Mines and Minerals
          (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules,
                                                                                    
          2015 as amended from time to time notified thereunder, the Government of Odisha has identified 2
          (two) Minerals Blocks (1 (One) block of Graphite and 1 (One) block of Limestone) for electronic
                                                                                    
          auction and hereby invites tenders for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease and Composite Licence.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in
                                                                                    
          digital and physical format from eligible bidders.                        
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the
                                                                                    
          Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and
          other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only
                                                                                    
          and   can  be   downloaded  from  the   website of   MSTC    Limited      
          (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).               
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above website. On successful
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-
                                                                                    
          auction process. Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summary are available free of cost on
          the website of MSTC Limited (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).
