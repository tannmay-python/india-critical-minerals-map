        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Sonbhadra is the nearest railway station from study area.
          Road             The area is well connected with Sonbhadra, Allahabad, Deen Dayal
                           Upadhyay Nagar and Varanasi by roads. Chakia is situated about 35
                           km from Deen Dayal Upadhyay Nagar on Delhi-Calcutta Grand Trunk
                           Road (NH-19).                                          
                                                                                  
          Airport          The nearest airport is Lal Bahadur Shashtri Airport, Babatpur,
                           Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.
