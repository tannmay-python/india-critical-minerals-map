                                                                                                                                                                                             
           In    early      days,      Minerals           exploration             was      done        by    individuals,            known         in   the     western                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           world       as    prospector.             Mineral          discovery           depended              on    the     skill,    diligence          and      persistence                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           of   these       individuals                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           In    India      , organized            mineral          exploration             was      initiated         by    the     GSI    sometimes               after     its                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           inception           in   1851.                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           Now        MECL,         State        DMGs,         Notified          Public       Exploration             Agencies,            Notified         Public                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           Exploration             Agencies           are     undertaking              exploration             as    an    agency         of   the     SG/CG         or    through                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
           NMET         funding.

                                                Exploration                                         Licence                            [EL]                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖Around            2  lakh    sq.km.       of   OGP      area    is  yet    to  be    covered        under       reconnaissance              by    Government             agencies.                
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖Before          2023       amendment,              there      was     no    provision         for   grant      of   a  mineral        concession           for   undertaking            full      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
        range      of   exploration          starting       from     reconnaissance               to   prospecting           operations.                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖World            over       exploration            of    deep-seated              minerals          is   done        by    junior       mining         companies             who        get       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
        exploration          rights     over     a  large     area.                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖Demand             was     made        by   industry       associations           to   bring     similar      concept        in  the    Act.                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖National            Mineral        Exploration           Policy      2016      (Pvt    agency       would        engage        in  exploration           on   Revenue          share      in      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
        Premium          or   royalty      accruing         to  the    State)                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖After          extensive          consultations             it   was       decided         to    allow       private        exploration            agencies          to    undertake              
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
        exploration          from      the    reconnaissance               stage,     within      the    auction        regime.                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
    ❖MMDR             Act    was     amended           w.e.f.     17.08.2023           to   introduce         exploration          licence.

      ❖    A   concession           granted        through         auction        for   undertaking             reconnaissance               and     prospecting            operations.                
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
      ❖    Granted         for    29   deep-seated             and     critical     minerals         in  7th     Schedule         to   the    Act:                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                    Potash,        REE,     Lithium,        Cobalt,        Copper,        Lead,       Graphite,         Vanadium,            Nickel,       Tin,    Platinum          Group             
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
      of    Elements,            Zinc,      Cadmium,             Gold,        Silver,      Diamond,             Rock       Phosphate,             Molybdenum,                  Rhenium,                
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
      Tungsten,          Tantalum,           Niobium,          Tellurium,         Selenium,          Indium,         etc.                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
      ❖Max.          area     of   EL   block      -1000      sq.km.;        Max.      area     limit    for   a   person       in   a  State     -  5000      sq.km.                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
      ❖Any        person        interested          may      suggest        an   area     to   Central*         Government              to   auction        for   EL.                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
      ❖Central           Government              may      initiate      auction        of   block       identified         by    the    GSI,     DMG       or    any    other       agency             
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
          of  central       Government.               (Committee             shall    identify       and     recommend               to  the     Central       Government)

       • Fragmented             and     uncoordinated              auction        of  exploration           license       blocks       by   different       State      Government                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
         have      resulted        in  lack    of   response.                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
       • To    give    the    prospective           bidders        a   bigger      picture       enabling         them      to  bid    for   the    block      which       best    suit                
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
         their    need      and     specialization.                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
       • Further,       as    the    Central       Government              conducts          auction       of   mining       leases       in   respect       of   Critical      and                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
         Strategic        Minerals

         scientific       and     sustainable           development             and     exploitation           of  the    mineral        resources           decided         that                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
         hence       forth    auction        of  EL     will   be   conducted            by   Central       Government.
