         4  Exploration Details                                                   
         i. Regional Geology         Regionally, rocks of CGGC are present while the mobile belt
                                     of NSMB lies adjacent to the eastern margin of the area.
                                     However, its disposition in the block is roughly North East -
                                     South West represented by Tamar - Porapahar Shear Zone or
                                     South Purulia Shear Zone (northern part of NSMB) which is
                                     known for copper-uranium-lead-apatite mineralization. The
                                     CGGC is an east-west trending sub-arcuate belt. The Tamar -
                                     Porapahar - Khatra fault (TPKF Zone), marks its southern
                                     boundary. The western side of CGGC is limited by the NW
                                     extension of Mahanadi Central India Gondwana Basin.
         ii. Geochemical Investigation Based on NGCM analysis of stream sediment samples, the
                                     maximum value of Cu is 92 ppm, Co is 50 ppm, Zn is 122
                                     ppm, Pb is 47 ppm, Ni is 223 ppm and Cr is 478 ppm. The
                                     highest value of total REE in stream sediment sample is 895
                                     ppm in and around Bartoli village, while many of the
                                     composite samples also give a total REE value of more than
                                     500 ppm.
