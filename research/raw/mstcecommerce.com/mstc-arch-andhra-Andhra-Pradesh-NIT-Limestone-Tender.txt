          Terms and conditions, timelines etc. for participating in the e-auction are provided in the tender
          document. The model tender document and mineral block summaries are available free of cost and can
          be downloaded from the website of MSTC Ltd. (www.mstcecommerce.com) and Department of Mines
          and Geology (‘Department’)(www.mines.ap.gov.in) from 18 July 2017, for the purpose of information
          only. Timelines, notifications, updates and other details are available on the website of MSTC Ltd.
          Interested and eligible participants can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the tender
          document on payment of non-refundable fee of Rs. 1.5 Lakhs plus applicable GST (18%) from the website
          of MSTC Ltd. Bidder shall be able to make payment of this fee through NEFT/RTGS/Netbanking (through
          the payment gateway of e-auction platform). After purchase of the tender document, eligible bidders can
          register themselves on MSTC’s website. On successful registration, bidders will obtain Login ID and
                                                                                    
          Password necessary for participation in e-auction process.                
          Last day for online purchase of tender document is 1500 Hrs IST on 26 August 2017 and the last date for
          submission of the bid is 1500 Hrs IST on 31 August 2017. Any further communication, amendments etc.
          will be published on websites of MSTC and the Department.
