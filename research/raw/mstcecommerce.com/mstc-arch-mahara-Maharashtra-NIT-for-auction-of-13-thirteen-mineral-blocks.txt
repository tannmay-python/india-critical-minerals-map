                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Iron ore, Bauxite and
                                                                                  
          Manganese Ore Minerals and for grant of Composite Licence for Copper Mineral”
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals
          (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction)
                                                                                  
          Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, the Government of Maharashtra has identified:
          a)  9 (nine) mineral blocks [6 (six) blocks of Bauxite, 3 (three) blocks of Limestone, and 1
                                                                                  
              (one) block each of Iron Ore and Manganese Ore] for electronic auction, and hereby
                                                                                  
              invites bids for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease.              
          b)  2 (two) mineral blocks of Copper ore for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids for
                                                                                  
              the purpose of grant of Composite Licence.

          The Government of Maharashtra reserves the Gojoli-Somanpalli Limestone mineral block for
          Specified End Use of production/manufacturing of cement.                
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited
                                                                                  
          both in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in
                                                                                  
          the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications,
          updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in
                                                                                  
          electronic form only and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC Limited:
                                                                                  
          (http://mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above website. On successful
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in
                                                                                  
          the e-auction process. Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summary are available
                                                                                  
          free of cost on the above website.                                      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In the event a Bidder has submitted a bid for a particular mineral block during the fourth
          phase of auctions (Notices Inviting Tender dated January 19, 2018), and the said auction(s)
                                                                                  
          was annulled by the State Government, such Bidder can avail the afore-mentioned tender
                                                                                  
          document of such mineral block without paying the above mentioned tender fee.
