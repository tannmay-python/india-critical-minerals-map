        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Features                              Details                           
          Mineralised Zones Cumulative strike length 1400m of graphite bearing mineralized zone
                          has been confirmed having a variable thickness ranging between 3m -
                                                                                  
                          18m with partings of garnet muscovite/biotite schist in between.
          Trend           The general trend of graphite bearing zone is NE-SW having moderate
          (Dip and Strike) dip of 45°- 50° towards Southeast.                     
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, Vanadium
                          and Graphite mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To
                          infer the details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is
                          required. Hence block area is recommended for grant of Composite
                                                                                  
                          Licence.                                                
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station is at Naharlagun which is approx. 150km
                          from the block.                                         
          Road            The block area is approximately 174km from the capital city Itanagar
                          and situated on Itanagar-Seppa National Highway (NH-13). The area
                                                                                  
                          can also be approached from Tejpur, Assam via seppa.    
          Airport         For air communication, there is a helipad at Lumdung village which is
                          approximately 17 km from the block area.                
        7 Hydrography                                                             
          Local     Surface Papu River is the major stream flowing adjacent to the mapped block
          Drainage Pattern, exhibits sinuous courses. The valley part is covered by alluvium.
          Rivers, etc     Tributaries of Papu River shows dendritic drainage pattern.
                                                                                  
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The annual rainfall in the region ranges between 200-400cm.
          Temperature     The temperature ranges between 20° to 30°C during summer (April to
                          October) and 01° to 15°C during winter (November to March).
