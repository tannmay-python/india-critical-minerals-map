                                 Notice Inviting Tender                     
                   "Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone mineral"
         In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation)
         Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 as amended from time to time notified
         thereunder, the Government of Uttar Pradesh has identified 01 Major Mineral Block for Mining Lease as
         under for auction and hereby invites tenders for:                  
           a) 1 (one)m ineral block of Limestone for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease
         Accordingly, technical bids are invited both in digital and physical format and financial bids are invited in digital
         format only from eligible bidders.                                 
         Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the auction are provided in the Tender Document.
         Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction
         process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only and can be downloaded from the website
         of MSTC Limited:(https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp)
         Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above website. On successful registration, eligible
         bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction process. Model Tender
         Document and Mineral Block Summary are available free of cost on the website of MSTC Limited.
         The date for commencement of sale of Tender Document is May 18,2026. The last date for purchase of Tender
         Document after payment of a tender fee on website of e- auction platform provider is July 06, 2026 on or
         before 17:00 hours (Indian Standard Time) and the last date for submission of the bid is July 13, 2026 on or
         before 17:00 hours (Indian Standard Time).
