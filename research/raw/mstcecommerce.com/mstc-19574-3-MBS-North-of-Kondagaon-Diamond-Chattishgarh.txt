        4   Exploration Details                                                   
        i.  Regional Geology              The area represents a transitional zone between
                                      basement crystalline and younger sediments. The eastern
                                                                                  
                                      and southern parts feature a granulite-gneissic complex
                                      intruded by dyke swarms, while the western part has
                                                                                  
                                      younger Indravati Group sandstone outcrops with laterite
                                      capping in some places. The Archaean Bengpal Group,
                                                                                  
                                      part of the Bastar Craton, includes meta-anorthosite, talc-
                                      tremolite schist, and BIF. The Kondagaon block, located in
                                      the northeast of toposheet 65E/10, comprises rocks from
                                                                                  
                                      the Sukma Group, Bengpal Gneiss Group, Bengpal Group,
                                      and Dongargarh Granite Formation. High-grade
                                                                                  
                                      metamorphic rocks like cordierite-sillimanite quartzite,
                                      pyroxene granulite, and charnockite occur in the
                                                                                  
                                      southeastern part. Basic and ultrabasic dykes, quartz veins,
                                      mylonite, epidote veins, and pegmatites are associated with
                                                                                  
                                      the Dongargarh Granite Formation. Structurally, the area
                                      has undergone multiple deformation phases, with NE-SW
                                                                                  
                                      and NW-SE fractures promoting silicification and dolerite
                                      intrusion.                                  
                                                                                  
        ii. Geochemical Investigation No NGCM  work has been carried out. However,
                                     during a study in Indravati basinal area, a garnet having
                                                                                  
                                     high MgO up to 12% was recovered which is falling
                                     under G5 category. In petrochemical analysis, one
                                                                                  
                                     sample has high MgO (13.23%) owing to the presence
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     of rich mafic minerals like pyroxene.        
                                                                                  
        5   Quantity of Minerals (Grade-wise)                                     
        i.  Mineral Commodity         Diamond                                     
        ii  Exploration Potential of Block The Late Archaean- Early Proterozoic Bastar
                                                                                  
                                      cratonic block is a gniessic complex which is overlain
                                      by schists, graywacke and metavolcanics of the
                                                                                  
                                      Sonakhan Group. Gneisses and greenstone     
                                                                                  
                                      assemblage of the Sonakhan Group are intruded by
                                      younger granitoids. The Chhattisgarh Supergroup,
                                                                                  
                                      overlies the crystalline, comprises conglomerate,
                                      sandstone, limestone and shale. The crystallines in the
                                                                                  
                                      Bastar craton are marked by two distinct structural
                                                                                  
                                      trends, NW- SE to NNW-SSE, and E-W. The cratonal
                                      area is characterized by NE-SW trending fractures
                                                                                  
                                      extending for long distances and E-W trending
                                      fractures occupied by extensive dolerite intrusions and
                                                                                  
                                      silicifications (quartz veins). The Eastern Ghat Mobile
                                                                                  
                                      Belt (EGMB) setup exhibits mainly N-S and E-W
                                      trends. The cover sediments are sub-horizontally
                                                                                  
                                      disposed and show N-S and E-W trending block fault
                                      system.                                     
                                                                                  
                                         As presence of suspected kimberlitic indicator
                                                                                  
                                      minerals was reported By Mishra, et.al (1995-96) in
                                      Indravati basinal area, the area north and northwest of
                                                                                  
                                      that basin was taken up for study by GSI during the
                                      FSP 2002-2003 and 2003-2004. During this study a
                                                                                  
                                      garnet having high MgO up to 12% was recovered
                                      which is falling under G5 category. In petrochemical
                                                                                  
                                      analysis, one sample has high MgO (13.23%) owing
                                                                                  
                                      to the presence of rich mafic minerals like pyroxene.
                                         The prominent four sets of lineaments present
                                                                                  
                                      are –i) NW-SE ii) ENE-WSW iii) NNW-SSE iv)  
                                      NNE-SSW. The higher frequency of occurrences of
                                                                                  
                                      NW-SE and NE- SW to ENE – WSW trending      
                                                                                  
                                      lineaments suggest that the study area may be
                                      proximal to Bastar Craton and Eastern Ghat Mobile
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                      Belt (EGMB) weak zone, hence, favorable for 
                                                                                  
                                      kimberlite emplacements. This is further evidenced by
                                      the proximity of the Indravati basinal area and as
                                                                                  
                                      deciphered from suspected kimberlite indicator
                                      minerals by previous studies (Prasun Ghosh; et al,
                                                                                  
                                      2002-2004). As per the studies, composition chromite
                                      grains indicated a depth of formation which falls in
                                                                                  
                                      the diamond stability field from back scattered image
                                                                                  
                                      studies. This is indicative of the presence of
                                      Kimberlite rock in the vicinity. The presence of dyke
                                                                                  
                                      swarm further indicates a favorable permeable zone
                                      for kimberlite intrusion.                   
                                                                                  
                                         On the basis of above information detailed
                                                                                  
                                      stream sediment sampling bed rock sampling and
                                      petrographic studies may be carried out in the
                                                                                  
                                      proposed area for kimberlitic indicators minerals
                                      (KIMs).                                     
                                                                                  
        6   Mineral Zones/Occurrences/                                            
            Structural Details                                                    
        i.  Number of Mineral Zones   The aeromagnetic total intensity anomaly map reveals
                                                                                  
                                     a variation of 391 nT, ranging from 73 nT to 318 nT.
                                                                                  
                                     Presence of two prominent circular anomalies in North
                                     of Kondagaon EL block indicates possible existence of
                                                                                  
                                     Kimberlite pipes in sub-surface.             
                                                                                  
        ii. Trend (Dip & Strike)       The presence of four sets of lineaments as –i) NW-
                                     SE ii) ENE-WSW iii) NNW-SSE iv) NNE-SSW,     
                                                                                  
                                     making it favorable for kimberlite emplacements. This
                                     is further substantiated by the proximity to the Indravati
                                                                                  
                                     basinal area and the identification of suspected
                                     kimberlite indicator minerals in previous studies.
                                                                                  
                                     Cross-cutting lineaments are the primary target zones
                                                                                  
                                     for locating potential kimberlites.
