        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head Lohardaga railway station is the nearest railway station at a distance of
                          about 25 km from the plateau.                           
          Road            Siskari Pat block is located at a distance of 25-40 kms from Kisko block
                          office and Lohardaga town respectively. Lohardaga town is well
                          connected to Ranchi Airport and Ranchi the state capital which is
                          about 110 km from the block.                            
          Airport         Nearest airport is at Ranchi about a distance of 115km towards east
                          from the block area.                                    
                                                                                  
        7 Hydrography                                                             
          Local     Surface The plateau located on the top has availability of water at depth,
          Drainage Pattern, during monsoon rainwater drains down the escarpments, the natural
          Rivers, etc     slope of the plateau acts as the drainage system, slope of the plateau
                          is towards kisko nala which flows in NW-SE in the valley and lies at
                          about 5km from the block.                               
                                                                                  
        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The district receives annual rainfall of 1000 to 1600 mm.
          Temperature     The annual average temperature is 23°C, the highest temperature goes
                          to 36°C in summer and lowest of 10°C in winter.
