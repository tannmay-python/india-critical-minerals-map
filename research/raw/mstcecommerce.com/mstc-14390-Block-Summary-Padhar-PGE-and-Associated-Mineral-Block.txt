                                     Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd carried out RP project
                                     in part of the EL area. In Temra ultramafic-mafic body,a
                                     gossan zones were delineated 1-2m wide with 1-2% visible
                                     sulphidesbyGeomysore Services. Sulphide assemblage is
                                     dominantly pyrrhotite with minor chalcopyrite and pyrite.
                                     Rock-chip sample east of Dhappawas analysed 0.5% Ni,
                                     0.11% Cu, 70ppb Pb and 68 ppb Pd. Channel profiles cut
                                     across the geochemically anomalous rock chip sample
                                     locations. The results show significant Ni- Cu-PGE
                                     anomalous values upto 0.38 Ni, 0.01% Cu and 0.1 g/t
                                     Pt+Pd.Rock-chip samples collected around Bacha have
                                     yielded highly anomalous PGE values with maximum value
                                     of 0.36 g/t and 0.37 g/t Pt+Pd. Near Baretha the PGE values
                                     come from carbonated ultramafics and gabbro, the later
                                     showing disseminations of sulphides. Green chlorite
                                     alteration is common. Channels cut along 3 profiles in the
                                     Bacha-Baretha area have analysed significant values for Ni-
                                     Cu-PGE. Profile 1 shows a first 4m section of the 12 m
                                     anomaly zone with samples analysing 0.14-0.46g/t Pt+Pd
                                     (Av.0.28 g/t Pt+Pd). This corroborates with the values
                                     obtained during rock-chip sampling, the zone yielded values
                                     of 0.28 to 0.9 g/t Pt+Pd. A 7 m anomalous zone was
                                     encountered where Pt ranged between 13-26 ppb, Pd ranged
                                     between 53-148 ppb.

                6  ACCESSIBILLITY                                                   
                   FEATURES          DETAILS                                        
                   NEAREST RAIL HEAD Betul (approximately 20 kmsouth from the block)
                   ROAD              The Betul city of Betul district of Madhya Pradesh is
                                     located 190 km south of State Capital Bhopal. It is well
                                     connected by state highway. The State highway no. 23
                                     (Bhopal - Nagpur road) passes through Betul town. The
                                     Betul town lies on main Nagpur-Delhi broad gauge line
                                     of Central Railway. The present study area can be
                                     approached from Betul by road. The EL area is located
                                     17 km north of Betul (tehsil) city of Betul District,
                                     Madhya Pradesh which is itself the district headquarters
                                     of Betul.                                      
                                                                                    
                   AIRPORT           The nearest airport is Raja Bhoj airport, Bhopal, which
                                     is 170 km north from Padhar.                   
                7  HYDROGRAPHY                                                      
                   LOCAL SURFACE     Dendriticdrainage.                             
                   DRAINAGE PATTERN                                                 
                   (CHANNELS)                                                       
                   RIVERS/ STREAMS   The drainage in the area forms a part of regime of the
                                     Machana River system. The area is drained by several
                                     major (Nimpani) and minor streams and several  
                                     ephemeral streamlets which are lower order tributaries
                                     forming sub-dendritic pattern and ultimately flowing
                                     into the Machana River, in NE direction in the central
                                     part of the EL area.

                   MORPHOLOGY OF THE The study area has exhibits highly rugged      
                   AREA              terrain/topography dotted with hillocks and ridges
                                     around west of Kevlajhir, north of Bacha and Temra
                                     villages. Isolated hillocks can be seen all around in
                                     Temra, Khokra, Khadara and Bacha areas. Quartz reefs,
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    

                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                     granitoids and mafic-ultramafic rocks exhibit highly
                                     elevated topography in the mapped area. The highest
                                     peak in the study is at west of Kevlajhir village having
                                     height of 636m and the elevation (RL) difference varies
                                     from 892m to 420m. The plain area is occupied by
                                     weathered mafic and ultramafic bodies and a patch of
                                     Gondwana sediments and is used for cultivation.
