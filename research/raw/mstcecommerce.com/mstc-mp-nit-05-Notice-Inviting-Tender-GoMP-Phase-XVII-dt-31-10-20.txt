                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
            “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Basemetal (Zinc) and Limestone
            mineral and for grant of Composite Licence for Copper and associated minerals
                                and Limestone mineral”                            
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
           In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B and Section 11 of the Mines and
           Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral
           (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder, as amended from time to time, the
           Government of Madhya Pradesh has identified:                           
             a. 12 mineral blocks (11 blocks of Limestone mineral and 01 block of Basemetal
               (Zinc)) for Electronic Auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant
                                                                                  
               of Mining Lease.                                                   
             b. 02 mineral blocks (01 block of Copper and Associated Mineral and 01 block of
               Limestone mineral) for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the
               purposes of grant of Composite Licence.                            
           Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited
           both in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.             
           Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms, and conditions etc. for participating in the
           electronic auction are provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents
           along with timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process for
           the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only and can be downloaded from the
           e-auction portal of MSTC Limited [Mineral Block Auction (mstcecommerce.com)].
                                                                                  
           Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above e-auction portal. On
           successful registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for
           participation in the e-auction process. Interested and eligible bidders can participate in
           the bidding only after online purchase of the Tender Document on payment of the
           Tender fee from the above e-auction portal. The Tender Fee shall be waived for bidders
           who have previously purchased the Tender Document for any specific block in prior
           auction attempts. Any revision, clarifications, addendum, corrigendum, time extensions,
           etc. to the tender document will be hosted on the above e-auction portal only and no
           separate notifications shall be issued in the press.                   
           Geological Reports, Part IVA, Plates, Model Tender Document, Mineral Block
           Summaries, and other technical documents are available free of cost on the e-auction
           portal of MSTC Limited website [Mineral Block Auction (mstcecommerce.com)] and
           Directorate of geology and mining.
