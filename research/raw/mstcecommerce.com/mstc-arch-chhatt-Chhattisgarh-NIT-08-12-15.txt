                                  Notice Inviting Tender                            
                                                                                    
           “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone Mineral Blocks & Composite
                    Licence for Gold Mineral Block in the State of Chhattisgarh”    
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section [10B/11] of the Mines and Minerals (Development
          and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified
          thereunder, Mineral Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh has identified the following
          limestone blocks for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease for specified end use of clinker/ cement
                                                                                    
          and gold block for the purpose of grant of Composite Licence, through electronic auction and hereby
          invites bids in digital format only from eligible bidders.                
          #   Name of the Block          District           Type of  Lease/         
                                                            Licence                 
          1   Karhi Chandi Limestone Block Balodabazar - Bhatapara Mining Lease     
          2   Kesla Limestone Block      Raipur                 Mining Lease        
          3   Mangsa-Pauni-Khauna Limestone Raipur              Mining Lease        
              Block                                                                 
          4   Mohra East Limestone Block Balodabazar - Bhatapara Mining Lease       
          5   Baghmara Gold Block        Balodabazar - Bhatapara Composite Licence  
          Terms and conditions, deadlines etc. for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the
                                                                                    
          Tender Document. The Model Tender Document for the mineral blocks and mineral block summary
          are available free of cost in electronic form and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC Ltd.
          (http://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp) and websites of Mineral Resources
          Department, Government of Chhattisgarh (www.chhattisgarhmines.gov.in) for the purpose of
          information only. Timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process are
          available on the website of MSTC Ltd.                                     
          Those interested and eligible for bidding can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of
          the Tender Document on payment of the non-refundable tender fee of INR 2,50,000 (Indian Rupees
                                                                                    
          Two lakhs Fifty thousand) + 14.5% Service Tax for each limestone blocks and INR 50,000 (Indian
          Rupees Fifty thousand) + 14.5% Service Tax for gold block, from the website of e-auction platform
          provider MSTC Ltd; Bidder shall be able to make payment of the Tender Document fee through
          RTGS/ net banking (through the payment gateway of e-auction platform). After registration at MSTC
          website, interested & eligible bidders can purchase the Tender Document. On successful
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain Login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-
          auction process.                                                          
          Last date for online purchase of Tender Document is 21st day of January, 2016 and the last date for
                                                                                    
          submission of the bid is 15:00 Hours IST on 22nd day of January, 2016. Any further communications,
          amendments etc. shall be available on the above website of MSTC and the websites of Mineral
          Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh.
