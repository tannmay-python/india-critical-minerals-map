           Highlights   of The specimen is predominantly composed of quartz, occurring as fine-
           Petrological studies to medium-grained, sub-rounded grains showing good sorting, tight
                           packing, and compact grain contacts. Glauconite is present as fine- to
                           medium-grained sub-rounded pellets and patches, occurring in
                                                                                  
                           disseminated form. Ferruginous matter and opaque minerals occur as
                           very fine aggregates and intergranular fillings, with minor anhedral
                           specks. Lithic fragments, mainly quartzitic, are present in minor
                           amounts. Tourmaline occurs as accessory grains, and calcite is present
                           as very fine interstitial fillings in trace amounts. Based on its
                           mineralogical composition and textural characteristics, the rock is
                           classified as a glauconite-bearing to glauconite-rich quartz arenite.
