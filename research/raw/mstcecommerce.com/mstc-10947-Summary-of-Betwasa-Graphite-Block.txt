                 Highlights of the The graphite mineralisation in the area is mainly
                 Petrographical/   associated with carbon phyllite belonging to Kalinjara
                 Petrochemical Studies Formation of Meghnagar Group of Aravalli Supergroup,
                                   which often grades into graphite schist at places. The
                                   mineralisation mostly occurs along the foliation planes as
                                   thin layers, small patches, pockets and lumps within the
                                   carbon phyllite/graphite schist/carbonaceous marble. The
                                   graphite mostly occurs as coarse to small flakes 
                                   (microcrystalline variety) which is intermittently mixed
                                   with mica flakes and grey powdery material. It is dark
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    

                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                   grey to steel grey in colour, soils hand and greasy in
                                   nature. The presence of graphite in the graphite bearing
                                   mineralization zones has been confirmed by study of thin
                                   polished sections by the Raman spectroscopy.     
              6  ACCESSIBILLITY                                                     
                 FEATURES          DETAILS                                          
                 NEAREST RAIL HEAD Meghnagar (approximately 60kms from the block)   
                 ROAD              Well connected by a 3 km long semi-metalled road which
                                   is well connected by Jobat-Bagh Road. Jobat is located
                                   approximately 04 kms from the Betwasa block. Jobat
                                   town is the main locality near to the block. Jobat lies on
                                   Alirajpur-Indore Road and is connected to Jhabua by a 48
                                   km long metalled road.                           
                 AIRPORT           Indore (approximately 180 kms from the block)
