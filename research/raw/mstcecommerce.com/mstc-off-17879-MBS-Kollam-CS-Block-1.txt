        6   Offshore Geomorphology The blocks fall in the Inner and Mid-Shelf Domain of
                                 Continental shelf off Kerala: Unconsolidated sediment with
                                 varying proportions of sand, silt and clay. Distance from the
                                 coast is 33km                                    
            Hinterland geology   The hinterland rocks include mainly charnockites and
                                 khondalites followed by gneisses and migmatites which are
                                 intruded by dolerite and gabbro dykes and quartzo-feldspathic
                                 veins.
