            Highlights of Geochemical study During large scale mapping, 10 nos. of bed rock samples
                                         were collected in the present area and analyzed for
                                         basemetal, As, Ag, Bi, Mo and Au. The chemical results
                                         show As values ranges from 80 ppm to 985 ppm with Bi

                                         BRS from this area is 0.25% and 0.53% respectively.
                                         Apart from that two petrochemical samples were also
                                         collected.                                 
            Highlights of Ground geophysical survey The Bouguer gravity map indicates moderate gravity
                                         anomaly which be occurring due to structural features
                                         such as break and shifts, inferred fault, contacts, and E-
                                         W shearing.

                                         one trench was excavated (TBG-T3; 10 cu.m).

           TREND (DIP AND STRIKE)        The area has undergone three phases of deformation.
                                         The D1 phase of deformation has resulted in S1
                                         schistosity which is parallel to the S0 bedding planes. F1
                                         folds which resulted from D1 deformation are
                                         manifested in the form of tight isoclinal folds. The D2
                                         stage of deformation has given rise to S2 schistosity
                                         which is more or less co-axial to S1 schistosity and
                                         defines regional strike of the area which is ENE-WSW
                                         to E-W.                                    
                                         The last phase of deformation in Mahakoshal is D3
                                         deformation which has resulted in open broad warps.

          7 HYDROGRAPHY                                                             
            LOCAL SURFACE DRAINAGE       Drainage pattern of the area is dendritic in nature and is
            PATTERN (CHANNELS)           lithologically controlled though at places phyllites have
                                         a sub-parallel to trellis type of drainage pattern which
                                         has structural control.                    
                                                                                    
            RIVERS/ STREAMS              The area is drained by easterly flowing Gotan nadi, a
                                         tributary of Rihand river which is again a tributary of
                                         Son River. South-easterly flowing Barda nala is an
                                         important tributary of Gotan nadi and exposes chloritic
                                         phyllite unit. Other 1st and 2nd order stream in the area
                                         join these two nalas.

            MORPHOLOGY OF THE AREA       Physiographically, the area is represented by hilly and
                                         rugged terrain with a series of E-W trending ridges. The
                                         northern and south-western part of the area is covered
                                                                                    
                                         with high hills with mix dense forest whereas low lying
                                         areas in the central part is under cultivation. The
                                         resistant lithologies of quartzite and BIF forms the hills
                                         and support dense vegetation whereas variegated
                                         phyllite in the central part supports cultivation.
