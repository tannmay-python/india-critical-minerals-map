                                 Notice Inviting Tender                             
            “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Bauxite, Copper, Iron ore, Limestone,
                             Manganese and Tungsten Minerals”                       
                                                                                    
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development
                                                                                    
          and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified
          thereunder, the Government of Maharashtra has identified 8 (eight) minerals blocks, 2 (two)
          blocks each of Bauxite and Tungsten mineral and 1 (one) block each of Copper, Iron Ore,
          Limestone and Manganese mineral, for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders for the
                                                                                    
          purposes of grant of Mining Lease.                                        
                                                                                    
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both
          in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.                     
                                                                                    
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the
                                                                                    
          Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and
          other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only
          and   can  be   downloaded from  the   website of   MSTC   Limited        
          (http://mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above website. On successful
          registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the
          e-auction process. Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summary are available free of
          cost on the website of MSTC Limited (http://mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).
          Last date for purchase of Tender Document, after payment of a tender fee of INR 5,00,000
                                                                                    
          [Rupees Five Lakh] per mineral block, on website of e-auction platform provider is 25th July
          2016 and the last date for submission of the bid is 29th July 2016 [on or before 17:00 hours
          (IST)].                                                                   
                                                                                    
          Note: It is clarified that the Bidders who have participated in the earlier phase of auction (NIT
                                                                                    
          Date: 20th November 2015) held for Nandgaon-Ekodi – Limestone block, Degve-Banda – Iron
          Ore block and Khobna – Tungsten block (i.e. the Bidders who have submitted their technical
          bids and bid security pursuant to the earlier phase of auction held for the above mentioned blocks
          and proposed to participate in 2nd phase of the said auction), shall not be required to pay any fee
          in respect of purchase of the Tender Document for the respective blocks.
