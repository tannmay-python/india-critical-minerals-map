                               Notice for Pre-Bid Conference                        
               This is with reference to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Mohra
                                                                                    
          (Block A) and Parsabhadar Limestone Mineral Blocks in the State of Chhattisgarh” dated
          October 06, 2018, issued by the Government of Chhattisgarh.               
                                                                                    
               A Pre-Bid Conference for registered bidders for the limestone blocks would be conducted
          on December 22, 2018 at 11:00 hours at the following address:             
                                                                                    
          Meeting Hall number - 2,                                                  
          Directorate of Geology & Mining                                           
          Indravati Bhavan, Ancillary Block,                                        
          Third Floor, Atal Nagar,                                                  
          Chhattisgarh - 492002                                                     
                                                                                    
               A maximum of three representatives of each Bidder shall be allowed to participate on
                                                                                    
          production of (i) duly issued authorised letter from the Bidder; (ii) identity documents and (iii) proof
          of purchase of bid document. Only those bidders who have purchased the Tender Document shall
                                                                                    
          be allowed to participate in the pre-bid conferences. During the course of pre-bid conference(s),
          the Bidders may seek additional clarifications and make suggestions for consideration of the State
                                                                                    
          Government.                                                               
               The key agenda of the Pre-Bid Conference shall include:              
                 1. Address by the Director, Directorate of Geology and Mining, Government of
                                                                                    
                    Chhattisgarh                                                    
                 2. Presentation on the limestone blocks to be auctioned            
                                                                                    
                 3. Presentation on the auction process.                            
                 4. Demo of e-auction platform by MSTC (Auction Platform Provider)  
                                                                                    
                 5. Open house session to address queries of potential bidders.     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
               In case of any queries/clarification the bidders are free to reach out to Mr. Anurag Diwan,
          Joint Director, 9425532588 or email their queries at dgm.cg@nic.in.
