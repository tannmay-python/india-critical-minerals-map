          Geochemical     The geochemical exploration in Sito Sikhe block was carried out by
          Exploration     collection of Bedrock samples through channeling, Pitting and
                          Trenching. The bed rock sample collected from carbonaceous phyllite
                          yielded vanadium value ranging from 242 ppm to 18500 ppm. Fixed
                          Carbon value recorded from carbonaceous phyllite in bed rock sample
                          ranges from 2.33% to 33.08%. The trench sample collected from
                          carbonaceous phyllite yielded Vanadium value ranging from 26 ppm to
                          2314 ppm. Wt. avg. of V concentration in trench samples ranges from
                          691.8 ppm to 1692.11 ppm. Fixed Carbon value recorded from
                          carbonaceous phyllite in trench sample ranges from 0.05% to 19.76%.
                          Wt. Avg. FC value in trench samples ranges from 10.75% to 14.77%.

                          oxide, Fe-Ti hydroxide, and REE and V bearing epidote. Laser Raman
                          spectroscopy studies identify the flaky graphite and vein type graphite
                          are mineralized in the carbonaceous phyllite band of Sito-Sikhe area.

        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Naharlagun is the nearest railway station.            
          Road            The study area can be reached from Assam plains and Itanagar by an
                          all-weather metalled road. Hapoli (Ziro), headquarter of Lower
                          Subansiri District is at a distance of about 64km from Sito.
          Airport         For air communication, there is a helipad at Naharlagun and airport at
                          Hollongi, Papum Pare, Arunachal Pradesh.
