                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone and Bauxite minerals and for grant
          of Composite Licence for Bauxite, Copper, Base Metal, and Manganese Ore minerals”
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development and
                                                                                  
          Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified thereunder,
          the Government of Maharashtra has identified:                           
          a)  2 (two) mineral blocks [1 (one) block of Limestone and 1 (one) block of Bauxite for electronic
                                                                                  
              auction, and hereby invites bids for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease.
          b)  4 (Four) mineral blocks [1 (one) block of Bauxite, 1 (one) block of Copper, 1 (one) block of
                                                                                  
              Manganese Ore and 1 (one) block of Base Metal for electronic auction, and hereby invites bids
              for the purpose of grant of Composite Licence.                      
                                                                                  
          The list of mineral blocks is as under:                                 
           S. No.        Name of Block           Mineral     Concession Type      
             1.  Pardi (Kinwat) Limestone Block Limestone    Mining Lease         
                                                                                  
             2.  Kudopi Bauxite Block            Bauxite     Mining Lease         
             3.  Bamhani Basemetal Block        Basemetal   Composite Licence     
             4.  Minzhari Copper Block           Copper     Composite Licence     
                                                                                  
             5.  Paliye Bauxite Block            Bauxite    Composite Licence     
             6.  Savali Manganese Block       Manganese Ore Composite Licence     
          Note: All the abovementioned blocks are under “second” attempt          
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          The price of Tender Document for each mineral block is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) inclusive of
          GST.                                                                    
                                                                                  
          Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in
          digital and physical format from eligible bidders.                      
                                                                                  
          Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender
          Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications, updates and other details
                                                                                  
          for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic form only and can be
          downloaded from the website of MSTC Limited:                            
          (http://mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp).

          Note:                                                                   
                                                                                  
          Bidders may note that for composite licences the Mineral (Auction) Amendment Rules, 2022 have
          dispensed with the requirement of indicating the division of the land into forest land, land owned by the
                                                                                  
          State Government, and land not owned by the State Government and the requirement of demarcating
          the area using total station and differential global positioning system and has provided the flexibility of
                                                                                  
          demarcating the concession area using global positioning system.        
          However, the requisite demarcation in respect of certain blocks, as and where available, may be
                                                                                  
          provided as part of the information memorandum.
