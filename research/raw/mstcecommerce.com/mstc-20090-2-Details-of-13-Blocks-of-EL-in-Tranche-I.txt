                                             
                                                                Exploration Licence Blocks By                                       
                                                                  Geological Survey  of India                                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                        •  The  exploration license means   a license granted for undertaking  reconnaissance  operation  or        
                                                                                                                                    
                           prospecting  operations or both.                                                                         
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                        •  The  exploration license (EL) blocks are carved out based  on available baseline geoscience  data        
                                                                                                                                    
                           as well as the information gathered  from  previously explored area adjacent  to the EL block.           
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                        •  The  reconnaissance  operations  means   any operation  undertaking  for preliminary prospecting         
                                                                                                                                    
                           of a  mineral  through   regional, aerial, geophysical  or geochemical   surveys  and  geological        
                                                                                                                                    
                           mapping,  input pitting, trenching, drilling and subsurface excavations.                                 
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                        •  There  are 13 Exploration Licence blocks under  Tranche-1  encompassing   10 states for 8 mineral        
                                                                                                                                    
                           commodities.
