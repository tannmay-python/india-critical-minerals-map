          Minerals        REE and Yttrium                                         
         Resources (332) of REE:                                                  
            • 11.48 million tonnes (11479638 tonnes) at 0.25% REE cut-off with average grade of 0.48% of

              content of REE estimated is 55276 tonnes.                           
                                                                                  
            • 6.35 million tonnes (6348793.1 tonnes) at 0.50% REE cut-off with average grade of 0.63% of
              total Rare Earth Element (tREE) and 0.74% of total Rare Earth Oxide (tREO) content. The
              metal content of REE estimated is 40261 tonnes.                     
                                                                                  
         Resources (332) of Yttrium: 7.18 million tonnes (7175604 tonnes) at 100 ppm cut-off with average
         grade of 142 ppm of Y and 181 ppm of Y2O3. The metal content Yttrium is 1020 tonnes.

        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Chamrajnagar Railway Station is the nearest railway station which is about
                          35 km from Gundlupet Town.                              
                                                                                  
          Road            Block is located nearly 199 km south west of Bangalore city and 61 km
                          South of Mysore city connected by NH 212 and NH-766.    
          Airport         Mandakalli Airport, Mysore is the nearest airport which is about 50 km.
