    NOTE:-                                                                        
                                                                                  
    The entire Thanewasana block put up for auction is divided into three-orebodies namely Northern ore body,
    Intervening ore body and Southern ore body. Thanewasana block was surveyed by DGPS for establishing all the
    corner points of the block (Plate A) andthe surveyed map was superimposed on the cadastral map (Plate B).
    Cadastral map showing the particulars of land is prepared (Plate C). The geological map of Thanewasan block
    indicating Northern, Intervening and Southern ore body along with borehole locations and old pit/quarry details is
    provided in Plate D.                                                          
                                                                                  
    Initially, GSI worked in all the ore bodies during the year 1971 – 1979 and submitted the report titled “Final Report
    on Thanewasana Copper Investigation, District Chandrapur, Maharastra” in the year 1984. The resource of the
    three ore bodies was estimated of 4.42 MT with average grade of 0.81% Cu between 150 mrl to 35 mrl (Level I and
    II).Later on, GSI further carried out the exploration work of Northern and Intervening ore body upto level III ( 35 mrl
    to -40 mrl) and submitted a report titled “Report on Assessment of Copper and Associated Mineralisation in
    Thanewasana Copper Prospect, Chandrapur District, Maharashtra” with additional resource (level III) of 2.21 MT.
    Thus the total resource of Northern and Intervening ore body upto level III is 3.62 MT with average grade of 0.87%
    Cu with cut off of 0.5% Cu after excluding resource of Southern ore body.     
                                                                                  
    MECL carried further exploration work in the Southern ore body and submitted report titled “Geological Report on
    Exploration for Copper Ore in Thanewasana Copper Deposit (South Block), District Chandrapur, Maharashtra”. The
    total resource of Southern ore body was estimated by MECL after taking into consideration of GSI earlier work
    comes to 4.41 MT with average grade of 0.84% Cu at of 0.5% Cu cut off. Total estimated resource of Thanewasana
    block comes to 8.02 MT.
