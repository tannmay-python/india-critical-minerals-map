     G              O                   A              P                                                                            
        ENERAL         UTLINE      OF     UCTION         ROCESS                                                                     
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • It is a 2-stage ascending forward online electronic auction.                                                              
                                                                                                                                    
        • The following are available on the auction portal in the public domain:                                                   
                                                                                                                                    
             o Mineral  Block Summary                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                    
             o Blank Tender  Document                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                    
        • Interested Bidders  can  register themselves  on the  auction portal for participating in the e-auction  of critical      
                                                                                                                                    
          mineral blocks.                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidders need  to pay a non-refundable  tender document   fee per mineral block to obtain:                                 
                                                                                                                                    
             o Block specific tender documents;                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                    
             o Geological report                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidders should  regularly visit the MSTC website for any corrigendum/addendum/updates     issued.                         
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidder to quote  a percentage  (%) equal to or above the Reserve  Price (i.e. the minimum  percentage  of the value       
                                                                                                                                    
          of mineral despatched)  to be a Successful Bidder (“SB”).

     A              P                                                                                                               
        UCTION         ROCESS                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
           Round  – I of e-auction                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                    
        • Submission  of Technical Bids                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Submission  of Initial Price Offer                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                    
                •  Bidder to submit Technical Bid and Initial Price Offer.                                                          
                •  Top 50%  technically qualified bidders (“TQB”) or 5 (whichever is higher) will proceed to Round II.              
                                                                                                                                    
                •  If TQBs are less than 3, auction process will be annulled.                                                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
           Round   – II of e-auction                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                    
        • Opening   of IPO                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Live forward auction                                                                                                      
                •  Highest Initial Offer in Round I will become the floor price for Round II.                                       
                                                                                                                                    
                •  Minimum    increment  in price bids shall be 0.05% during the live auction stage.                                
                                                                                                                                    
                •  At least one Final Price Offer (“FPO”) is needed for successful auction.                                         
                •  Bidder who  submits  highest FPO is declared as Preferred Bidder (“PB”) immediately  on                          
                                                                                                                                    
                   conclusion of auction.

        IDDING       ARAMETER

        • As per Rule 8 (2) of the Auction Rules, the amount payable  by the Successful Bidder will be a multiple of:               
                                                                                                                                    
          o   Value of Mineral Despatched                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
          o   and a percentage  (%) quoted by the Successful Bidder.                                                                
                                                                                                                                    
        • Further, the Value of Mineral Despatched  shall be an amount  equal to the product of:                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
          o   mineral despatched  in a month; and                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
          o   sale price of the mineral (grade-wise and State-wise) as published by Indian Bureau of Mines for such month           
              of despatch.

       ECTION          OF   THE      CT       AXIMUM           REA                                                                  
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        Explanation of the Act                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                    
        • As per Section 6 of the Act, no person shall acquire:                                                                     
                                                                                                                                    
          o    one  or more  prospecting licences covering a total area of more than 25 Sq Kms; or                                  
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
          o    one  or more  mining leases covering a total area of more than 10 Sq Km.
