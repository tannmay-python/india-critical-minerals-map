          3        Mineral Potential                                              
                   Target Mineral     Gold, Copper & Lithium                      
                   Exploration Potential  The block area forms a part of Eastern Dharwar
                   of Block                                                       
                                        Craton and  comprises granitoids (Closepet
                                        granite) with pegmatites and giant quartz reefs
                                        associated with brecciated dykes and greenstone
                                                                                  
                                        sequences pertaining to Raichur-Devdurga Schist
                                        belt.                                     
                                       The pegmatites with spodumene, tourmaline and
                                                                                  
                                        phlogopite are proved potential for lithium in the
                                        area falling closer to the proposed EL block.
                                                                                  
                                       Prospects like Yerejanti, Tintini and Machanur
                                        which are known for Cu and Au mineralisation
                                        through breccia zone are falling closer to the
                                                                                  
                                        proposed Devadurga EL Block. These breccia
                                        zones wherever found associated with basic dykes
                                                                                  
                                        are promising for base-metal mineralisation.
                                        Sympathetic ENE-WSW   trending dykes are  
                                        reported within the EL Block which may be 
                                                                                  
                                        targeted for similar base-metal mineralisation.
                                       Giant quartz reef trending in sympathetic trend of
                                                                                  
                                        major Krishna lineament in the proposed block
                                        can also be attempted for Au and base-metal
                                        mineralisation.

                                       The EL block is also falling in close proximity to
                                        Raichur Kimberlite field, thus requiring attention
                                                                                  
                                        towards search of kimberlites and lamproites.
                                       Considering the rheological contrast as a potential
                                                                                  
                                        conduit for fluid migration, the contact zone along
                                        the greenstone and the granitoids has to be
                                        evaluated for shear controlled polymetals.
                                                                                  
          5        Mineralised Zones   During the RMT-1 (2018-22), two major Mineral
                                        systems namely Archean lode Gold and Breccia
                                                                                  
                                        hosted Cu+Au mineral system were identified.
                                       Reconnaissance   studies  around   the    
                                                                                  
                                        Chikahonakuni area for Gold and associated
                                        minerals have established three NNW-SSE   
                                        trending Mineralized zones.               
                                                                                  
                                       These zones are associated with breccia zones
                                        running sympathetic to ENE-WSW  Krishna   
                                                                                  
                                        lineament with sub-vertical dip.          
                                       Pegmatite potential for lithium and possible rare
                                        metals are reported in various directions. Depth
                                                                                  
                                        persistence of which is not been established.
                                        The alteration zones associated with shears are
                                                                                  
                                        delineated in the NW-SE direction which is
                                        sympathetic to regional trend of greenstone
                                        sequence.
