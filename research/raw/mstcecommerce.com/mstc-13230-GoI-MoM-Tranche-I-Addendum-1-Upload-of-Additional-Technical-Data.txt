                          Addendum 1 – Upload of Additional Technical Data          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          1. This is with reference to “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Mineral Concession of Critical and
            Strategic Minerals” dated November 29, 2023, issued by the Government of India.
                                                                                    
          2. During the Pre-Bid Conference held on December 22, 2023, certain potential bidders
                                                                                    
            requested that access be given to digital copies of the technical data pertaining to the blocks
            put on auction in NIT stated above, in addition to the information already uploaded in the
            paid section of the MSTC portal of the respective critical mineral blocks.
                                                                                    
          3. Accordingly, the additional technical data available is being uploaded on the MSTC portal in
            the paid section of the respective critical mineral blocks.             
                                                                                    
          4. Kindly note that the following terms and conditions pertaining to the additional data being
            uploaded:                                                               
                                                                                    
                The technical data pertaining to the blocks shall only be used for the purpose of
                 analysis of the said blocks for submission of the bids with respect to point 1 stated
                 above.                                                             
                                                                                    
                The same may contain confidential, proprietary or privileged information, and shall
                 accordingly not be disclosed to any third party, except, where required, to the
                 potential bidder’s directors, partners, personnel, consultants, employees, officers,
                 and/ or agents who have a need to know such information and who are contractually
                                                                                    
                 bound by confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations no less restrictive as those set
                 out hereinabove.                                                   
                The technical data uploaded on the MSTC portal is non-transferable. Other than
                                                                                    
                 expressly required for the purpose of participating in the auction, please do not copy,
                 forward, publish, or disclose, or take any action based on, information contained in
                 the subject, or part thereof. In the event this information is used for any purpose other
                 than as aforesaid, Ministry of Mines, Geological Survey of India or Mineral
                                                                                    
                 Exploration and Consultancy Limited may initiate appropriate legal action against the
                 potential bidder(s).
