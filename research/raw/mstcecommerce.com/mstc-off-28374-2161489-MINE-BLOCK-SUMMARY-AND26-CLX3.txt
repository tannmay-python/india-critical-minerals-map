            Geology and Geomorphology of the exploration area                     
        6   Offshore Geomorphology Sewell Rise is a NE-SW trending bathymetric high
                                   located in the back-arc region of the southern Andaman
                                   Sea, occurring between the inner volcanic arc and the East
                                   Basin. The Sewell Rise, together with Alcock Rise,
                                   opened during the back-arc extension of the Andaman
                                   marginal sea, and its surface features trend approximately
                                   parallel to the spreading axis of the basin. The area
                                   comprises a major NE-SW trending seamount with 
                                   associated linear ridges (NE-SW and NW-SE trending),
                                                                                  
                                   basins, occurring within water depths of 616 m to 2297 m.
                                   The seamount and ridges in the central, south-eastern and
                                   south-western parts of the area show rugged, massive
                                   bouldery exposures with encrustations and scattered
                                   nodules, while the valley and basinal portions are
                                   dominated by pelagic and hemi-pelagic sediments.

            Seabed Morphology       The area comprises a major NE-SW trending seamount
                                    with associated linear ridges (NE-SW and NW-SE
                                    trending), basins, occurring within water depths of 616
                                    m to 2297 m. The seamount and ridges in the central,
                                    south-eastern and south-western parts of the area show
                                    rugged, massive bouldery exposures with encrustations
                                    and scattered nodules, while the valley and basinal
                                    portions are dominated by pelagic and hemi-pelagic
                                    sediments. Most of the area is characterized by gentle
                                    slopes, whereas the flanks of seamounts and ridges
                                    bearing polymetallic crusts and nodules exhibit slopes
                                    ranging from 7.5° to 20.7°.                   
                                                                                  
            ROV videography details Seafloor scanning was carried out through videography
                                   during 37 Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) dives, for
                                   visual assessment of the seafloor. Polymetallic crusts, and
                                   nodules were observed over hard substrate and sediment-
                                   covered terraces of the seamounts and ridges between
                                   water depths of about 616 m and 2297 m. Massive rock
                                   exposures with Fe-Mn crusts/coatings were observed on
                                   the rugged seamount tops and flanks, with intervening
                                   areas covered by foraminiferal ooze and silty clay.
