                                Notice for Pre-Bid Conference                     
                                                                                  
           1. With reference to the “Invitation of Bids for Grant of Operating Rights of Offshore Areas” dated
             October 01, 2026, issued by the Government of India through the Administering Authority, Pre-
             Bid Conference will be conducted as follows:                         
                                                                                  
               Date: Wednesday, November 04, 2026                                
               Time: 11:00 hours                                                 
                                                                                  
               Mode: Hybrid (Physical and Online)                                
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
           2. All the representatives of potential bidders who wish to attend in person are advised to submit
             a copy of the authority letter from the potential bidder and produce one government-issued
                                                                                  
             identity document for entry into the Pre-Bid Conference Venue.
