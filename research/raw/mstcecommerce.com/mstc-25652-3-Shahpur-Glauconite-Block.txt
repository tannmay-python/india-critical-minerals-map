        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface Regionally, the Sone river, which flows from west to east-northeast,
          Drainage Pattern, forms the main drainage in the area. Most of the feeder nalas
          Rivers, etc     originating from the plateau are seasonal in nature and form huge
                                                                                  
                          water-falls during rainy season. The general pattern of the drainage
                          system is dendritic.                                    
        8 Climate                                                                 
                                                                                  
          Mean Annual Rainfall The average annual rainfall is 1100 mm.            
          Temperature     The maximum temperature during summer is 50oC           
        9 Topography                                                              
                                                                                  
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 63 P/14                  
          Morphology of the Regionally the elevation of the alluvial plains varies from 90 to 140 m
          area            above the Mean Sea Level (MSL), while the top of the Kaimur plateau
                          ranges in the height from 400 to 500m above the MSL. Towards the
                                                                                  
                          north and north-east, the plateau exhibits an undulating topography
                          where its southern face is very steep (slope angle varies from 30° -
                          80°) involving a sudden drop of 20° to 50°, whereas the northeastern
                                                                                  
                          side of the area looses the height gently.
