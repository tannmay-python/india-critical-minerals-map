         4  Exploration Details                                                   
         i. Regional Geology          The EDC is characterized by late Archaean TTG and calc-
                                      alkaline granites which are interlayered by long and narrow
                                      greenstone belts. These greenstone belts are trending in the
                                      N-S to NNW-SSE direction. These schist belts are
                                      surrounded by different types of granitoids which are
                                      equivalent to Closepet granites. Granitoids occurring
                                      surrounding HMSB which is known as Kavital granite has
                                      shown U-Pb zircon age of 2545±7 Ma. The pink coloured
                                      Yelagatti granites occurring in the northern part of the
                                      HMSB has yielded 207Pb-206Pb ages of 2221±99 Ma.
                                      Emplacement of Kavital granitoid coincides with the age of
                                      gold mineralization in HMSB which indicates syn-
                                      magmatic gold mineralization.               
         ii. Geochemical Investigation Geochemical mapping in the EL block reported 100-
                                      272ppm Cu and 3-12ppb Au from stream sediment
                                      samples.
