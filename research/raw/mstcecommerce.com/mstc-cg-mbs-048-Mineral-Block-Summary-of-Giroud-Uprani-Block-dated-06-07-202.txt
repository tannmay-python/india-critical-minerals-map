           5      Mineralised Zones                                                 
                  Number of mineral zones -                                         
                  Trend (Dip and Strike) Glauconite in the study area is present as an
                                        important mineral in quartz arenite. General strike of
                                        the glauconitic sandstone is E-W to ENE-WSW,
                                        dipping 5 to 7° towards North.              
                  Total Thickness       The glauconitic sandstone extends in ENE-WSW
                                        trend with approximate width varying from 0.35 Km to
                                        a maximum of 4.5 Km. It shows glauconites of
                                        various sizes (0.5mm to 2mm) and shapes     
                                        (spherical, sub-spherical, cubic, mainly elliptical),
                                        color of glauconite grains varies from pale green to
                                        green                                       
           6      Accessibility                                                     
                  Nearest Rail Head     Bagbahra                                    
                  Road                  District headquarter Baloda Bazar is 65 Km away from
                                        the block area                              
                  Airport               Raipur                                      
           7      Hydrography                                                       
                  Local Surface Drainage Pattern The lower order nalas exhibit parallel, dendritic
                  (Channels)            drainage pattern being coarse textured in the east to
                                        medium textured in the west. All the nalas in this area
                                        are seasonal.                               
                  Rivers/Streams        The area is drained by Jonk River and its tributaries
                                        and distributaries. Jonk flows from south-east to north-
                                        west in the western part of the study area  
           8      Climate                                                           
                  Mean Annual Rainfall  1200 to 1500 mm.                            
                  Temperatures (December) 8°C                                       
                  Temperatures (June)   up to 46°C                                  
           9      Topography                                                        
                  Toposheet No.         64 K/10                                     
                  Morphology of the Area The general elevation of the area varies from 362 m to
                                        238 m above MSL. Highest point within the block (362
                                        m above MSL) is in the southern part of the block. It is
                                        part of the Gindola Reserved Forest hill system. The
                                        lowest point within the block (238 m above msl) is in
                                        the Jonk riverbed in the north-western part of the
                                        block.
