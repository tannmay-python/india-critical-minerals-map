        5 MINERALISED                                                               
          ZONES                                                                     
          NUMBER OF        One band of phosphorite dolomite and ferruginous chert.  
          MINERAL ZONES                                                             
          NATURE OF        Block exposes a sequence of thinly laminated dolomite, chert and shale
          MINERALIZATION   intercalations with cherty phosphate bands. Phosphate mineralization in
                           the dolomitic rocks is confined to lenses and is highly irregular irregular,
                                                                                    
                           weak without any distinct control. It is mostly associated with
                           ferruginous chert which might be of epigenetic origin as the phosphate is
                           associated with specularite veins. At places manganese veins are also
                           noticed intruding this interlayered sequence             
          TREND (DIP AND   The general strike of the dolomite here is varying between to N60°E-S60°W
          STRIKE)          with dips 70° due SSE and exhibits tight folding         
          TOTAL THICKNESS  -                                                        
                                                                                    
        6 ACCESSIBILLITY                                                            
          NEAREST RAIL HEAD Indore is the major nearest railheads connecting the area. beside this
                           Khandwa-Ratlam-Ajmer section (meter gauge) of the western - railway
                           passes through the Barwaha tehsil.                       
          ROAD             Sadkhera is situated 10 km North-East of Barwaha and Barwaha is
                           connected with the Indore Khandwa state highway. motorable roads-
                           namely Barwah-Katkut road connects this area. the interior parts of the
                           area can be approached by cart and foot tracks by fair weather roads..
                                                                                    
          AIRPORT          the nearest airport at Indore is 68 km

          DRAINAGE PATTERN                                                          
          (CHANNELS)                                                                
          RIVERS/ STREAMS  Narmada river, which flows from ENE to WSW in extreme southern
                           part of the area of investigation, is the main drainage. it forms an island
                           named Omkarmandhata. the other important river draining the area is the
                           Choral river which originates from the Malwa plateau and flows
                                                                                    
                           southerly to meet the Narmada river. the Odhali nala, a tributary of
                           choral river also flows southerly to meet the choral river near Barwah.
                           Kariakund nala is another important drainage in the area.
        8 CLIMATE          The Block experiences semi-arid to dry climatic conditions with very hot
                           summers from April to June and mild winters from December to
                           February. the temperature rises up to 45°c in summer. during winter
                           season the temperature drops to around 10°c. dust storms and small wind
                           vortices are quite common in summer. rains normally commence from
                                                                                    
                           end of June and continue till September.                 
          MEAN ANNUAL      The average annual rainfall is about 100 cm.
