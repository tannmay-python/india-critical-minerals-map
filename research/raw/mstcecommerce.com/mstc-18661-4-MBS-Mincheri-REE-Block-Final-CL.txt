          Justification  The analytical results of the core samples received have anomalous ΣREE
                                                                                  
                         values ranging from 0.004 % to 6.67%. The distribution of the REE’s
                         indicate that the Mincheri REE prospect is found to be highly enriched in
                         LREE (99.44%) when compared to HREE (0.56%). A cumulative length of
                         24.8metres of ore zone has been defined using the anomalous range. As
                         the mineralization zone in the area has been proved in sub-surface by
                         drilling, the area may be proposed for G2 stage investigation. Hence,
                         block is recommended for Composite Licence.
