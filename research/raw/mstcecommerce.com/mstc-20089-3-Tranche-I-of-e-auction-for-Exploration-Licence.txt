     I                                                                                                                              
       NTRODUCTION                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Mines    and   Minerals     (Development        &  Regulations)     1957    (“MMDR      Act”)   is  enacted     by        
                                                                                                                                    
          Government       of  India  for  development       as  well   as  regulations    of  mines    and   minerals    in        
                                                                                                                                    
          the  country.                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Central    Government       has   legislated    the  Mineral    Auction    Rules,   2015    (“Auction    Rules”)          
                                                                                                                                    
          under    the  Act   to govern    the  process     of grant   of  mineral    concessions      such    as-  Mining          
                                                                                                                                    
          Lease,   Composite       Licence   etc.                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Further,    29  Critical   &  Deep-seated        Minerals     are  notified   in  the  Seventh     Schedule     of        
                                                                                                                                    
          the  MMDR     Amendment        Act,  2023.   The  Act  inter-alia   introduced     provisions    of Exploration           
                                                                                                                                    
          Licence    for undertaking      reconnaissance       operations     or prospecting     operations     or both.            
                                                                                                                                    
        • Central    Government       issued   an  order   dated   21.10.2024,     under   Section    20A   of the  MMDR            
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
          Act,   conferring    the  power     to conduct      e-auction    of  EL  under    Section    10BA    to  Central          
                                                                                                                                    
          Government

     K       F                     E                       L                                                                        
        EY     EATURES        OF      XPLORATION             ICENCE                                                                 
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        Max.  area  of EL block  -1000  sq.km.;   Max.   area limit for a person   in a State  - 5000 sq.km                         
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        EL holder  is entitled to receive   revenue   share  only for the  minerals   listed in the 7th schedule                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        Period  of EL: 5 years;  extendable    by 2 years                                                                           
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        EL holder  to relinquish   75%  area  so  that only  25%  remains    with the  holder  after 3 years                        
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        Deliverable  of EL  Lessee   – G2  level Geological   Report                                                                
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        Upon   completion    of exploration,   Government     (Centre   if, the mineral  is in list of 24 strategic/ critical       
        minerals)   to complete     auction   of the  area  for  ML  within   1  year  from   submission     of Geological          
                                                                                                                                    
        Report  by EL  holder                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
            Any  person   interested  may   suggest   an area  to auction   such  area  for EL.

     G                O                    A               P                                                                        
         ENERAL          UTLINE      OF      UCTION          ROCESS                                                                 
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • It is a 2-stage Reverse   Descending     Online   Electronic  Auction                                                     
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • The  following  are  available  on  auction  portal  in public  domain                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
             • Mineral   Block  Summary                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
             • Blank  Tender   Document                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Interested   Bidder  can   register itself on the  auction   portal for participating   in e-auction   of Critical        
          Mineral   Blocks                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidders   need  to pay  non-refundable     tender   document    fee  per mineral   block  to obtain                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
             • Block  Specific   Tender  Document                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                    
             • Project  Proposal   (Technical   Data)                                                                               
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidders   should   regularly visit the MSTC    website   for any corrigendum/addendum/updates            issued

     B               P                                                                                                              
        IDDING         ARAMETER                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidder   to quote  a Percentage    (%) equal   to or less than  the  Ceiling Price  to be  a Preferred  Bidder            
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Bidders   shall  quote,  for the purpose    of receiving  payment     from  State  Government,     a percentage           
                                                                                                                                    
          share  of the  auction  premium     payable   by the future  lease  holder  of ML.                                        
                                                                                                                                    
        • The  bidder  quoting   the minimum     percentage    shall  be the  Preferred  Bidder.                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Ceiling  price-  40%                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
        • Amount    payable   to EL  holder  by  State  Government/ML      Holder   will only  be in respect   of minerals          
          listed in the  Seventh   Schedule

     A               P                                                                                                              
        UCTION          ROCESS                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
     Round   – I of e-auction                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
     •  Submission   of Technical Bids                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                    
     •  Submission   of IPO                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
           •  Bidder to submit Technical Bid and Initial Price Offer (IPO)                                                          
           •  Bidder to submit IPO equal to or less than Ceiling Price                                                              
                                                                                                                                    
           •  Top 50% ranks or top 5 technically qualified bidders (TQB) whichever is higher will proceed to Round II               
           •  If TQBs are less than 3, auction process will be annulled                                                             
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
     Round   – II of e-auction                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                    
     •  Opening  of IPO                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                    
     •  Live forward auction                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
           •  Lowest IPO in Round I will become the Limiting Price for Round II                                                     
           •  Minimum  decrement  in price bids shall be 0.05% during the live auction stage                                        
                                                                                                                                    
           •  At leastone Final Price Offer (FPO) is needed for successful auction                                                  
           •  Bidder who submits lowest FPO is declared as Preferred Bidder (PB) on conclusion of auction

     Explanation   for the calculation of Net Worth:                                                                                
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
     •   Company:                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                    
          •   Net worth  shall be the sum  of paid-up share  capital and the free reserves                                          
                                                                                                                                    
          •   As on the last day of the financial year ended immediately   preceding  the date  of NIT                              
          •   In case of Bidder using  the Net Worth  of Holding  Company,   the  Shareholding  Pattern  for establishing the       
                                                                                                                                    
              relationship between   the Holding Company    of the bidder shall be of the last day of the financial year            
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
     •   Partnership  Firm:                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                    
          •   Net Worth  shall be as per the partners’capital  account,  and  is equivalent to the sum  of the paid-up share        
                                                                                                                                    
              capital and the free reserves of a company                                                                            
          •   As on the last day of the financial year ended immediately   preceding  the date  of NIT                              
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
     •   Individual:                                                                                                                
          •   Net worth  of Individual may  instruments  like amount  in savings bank  accounts,   free and un-encumbered           
                                                                                                                                    
              FDs, Kisan  Vikas  Patra, NSCs,   Bonds,  Shares  of Listed  Companies,    Listed Mutual   Funds,  Unit Linked        
                                                                                                                                    
              Insurance  Plan, PPF,  Surrender  Value  of Life Insurance   policies, un-encumbered     immovable    property,       
              etc.                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                    
          •   The valuation of unencumbered     immovable   property  shall be based  on the relevant circle rate

        Explanation   and implication   of the Act                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                    
             • Section  6 of the Act lays restriction on holding of Exploration Licence for a particular mineral in a state         
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
             • It disallows participation of persons  who  are holding or may, in the event of becoming   highest bidders  in       
                                                                                                                                    
               the auction, hold  the area in excess of the area limit under Section  6 (1) of the MMDR  Act.                       
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
             • Provided  that if the Central Government   is of the opinion that in the interest of the development   of any        
                                                                                                                                    
               mineral  or industry, it is necessary  so  to do, it may  increase  the aforesaid  area  limits in respect  of       
               prospecting  licence  or mining lease pertaining  to any particular mineral located  in any particular area.         
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
             • The  existing maximum   area  limit for Seventh Schedule  Minerals  is as below:                                     
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                 ➢ Max.  area of EL block -1000  sq.km.;                                                                            
                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                    
                 ➢ Max.  area limit for a person in a State - 5000 sq.km
