             NUMBER OF MINERAL  Mardeora ’C’ Block:                                 
             ZONES                        It extends for 400m along N & and 600m along E-W
                                directions, the eastern part of the block is mainly covered by light
                                brown shale and iron concretionary shale whereas the western side is
                                occupied by the laminated phosphorite. This block bears two types of
                                phosphorite viz. laminated phosphorite and phosphorite with chert
                                nodules. The block consists of three bands of phosphorite.
                                                                                    
                                Band-I: It consists of laminated phosphorite and extends for 320m in
                                NNW-SSE direction from 15 to 35%. The P2O5 grade is little higher
                                towards the southern half.                          
                                Band-II: It lies 25m east of band-I and consists of laminated
                                phosphorite. It extends for 90m in NNW direction with a width of 5m
                                and P2O5 content is 12 to 33%.                      
                                Band-III: It is situated at 120m east of Southern end of band-I and
                                consists of phosphorite with chert nodules. It extends for 95 m in
                                north south direction with a width varying from 10 to 50m. The P2O5
                                                                                    
                                content of the band varies from 15 to 31%.          
                                Mardeora ’D’ Block:                                 
                                          This is the largest block of Mardeora sector having
                                dimension of 860x800m.it consists of all the three types of
                                phosphorite viz. brecciated, laminateu and phosphorite with chert
                                nodules. There are 10 bands of phosphorite in this block out of which
                                band-I to VI lie in the western half of the block and band VII to X lie
                                in the eastern half (between local survey coordinate 2700 and 3100
                                                                                    
                                E).                                                 
                                Band-I: It commences from the southwestern comer of the block and
                                extends in NW direction for 160m with a width varying from 10 to
                                50m. It consists of brecciated phosphorite and the P2O5 content
                                varies from 6 to 25% (plate-9).                     
                                Band-II: It commences from 10m north of northwestern end of band-
                                I and extends for 420m towards NNW direction with a width varying
                                from 10 to 30m. It consists of laminated phosphorite and P2O5
                                                                                    
                                content varies from 10 to 27%. The southern half of this band is
                                comparatively significant than the northern half.   
                                Band-III: It draw its physical continuity from the band-I of Mardeora
                                'C' block and extends for 320 m in NNW direction with a width
                                varying from 15 to 60m. It consists of laminated phosphorite and the
                                P2O5 content varies from 13 to 33%.                 
                                Band-IV: It lies 150m NE of the northern end of band-III and
                                consists of phosphorite with chert nodules. It extends for 30m along
                                E-W with an average width of 10m. The P2O5 content is about 15%.
                                                                                    
                                Band-V: It lies adjacent to band-IV and consists of laminated
                                phosphorite. It extends along NE- SW for 40m with an average width
                                of 15m. The P2O5 content is about 15%.              
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    

                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                Band-VI: It lies 40m north of band-V and has an arcuate pattern with
                                convexity towards south. Its arcuate length is 140m with a width
                                varying from 10 to 40m. It consists of phosphorite with chert nodules
                                and the P2O5 content is 8%.                         
                                Band-VII: It lies in the eastern half of the block and commence from
                                90m northeast of the southern end of band-VI. It extends towards SE
                                for 430m with a width varying from 10 to 30m. It consists of
                                                                                    
                                laminated phosphorite and the P2O5 content varies from 13 to 31%.
                                Band-VIII: It lies 20m east of northern end of band-VII with a
                                dimension of 40x15m. It consists of laminated phosphorite with 29%
                                P2O5 content.                                       
                                Band-IX: It starts from a phosphorite quarry and extends in an
                                arcuate pattern with convexity towards east for 510m with & width
                                varying from 5 to 50m. It is wider towards south and tapers towards
                                north. It consists of phosphorite with chert nodules and the P2O5
                                content varies from 10 to 31%. The P2O5 content of quarry varies
                                                                                    
                                from 15 to 29%.                                     
                                Band-X: This is the easternmost band of the block and starts from a
                                quarry and extends towards NE for 200m with a width of 5 to 30m. It
                                consists of phosphorite with chert nodules and the P2O5 content of
                                this band at and around the quarry is 34%.          
                                Mardeora ’E’ Block:                                 
                                          From Mardeora 'D' to 'A' block the general trend of
                                the deposit is N-S whereas from 'A' block the deposit swerves
                                                                                    
                                towards west where Mardeora 'E' block is situated. It starts from
                                700m west of band-II of Mardeora 'A' block and extends for 800m
                                towards NW with a width varying from 100 to 400m. It consists of 6
                                bands of laminated phosphorite out of which the VIth band is
                                important. Most of the bands lie over the dolomite. 
                                Band-I: It lies 830m west of band-II of Mardeora 'A' Block trending
                                NW-SE for 80m with an average width of 15m. The P2O5 content is
                                15%.                                                
                                                                                    
                                Band-II: It lies 110m south of southern end of band-I and trends NE-
                                SW for 70m with an average width of 10m. The P2O5 content is
                                15%.                                                
                                Band-III: It lies 20m west of band-II and extends towards west for
                                100m with a width varying from 5 to 25m. The P2O5 content is 20%.
                                Band-IV: It lies 20m west of band-III and has an arcuate shape with
                                convexity towards south. The arcuate length of the band is 160m with
                                a width varying from 10 to 20m. The P2O5 content is 15 to 20%.
                                Band-V: It lies 130m west of band-IV and extends in NW-SE
                                                                                    
                                direction for l00 m with an average width of 15m. The P2O5 content
                                is 10 to 15%.
