                 NATURE        OF Phosphorite is confined to the dolomite and dolomitic
                 MINERALIZATION   intercalation within the chert horizons. The part of chert that
                                  has the embedded stromatolitic columns gives high 
                                  phosphatic value, whereas the adjacent massive part of chert
                                  gives very low phosphatic value. In some cases, the colour
                                  of the dolomite acts as a field criterion for delineating the
                                  phosphatic horizons e.g. the buff coloured and ochre yellow
                                  coloured dolomitic bands contain in general the low grade

              6  ACCESSIBILLITY                                                     
                 FEATURES         Physiographically, the area represents the southeastern part of
                                  Aravalli Fold Mountain Belt. The area is characterized by
                                  rugged and rolling topography with ridges, valleys and
                                  pediplain. The area is drained by Anas River, Pat River, and
                                  other ephemeral streams giving rise to dendritic drainage
                                  pattern.                                          
                 NEAREST RAIL HEAD Bhopal-Ratlam-Vadodara-BG Rail line              
                 ROAD             The area is well connected by rail and roads. The area is
                                  approachable by National Highway No. 69 (NH-69, Bhopal-
                                  Indore-Jhabua Road) and by rail (Bhopal-Ratlam-Vadodara
                                  Railway line). The interior parts of the area can be approached
                                  by forest roads.                                  
                 AIRPORT          Indore                                            
              7  HYDROGRAPHY                                                        
                 LOCAL SURFACE    The area is drained by Anas River, Pat River, and other
                 DRAINAGE PATTERN ephemeral streams giving rise to dendritic drainage pattern.
                 (CHANNELS)       The Anas is a large tributary of the Mahi River. It rises from
                                  the south-eastern part of Jhabua district, flowing almost from
                                  south-west to north-east direction. The northern off-shoot of
                                  the Vindhyachal range bifurcates the Anas River at Phurtalao
                                  hill (1770 ft.) into the northern and western branches. Most of
                                  the river course lies in Gujarat. The Pat Nadi is a tributary of
                                  the Anas River. It rises from the south-eastern part of Jhabua
                                  district                                          
                                                                                    
                 RIVERS/ STREAMS  The area is drained by Anas River, Pat River, and other
                                  ephemeral streams
