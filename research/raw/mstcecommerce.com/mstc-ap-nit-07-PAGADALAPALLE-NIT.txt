          3.   Introduction                                                         
                3.1. The Act stipulates grant of Composite Licence through e-auction in respect of minerals vest in
                                                                                    
                     the government other than                                      
                      (i) Minerals notified as minor minerals specified under clause (e) of Section 3 of the act;
                                                                                    
                      (ii) Minerals listed in Part A of First Schedule of the Act; and
                                                                                    
                      (iii) Minerals specified in Part B of First Schedule of the Act having grade equal to or more
                                                                                    
                        than the threshold value as specified and notified under the Atomic Mineral Concession
                        Rules, 2016                                                 
                                                                                    
                3.2. In exercise of powers conferred by the section 13 of the Act, the Central Government has
                     notified the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 and its subsequent amendments, and the Minerals
                     (Evidence of Mineral Contents) Rules, 2015 and subsequent amendments. The Bidders are
                                                                                    
                     encouraged to acquaint themselves with the Act and the rules made thereunder.
                                                                                    
                3.3. This Tender Document is being issued by the State Government, for e-auction for grant of a
                     Composite Licence for the [Name of Mineral Block].             
                                                                                    
                3.4. The e-auction would be conducted in accordance with the Act, the Auction Rules, the Minerals
                     (Evidence of Mineral Content) Rules, 2015, any other order or notification issued by the Central
                     Government pursuant to the Act and this Tender Document.       
                                                                                    
                3.5. The Bidders are expected to conduct due diligence regarding the land to be comprised in the
                     mining lease and also familiarise themselves with all Applicable Laws relating to acquisition of
                                                                                    
                     rights over such land to be comprised in the mining lease, including, without limitation, the Act,
                     the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980, the Panchayats (Extension to the
                     Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996, the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land
                                                                                    
                     Acquisition, Rehabilitation & Resettlement Act 2013, and respective laws of State Government
                     with respect to land reforms, scheduled areas and any other Applicable Laws.
                                                                                    
                3.6. Minor minerals available in the Mineral Block, if required by the successful bidder, shall be dealt
                     in accordance with Andhra Pradesh Minor Minerals Concession Rules 1966 separately and the
                                                                                    
                     said minerals will not be under the purview of this Tender Document.

        6.   Review period and site visit

                 to study the Tender Document, and the bidding process shall commence only after such period.

                 dates mentioned in Clause 11.

                 risk to ascertain for themselves the site conditions, location, communication, climate, availability of

                 Mineral Block. The site visit must be in compliance with Applicable Law and rights of person(s)

                 Schedule II (Format for seeking clarifications regarding the Tender Document).

                 Information: Tender Document for [Insert Name of Mineral Block]”.
                                                                                  
            7.4. Each query should contain complete details of facts, information and Applicable Law relevant to
                                                                                  
                 the query and also the particulars of the person or Company posing the query. The State
                 Government reserves the right to not answer any query, including any query which is incomplete
                 or anonymous.

                 11. However, the State Government reserves the right to not respond to any question or provide any
                 clarification, in its sole discretion, and nothing in this Tender Document shall be taken or read as
                                                                                  
                 compelling or requiring the State Government to respond to any question or to provide any
                 clarification.

                 website, without mentioning the source of queries.

                 11 at a time and place specified by the State Government. Only those Bidders who have purchased

                 bid conference(s), the Bidders may seek additional clarifications and make suggestions for

            13.1. Upfront Payment

                Dispatched quoted as the Final Price Offer on commencement of production of mineral(s) as

                Value of Estimated Resources computed on the basis of the findings pursuant to the prospecting
                operations undertaken pursuant to the Composite Licence.

                proportion to the actual quantity of mineral dispatched against the amount payable in
                                                                                  
                accordance with the percentage of Value of Mineral Dispatched quoted as the Final Price Offer.

                Rule 8 of Auction Rules with respect to the Value of Mineral Dispatched or the minimum
                production requirement, as specified in the MDPA, whichever is higher

        17 Other provisions                                                       
                                                                                  
        17.1 Exploration Obligation                                               
                                                                                  
          a) The holder of a Composite Licence shall conduct geological exploration of the area under the relevant
             licence or lease, so as to ascertain evidence of mineral contents and shall submit periodic reports in
                                                                                  
             accordance with the Act and rules made thereunder, as applicable to a prospecting licence and all
             reports, studies and other documentation related to the geological exploration of the area under the
             Composite Licence shall be submitted to the State Government and Indian Bureau of Mines.
                                                                                  
          b) The holder of a mining lease shall complete detailed exploration (G1 level exploration) and prepare
             a detailed feasibility study report conforming to Part IV and V of the Mineral (Evidence of Mineral
             Contents) Rules, 2015 over the entire area under the mining lease, within a period of five years from
                                                                                  
             the date of commencement of such Mining Lease.

                    Schedule III: Technical details regarding online electronic auction
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          1. Registration Methodology:                                            
                                                                                  
              In order to submit online bids in the e-bidding process for Minor Mineral Block auction, a Bidder needs to register
              itself with the e-auction website of MSTC Limited, A Government of India Enterprise. For this the Bidder should
              visit the website at (https://mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp) and click on the button
              “Registration”. Bidders already registered with the said website need not register again.
                                                                                  
              On the next page, the ‘General Terms and Conditions’ will be shown– Register as Buyer option . The Bidder shall
              have to click on the link Register as ‘Buyer’ to proceed.           
              On the next page, bidder will be displayed the ‘Buyer Specific Terms and Conditions’ pages. Bidder has to ‘Agree’
              to the terms and conditions and a page to enter the e-mail and mobile number will open. The e-mail and mobile
              will be verified by one time password (OTP).                        
                                                                                  
              After verification via OTP, an online ‘Buyer Registration Form’ will appear on screen and the Bidder has to fill up
              the same with correct information. During this process, the Bidder shall create his user id and password and keep
              note of the same. The Bidder shall ensure that the secrecy of his user id and password is maintained at all times,
              and he/she shall alone be responsible for any misuse of the user id and password.
              While filling in the form, the buyer/bidder has to enter the correct details such as company name, GSTIN and
              address as mentioned on GST certificate, or which is available with the GST portal. The above details will be
              verified with the data available with GST portal. Only when the correct details as required above have been
              mentioned, the bidder shall be able to ‘Preview’ the entered details before ‘Submission’.
                                                                                  
              The Bidder may check the details entered from the ‘Preview’ page before final submission. On successful
              submission of the online registration form, the Bidder shall receive a confirmation mail in the registered email
              address advising the Bidder to remit the registration fee via ‘e-payment’ link. The e-payment link shall be accessible
              to the bidder when he logs - into the portal via the ‘User ID’ and ‘Password’ created by him during registration.
              The fee has to be paid via ‘NEFT/RTGS’ or ‘Online pay’ options under e-payment link.
                                                                                  
              A non-refundable registration fee of INR 10,000/- plus applicable taxes (Rs. 11,800.00) is to be paid online.
                                                                                  
          Note:                                                                   
             I.   The activation of registration via fee received from NEFT/RTGS is subject to the realization of funds by the
                  bank.                                                           
             II.  II. GSTIN is mandatory for completing the registration except for individuals Bidder , however DMG
                  Andhra Pradesh may ask for GSTIN at later stage from Individual Bidders.

             Stage 2: Electronic Auction – Final Price Offer                      
                                                                                  
             a. Conduct of e-auction                                              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
             E-auction is the process of inviting binding Price Offer(s) from Technically qualified bidders through internet for the
             purpose of determining the Successful bidder. During this process, the Technically qualified bidder will be able to
             submit its Price Offer as many times as it wishes against the same mineral block. The Technically qualified bidder will
             remain anonymous to other Technically qualified bidders participating in the electronic auction process as well as to
             MSTC / State Government. The Technically qualified bidder will be able to see the prevailing highest Price Offer
             against the mineral block, but the name of the highest Technically qualified bidder at any point of time will not be
             displayed. The Technically qualified bidder shall have to put its Price Offer over and above the displayed highest bid
             by a minimum increment of Rs 25,000 to become the highest Qualified- Bidder. The electronic auction process will
             have a scheduled start and close time which will be displayed on screen. A Technically qualified bidder will be able to
             put its Price Offer after the start of bid time and till the close time of electronic auction. The current server time (IST)
             will also be displayed on the screen. In the event a Price Offer is received during the last 8 (Eight) minutes before the
             scheduled close time of electronic auction, the close time of electronic auction will be automatically extended by 8
             (Eight) minutes from the last received bid time to give equal opportunity to all other Technically qualified bidders.
             This process of auto extension will continue till no Final Price Offer is received during a period of 8 (Eight) minutes.
             For example, assuming that the initial scheduled close time for a particular electronic auction is 1:00 pm and a Final
             Price Offer is received at 12:55 pm, the scheduled close time shall be revised to 1:03 pm. Again, if a Final Price Offer
             is received at 1:01 pm, the scheduled close time shall be revised to 1:09 pm and so on. In the event that no further Final
             Price Offer is received till 1:09 pm, the electronic auction will close at 1:09 pm. The revised close time will be displayed
             on screen and the Technically qualified bidders are advised to keep refreshing its webpage to get the latest information.
             The above example is only illustrative and meant for explaining the e-auction process only.
             Bidder may please note that first bid to be quoted shall not be more than 10 times the bid increment. Example, Start
             Price is Rs 10,00,000/-, first bid shall be 10,25,000/- or in multiples of Rs 25,000 upto 10 times the increment i.e. Rs
             12,50,000/- only. Bidder shall not be able to submit the first bid greater than 12,50,000/- as first bid.
             Bidders may use Autobid facility available in their logins . Autobid can be used only once per auction and it can be
             enabled by clicking on ‘Autobid’→ enter the ‘Your Bid Max’ value, increment is fixed at Rs 25,000 per bid and
             clicking on ‘Submit’ will enable Autobid on bidder’s behalf. For example, current bid is Rs 15,00,000/- , bidder has
             given Autobid value of Rs 20,00,000/-, if any bid comes lets say 15,25,000/-, the system will enter a bid of Rs
             15,50,000/- on bidder’s behalf and this will continue till bid value reaches Rs 20,00,000/-. Post this set value,
             buyer/bidder needs to manually increase his bid as per normal auction process.
                                                                                  
             In addition to above, bidders shall be able to submit bids as many times they wish to until the auction's closure.
             However, each bid value shall be limited to the value of the immediately preceding bid plus ten (10) times the increment
             only.                                                                
                                                                                  
             Bidders may use ‘EMD Payment and Participation Process’ link available on login page for the detailed procedure.

        SCHEDULE G – Treatment of Shortfall Quantity                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Iron ore                                                                  
                                                                                    
          Grade of mineral for shortfall quantity: The grade to be considered for the shortfall quantity will be based on the weighted
          average grade of mineral actually dispatched in a year; and               
                                                                                    
          Price to be applied for shortfall quantity: the average of the monthly Average Sales Price (Andhra Pradesh State)
          published by IBM for the grade arrived at and in the event Andhra Pradesh State price is not published by IBM then all
          India published price will be considered;                                 
                                                                                    
          Provided in case of no production during a year, weighted average grade, as per dispatches in the preceding year will be
          considered for treatment of shortfall quantity. However, price to be applied for the shortfall quantity shall be based on
                                                                                    
          average of the monthly Average Sales Price (Andhra Pradesh State) published by IBM or all India published price (in
          the event Andhra Pradesh State price is not published by IBM) for the concerned grade for the financial year with no
          production for the purpose of calculation of payment related to Minimum Production and Dispatch Requirement.
                                                                                    
          Provided further in case of no production after the grant of Mining Lease, average grade in the geological report shall
          be considered. Further, the price to be applied for the shortfall quantity shall be based on average of the monthly
          Average Sales Price (Andhra Pradesh State) published by IBM or all India published price (in the event Andhra
                                                                                    
          Pradesh State price is not published by IBM) for the concerned grade for the financial year with no production for the
          purpose of calculation of payment related to Minimum Production and Dispatch Requirement.
