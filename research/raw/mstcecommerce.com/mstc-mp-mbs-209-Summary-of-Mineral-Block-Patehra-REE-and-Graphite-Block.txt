        9  Topography                                                               
           Toposheet Number           63H/15                                        
           Morphology of the area     The area is in form of a ENE-WSW to E-W trending ridge
                                      with moderate to steep slopes towards northern and
                                      southern side. The impressions of gullied erosion were
                                      also observed. The core of the ridge (hill top) is occupied
                                      by BIF and hill slopes are covered with highly foliated
                                      dark grey to black, weathered, carbonaceous phyllites.
