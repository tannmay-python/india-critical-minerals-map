                              Declaration of Preferred Bidder                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          With reference to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone, Graphite
          and Chromite Minerals” dated July 31, 2019 issued by the Government of Odisha for grant
          of Mining Lease, forward e-auction of Jagdalpur Graphite Block was held on October 24,
          2019 on the MSTC e-auction portal.                                      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          We are pleased to inform that Kalinga Enterprises PVT LTD (81661) has submitted the
          highest Final Price Offer of 63.55% (Sixty Three point Five Five percent) and is hereby
          declared the Preferred Bidder for grant of Mining Lease of Jagdalpur Graphite Block.
