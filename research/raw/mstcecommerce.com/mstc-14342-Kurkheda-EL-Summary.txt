                                     granites intruded at different periods which constitute a composite

         6  Mineral Zones/Occurances/ The different Archean rock units surveyed in the area display no
            Structural Details       marked structural peculiarities helpful in unraveling the regional
                                     structure. Even the granites, the largest unit mapped, do not show
                                     any structural features. However, a few zones of shearing and
                                     brecciation are observed inthem. Thesezones trendapproximately
                                     E-W in contrast to the NNW-SSE shearing trends observed in
                                     (sheets64D/02,64D/03and64D/04).Theseminorzonesmayhave
                                     been perhaps developed subsequent to the consolidation of the
                                     granite massif and during one of the post granitic deformation of
                                     minor intensity. The granites appear to have forcefully emplaced
                                     itself in the older rock units along their weaker zones and fold
                                     axes. The older rock units inferred to represent particularly the
                                     metasediments are roof pendants or inclusions. The dykes appear
                                     to have intruded along the pre-existing planes of dislocation or
                                     weakness in a NW-SE trend.
