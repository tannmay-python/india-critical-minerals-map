          Geophysical Survey Geophysical borehole logging has been carried out in all 6 boreholes to
                          identify location of facture, conductive/mineralization zones,
                          subsurface lithology along the borehole to record the physical
                          parameters. Conductive zones are characterized by low values of SPR,
                          Short normal & Long normal resistivity. It has been observed that most
                          of the zones are in clay-dominated areas. The clay-rich layers also
                                                                                  
                          exhibit positive indications of potash mineralization, as evidenced by
                          high gamma counts and low neutron counts, due to the presence of K-
                          bearing minerals in the clay.                           
          XRD Analysis    As per XRD analysis, it is clear, that halite is the major mineral followed
                          by anhydrite, polyhalite and traces of magnesite, quartz and sylvine in
                          Evaporite Group. As per XRD analysis, potash is mostly in polyhalite
                          phase.                                                  
          Drilling        06 boreholes with total drilling meterage of 7017 m.

        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
                                                                                  
          Mineralised Zones Geologically the area comprises alluvium and aeolian sediments of
                          Middle Pleistocene to Holocene age. As the investigation area is covered
                          by thick blanket of alluvium sediments of Middle Pleistocene to
                          Holocene age. Hence, the surface indication for mineralisation is absent
                          in the area.                                            
          Trend           The basin is horizontal to sub-horizontal with dip of stratifications
                                                                                  
          (Dip and Strike) varying from 2º to 3º towards West.                    
          Justification   Based on the G4 level of exploration carried out in the area, Potash and
                          Halite mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer the
                          details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence
                          block area is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station is at Abohar.             
                                                                                  
          Road            The National Highway NH-7 (Chandigarh- Fazilka) passes through the
                          area via. Chandigarh- Patiala bypass- Sangrur-Bhatinda-Fazilka. Entire
                          area is well connected with metalled road and jeepable track.
          Airport         The nearest airport is at Bhatinda and it is 250 Km from Chandigarh.
        7 Hydrography                                                             
                                                                                  
          Local     Surface The area has vast network of minor canals (Distributaries of Lambi
          Drainage Pattern, canal). These minor canals provide water for irrigation and agriculture.
          Rivers, etc     There is no perennial stream in the area.
