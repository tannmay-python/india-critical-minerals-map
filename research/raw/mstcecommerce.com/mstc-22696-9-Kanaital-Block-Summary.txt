        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones Five mineralized bodies named as BAND I to V has been established.
                          The mineralized bodies have both manganese and graphite 
                          mineralization.                                         
          Trend  (Dip and General trend of the mineralization is NE-SW to NW-SE and
                                                                                  
          Strike)         somewhere E-W which is different from the regional trend due to
                          folding and dips SE to SW with an amount 50º to 80º.    
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area,
                          Manganese, Graphite and associated minerals mineralisation has been
                          identified in the block area.                           
                          To infer the details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is
                          required. Hence block area is recommended for grant of Composite
                          Licence.                                                
                                                                                  
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railhead is Balangir which is 35km from the block in the
                          southeast which is part of the Jharsuguda–Vizianagaram line.
          Road            The block is well connected with the State Highway 42 which connects
                          Balangir –Patnagarh-Kantabanji. It is located around 35km northwest
                          of the district head quarter Balangir. The block can be approached
                                                                                  
                          from Patangarh tehsil which is located 20km south of the block.
          Airport         The Jharsuguda Airport at Jharsuguda is the nearest airport which is
                          200km in the north while Swami Vivekananda Airport at Raipur,
                          Chhattisgarh is 210 km away in the west. 11 Biju Patnaik International
                          Airport in the state capital, Bhubaneswar is 360 km away in the east.
