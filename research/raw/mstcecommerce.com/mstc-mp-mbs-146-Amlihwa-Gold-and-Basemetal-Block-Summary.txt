           5  MINERALISED ZONES                                                     
              NUMBER OF MINERAL ZONES Amlihwa gold and basemetal block lies in western
                                     extension of the Chakariya gold mineralised zone in
                                     Amalihwa-Hathipathar-Kapurdei areas. The detailed
                                     studies and prospecting work have been carried out
                                     in Byodhihar, Bagdha, Sonkurwa, Amalihwa,      
                                     Senduriya, Tisihwa, Jamalihwa, Khokhawa and    
                                     Harrahwa villages to find out possible occurrences
                                     of gold mineralisation. Amlihwa block is partially fall
                                     within the mineralized zone-I and extends from 
                                     Amalihwa in the west to Byodhihar in the east. This zone
                                     has strike length of 4 km in discontinuous manner with
                                     thickness of mineralized quartz vein ranging from 0.5 m
                                     to 1 m. This zone is trending in ESEWNW direction.
                                     Scorodite is common in mineralized zone-I.     
              TREND (DIP AND STRIKE) Two main events of deformation have been noticed
                                     which are responsible for the regional WNW-ESE trend

                                     pronounced in intensity and developed at high angle to
                                     the first two deformations. Overall trend of pyllite is
                                     N280° dipping subvertical to vertical in either side i..
                                     NE-SW.

              TOPOSHEET NUMBER       63L/11                                         
              MORPHOLOGY OF THE AREA The topography of the area (63L/11) is rugged, with low
                                     to moderately high strike ridges and elongated narrow
                                     valleys. The maximum elevation is 420 metres above
                                     M.S.L. in south western part of the area and minimum
                                     elevation is 290 metres above M.S.L. in the eastern part
                                     of the area. The area is drained by Gotan and Bijulnadi
                                     and its tributaries.
