          Explanatory Note                                                          
                                                                                    
          Dubarpeth, Copper ore Auction block forms major part of the area of the report entitled
          “Report on Assessment Of Copper And Associated Mineralisation In Dubarpeth-Karanji Block,
                                                                                    
          Chandrapur, Maharashtra”. prepared by Geological Survey of India . Following additional
          information has also been collected from the block area which is as follow:
                                                                                    
            1  The block corner coordinates and area by carrying of DGPS survey (plate A).
            2  Topographical and cadastral details                                  
                                                                                    
            3  Mineralized zone and non mineralized zone and land use details (plate C) have been
               collected for the block after carrying out DGPS survey.              
            4  The block boundary has been superimposed on the Geological map (plate D) of the
               original report for better clarity.                                  
                                                                                    
            5  In the Geological Report (GR) wherever the word reserve is written should be read as
               resource.                                                            
            6  Following new plates have been prepared for the block which will be enclosed along
                                                                                    
               with block summary:                                                  
                   Plate (Plate A) has been prepared indicating the block coordinate, location and
                    cadastral details which is enclosed with block summary          
                                                                                    
                   Plate (B) has been prepared indicating cadastral details, mineralized & non
                    mineralized area and borehole location                          
                   Plate C indicating land use details                             
                                                                                    
                   Plate D showing geological map prepared for the block by taking DGPS survey
                    details and lithology, borehole location from the Geological map of GR
            7  Block summary has been prepared for the auction block from the data collected from
               original report and additional information collected; as             
                                                                                    
                   Block co-ordinates, land use details as per the DGPS survey     
                   Block area given is as per the DGPS survey                      
                   No of boreholes and meterage drilled indicated in the block summary for those
                                                                                    
                    boreholes falling within the block is taken from GR             
                   Resource has been given for the boreholes falling within the block taken from
                    GR. GSI had carried out exploration for copper in Thanewasana and other areas
                                                                                    
                    of Chandrapur district, and based on the studies in these areas it can be safely
                    concluded that the specific gravity (2.8) used in resource calculation of copper
                    ore.                                                            
                                                                                    
                   Based on the density of boreholes drilled and other information available in the
                    block indicates that, the investigation carried out in the block is of Prospecting
                    stage (G-3) as per Mineral (Evidence of Mineral Content) Rules, 2015.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    

                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                   The resource indicated in the block is classified as inferred resource with a code
                    of 333 as per Mineral (Evidence of Mineral Content) Rules, 2015.
