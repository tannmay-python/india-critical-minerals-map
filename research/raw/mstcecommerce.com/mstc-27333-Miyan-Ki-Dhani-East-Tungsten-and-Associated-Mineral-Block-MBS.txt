        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Number of Mineral                                                       
                           Eight (08) mineralized zones (MZ-I to MZ-VIII) have been identified.
          Zones                                                                   
          Trend (Dip and Strike) The mineralized quartz and pegmatite veins in the block
                           predominantly trend NW–SE and NNW–SSE to N–S, with dips varying
                           from 30°–35° towards the west, 55°–65° towards the east, and locally
                           exhibiting vertical to sub-vertical attitudes.         
          Justification    Based on G-3 stage exploration carried out by GSI, tungsten
                           mineralisation has been established and inferred resources have been
                           estimated in the block. Further detailed exploration is required to
                           upgrade the resource confidence level, establish continuity of
                           mineralisation and assess mineability. Hence, the block is
                           recommended for grant of Composite Licence.

          Features                              Details                           
          Road             The block area is located near Rewat Village in Nagaur District,
                           Rajasthan, and is accessible from Degana. The area is well connected
                           to major towns such as Degana, Merta, Nagaur, Ajmer, and Didwana

                           roads, ensuring easy access to the block.              
                                                                                  
          Airport          Jodhpur Airport                                        
        7 Hydrography                                                             
          Local Surface    The block area falls in a semi-arid terrain of Nagaur District. No
          Drainage Pattern, perennial river flows through the block area. Surface runoff is
          Rivers, etc      controlled by seasonal drainage channels.              
        8 Climate                                                                 
                                                                                  
          Mean Annual Rainfall The average rainfall in the district is 36.16 cm.  
          Temperature      The area experiences a semi-arid climate with significant diurnal
                           temperature variation. Mean monthly temperatures range from about
                           17°C in January to 34.5°C in May, while winter temperatures
                           occasionally approach the freezing point. The average daily
                           temperature range is around 14°C.                      
        9 Topography                                                              
                                                                                  
          Toposheet Number Survey of India Toposheet No- 45J/5                    
          Morphology of the The block area forms part of the Degana tungsten belt and is
          area             characterized by an undulating terrain comprising isolated rocky hills
                           rising prominently above the surrounding sandy plains. The area is
                           represented mainly by three residual hills, namely Rewat Hill, Tikli Hill
                           and Phyllite Hill, with elevations ranging from about RL 340 m in the
                           surrounding plains to about RL 470 m at the hill tops.
