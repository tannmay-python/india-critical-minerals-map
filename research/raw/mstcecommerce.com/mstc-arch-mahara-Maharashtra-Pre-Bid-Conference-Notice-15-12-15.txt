                             Notice for Pre-Bid Conference                          
                                                                                    
          1. With reference to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone,
            Iron ore, Bauxite and Tungsten Minerals” dated November 19, 2015 issued by the
                                                                                    
            Government of Maharashtra, a Pre-Bid Conference will be conducted at 1500 hours on
            December 21 2015, at the following venue:                               
                                                                                    
               Yashwantrao Chavan Centre                                            
               General Jagannathrao Bhosale Marg                                    
               Nariman Point                                                        
               Mumbai - 400021                                                      
                                                                                    
          2. All the interested potential Bidders may participate in the Pre-Bid Conference. A
            maximum of 3 (three) representatives of each potential Bidder will be permitted entry.
                                                                                    
            The representatives are advised to produce (i) duly issued authority letter from the
            potential Bidder and (ii) identity documents for entry into the Pre-Bid Conference.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          3. The potential bidders are advised to send an email on or before 12 noon on 19th
            December 2015, to mineralblockauction@mahadgm.gov.in with the names of  
                                                                                    
            representatives of potential Bidders attending the Pre-Bid Conference. Such bidders
            may also send their queries to the email address mentioned above. The email should
            clearly bear the following subject line: “Pre-Bid Conference Participants | bidder name”.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          4. The Agenda of the meeting will be as follows:                          
                                                                                    
              a) Address by the Principal Secretary, Industries Department, Government of
                Maharashtra                                                         
              b) Presentation on the auction process by SBICAP (Transaction Advisor)
                                                                                    
              c) Presentation/Demo of e-auction platform by MSTC (Auction Platform Provider)
              d) Open house session to address queries of potential bidders
