             EXPLORATION      GEOLOGICAL SURVEY OF INDIA                          
             AGENCY                                                               
             Highlights of    Based on the geological investigation and chemical analysis of it
             Geochemical      and trench samples, ore zones of 7m, 7m, 10m, 10m, and 12m
             Sampling         with average Zn value of 843ppm, 1074 ppm, 794 ppm, 848 ppm
                              and 1492 ppm in trench no. TRK-01, TRK-02, TRK-03, TRK-04
                              and TRK-05 respectively could be inferred in the Kehalpur (East)
                              block, Betul district, Madhya Pradesh at 500 ppm Zn cut-off.
                              Based on these chemical analyses, an ore zone of 500m length
                              with average width of 9.2m and 1038 ppm average Zn could be
                              inferred in the area.                               
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                              The geochemical survey on 100mx20m during FS 1991-92 by
                              Mahakud and Raut (1999) established three 500 ppm Zn surface
                              geochemical anomalies.                              
                              Based on the chemical analyses of drilled boreholes data in FS
                              2017-18, it is found that the Zn values varies from 20 ppm to
                              3.33%. The Cu values and Pb values are not significant.
                              Mineralized zones are demarcated with 0.2%, 0.5% and 1% Zn
                              cut-offs at minimum 2m stopping width.              
                                                                                  
             Highlights of    Surface geophysical survey like magnetic, IP, SP and resistivity
             Geophysical survey were carried out to identify the anomalous zones. The results of
                              IP surveys help to draw a conclusion of presence of interesting
                              anomaly zones. These IP zones are associated with high resistivity
                              values, which could be due to resistive metarhyolite and quartz
                              mica schist with which the mineralization is closely associated.
                              Geochemical anomalous zones are more or less corresponding
                              with geophysical anomalies. Magnetic survey has not brought out
                              any significant anomaly over the geochemical anomaly zone.
                                                                                  
                              However, this survey confirmed the presence of anomalous zone
                              in the central part of survey area towards north of base line, maybe
                              because of presence of causative source.            
             Pitting/Trenching Five trenches were excavated (59 cu.m in 5 trenches) to expose
                              the host rock on surface and to check the concentration of
                              basemetal near surface over northern soil geochemical anomaly
                              zone. Its analyses indicate presence of a zinc zone of 500m strike
                              length with average width of 9.2m and 1038 ppm average Zn.

             MORPHOLOGY   OF  Physiographically, the present study area is almost plain land with small
             THE AREA         isolated hillock near Bargaon and Khajri. Gentle undulation can be
                              marked all around in Kehalpur area. Vein quartz, metabasalt, altered
                              metarhyolite form slightly elevated topography whereas gneissic
