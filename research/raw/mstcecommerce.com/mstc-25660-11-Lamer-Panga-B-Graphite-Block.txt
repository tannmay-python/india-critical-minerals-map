          Petrographic Studies Petrographic studies confirm that graphite in Lamer-Panga Block-B
                          occurs as disseminated flakes and thin veins, structurally controlled by
                                                                                  
                          foliation and fractures, with clear evidence of remobilization during
                          metamorphism.                                           
          XRD Studies     The XRD studies validated the presence of crystalline graphite in the
                          block, with quartz and feldspar as common associates.

          Justification   Based on the G3-level exploration carried out in the area, graphite
                          mineralisation has been identified within the block. In view of the
                          presence of potential graphite-bearing zones, it is necessary to establish
                          both lateral and depth continuity of the mineralisation, with focused
                          evaluation of the mineralised areas. Accordingly, the block is
                          recommended for advancement to the next stage of exploration and for
                          grant of a Composite Licence.                           
        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head Nearest railway station is Narla. Railway station which is 25 km from the
                          study area.                                             
          Road            The graphite block is located about 5 km from State Highway–6A, which
                          lies along the western and northwestern margins of the block. The main
                          approach road connects SH-6A at Sirpali, traverses the block, and rejoins
                          the highway near Jamguda village on the western side of the block.
                                                                                  
          Airport         The nearest airport is Utkela Airport, located approximately 45 km from
                          the area.
