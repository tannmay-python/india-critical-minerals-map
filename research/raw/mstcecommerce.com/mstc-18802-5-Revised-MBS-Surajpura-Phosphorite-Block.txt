        6 Accessibility                                                           
                                                                                  
          Nearest Rail Head Sagar is nearest railhead on Bina-Itarsi broad gauge section line of
                          West- Central Railways.                                 
          Road            The study area is well connected upto Hirapur by means of pitch road
                          from Jabalpur, Sagar and Chhatarpur. Hirapur is situated about 80 km
                          north of Sagar on Sagar-Chhatarpur state highway (SH No.15), 190 km
                          from Jabalpur via Damoh and 126 km from Khajuraho, a world-famous
                          heritage and tourist place.                             
                                                                                  
          Airport         Khajuraho Airport from where domestic air services to Delhi and
                          Varanasi are available.                                 
        7 Hydrography                                                             
          Local     Surface The dendritic type of drainage system in the area is controlled by Sira
          Drainage Pattern, nala, Sukku nala and Kalidhar; which are tributaries of Bila River
          Rivers, etc     flowing towards north easterly direction                
        8 Climate                                                                 
                                                                                  
          Mean Annual Rainfall The average annual rainfall is 1168 mm.            
          Temperature     The area experiences sub-tropical climate with temperature varying
                          from 4oC in winter to 47oC in summer.
