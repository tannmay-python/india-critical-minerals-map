                                 Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                  
           “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Iron Ore and Manganese
                                       Mineral”                                   
                                                                                  
         In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals
         (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction)
                                                                                  
         Rules, 2015 as amended from time to time notified thereunder, the Government of
         Odisha has identified 8 (eight) Minerals Blocks as under for electronic auction and hereby
                                                                                  
         invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease:               
                                                                                  
            a) 4 (four) mineral blocks of Iron Ore                                
                                                                                  
            b) 1 (one) mineral blocks of Iron & Manganese                         
            c) 2 (two) mineral blocks of Manganese & Iron Ore                     
                                                                                  
            d) 1(one) mineral block of Manganese                                  
                                                                                  
         Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited
                                                                                  
         both in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.               
                                                                                  
         Eligibility conditions, date and time for participating in the electronic auction are provided
         in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications,
                                                                                  
         updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available
         in electronic form only and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC Limited:
                                                                                  
         (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/).                      
                                                                                  
         Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above website. On
         successful registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for
                                                                                  
         participation in the e-auction process. Model Tender Document and Mineral Block
                                                                                  
         Summary are available free of cost on the website of MSTC Limited.       
                                                                                  
         Last date for purchase of Tender Document after payment of a tender fee on website of
         e- auction platform provider is Wednesday, April 16, 2025 and the last date for
                                                                                  
         submission of the bid is Friday, April 25, 2025 on or before 17:00 hours (Indian Standard
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
         Time). The Price of Tender Document for each mineral block is Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees
                                                                                  
         Five Lakh) plus applicable GST. GST is payable under the Reverse Charge Mechanism.
