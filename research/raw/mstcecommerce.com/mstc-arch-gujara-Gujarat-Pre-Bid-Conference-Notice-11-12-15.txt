                             Notice for Pre-Bid Conference                          
                                                                                    
          1. With reference to the “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone
                                                                                    
            Mineral” dated November 9, 2015 issued by the Government of Gujarat, a Pre-Bid
            Conference will be conducted at 1500 hours on December 16 2015, at the following
            venue:                                                                  
                                                                                    
               GMDC Auditorium                                                      
               Khanij Bhavan, 132 feet Ring Road                                    
               Near University Ground, Vastrapur                                    
               Ahmedabad – 380052                                                   
                                                                                    
          2. All the interested potential Bidders may participate in the Pre-Bid Conference. A
                                                                                    
            maximum of 3 (three) representatives of each potential Bidder will be permitted entry.
            The representatives are advised to produce (i) duly issued authority letter from the
            potential Bidder and (ii) identity documents for entry into the Pre-Bid Conference.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          3. The potential bidders are advised to send an email on or before 12 noon on 15th
            December 2015, to addldir-tech-imd@gujarat.gov.in with the names of representatives
                                                                                    
            of potential Bidders attending the Pre-Bid Conference. Such bidders may also send their
            queries on the email address mentioned above. The email should clearly bear the
            following subject line: “Pre-Bid Conference Participants | Bidder Name”.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          4. The Agenda of the meeting will be as follows:                          
                                                                                    
              a) Address by the Commissioner, Geology and Mining, Government of Gujarat
              b) Presentation on the auction process by SBICAP (Transaction Advisor)
                                                                                    
              c) Presentation by Geovale/GMDC (Technical Advisor)                   
              d) Presentation/Demo of e-auction platform by MSTC (Auction Platform Provider)
              e) Open house session to address queries of potential bidders
