                                Notice Inviting Tender                              
               Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for limestone blocks    
                                                                                    
         In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10B of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act,
         1957, its subsequent amendments and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 and its subsequent
         amendments notified thereunder, Mineral Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh has identified the
         following limestone blocks for the purpose of grant of Mining Lease (ML) (without specified end-user reservation)
         through electronic auction and hereby invites bids in digital format only from eligible bidders.
                                                                                    
          Sr    Name of the Block Tehsil, District     Area (Ha.) Specified end user
          No                                                     reservation        
          1     Mohra (Block A) Balodabazar,Balodabazar-Bhatapara 127.046 None      
          2     Parsabhadar    Balodabazar,Balodabazar-Bhatapara 28.461 None        
                                                                                    
         Terms and conditions, deadlines etc. for participating in the electronic auction are provided in the Tender Document.
         The Model Tender Document for the mineral blocks and mineral block summary are available free of cost in electronic
                                                                                    
         form   and   can   be   downloaded from   the   website of   MSTC    Ltd.  
         (http://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcl/index.jsp) and websites of Mineral Resources Department,
         Government of Chhattisgarh (www.chhattisgarhmines.gov.in) for the purpose of information only. Timelines,
         notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process are available on the website of MSTC Ltd.
                                                                                    
         Those interested and eligible for bidding can participate in the bidding only after online purchase of the Tender
         Document on payment of the non-refundable tender fee of INR. 50,000 (Indian Rupees Fifty Thousand only) excluding
         Goods and Service Tax (GST shall be payable at 18% of tender fee) for Mohra (Block-A) and Prasabhadar Limestone
         blocks separately from the website of e-auction platform provider MSTC Ltd. Bidder shall be able to make payment
         of the Tender Document fee through RTGS/ net banking (through the payment gateway of e-auction platform). After
         registration at MSTC website, interested & eligible bidders can purchase the Tender Document. On successful
                                                                                    
         registration, eligible bidders will obtain Login ID and password necessary for participation in the e- auction process.
         Last date for online purchase of Tender Document is [Monday, July 27, 2020] and the last date for submission of the
         bid is 15:00 Hours IST on [Tuesday, July 28, 2020]. Any further communications, amendments etc. shall be available
                                                                                    
         on the above website of MSTC and the websites of Mineral Resources Department, Government of Chhattisgarh.
