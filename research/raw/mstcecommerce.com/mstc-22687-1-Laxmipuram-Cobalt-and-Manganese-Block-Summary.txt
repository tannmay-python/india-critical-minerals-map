        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
                                                                                  
          Mineralised Zones Based on the disposition of mineralized zone within these old mines and
                          litho-structural setup, an interpreted mineralized zone of about 300 m
                          strike length with a varying width of 10-20 m was identified.
          Trend           The mineralization in this Block trending in NNW-SSE direction, dipping
          (Dip and Strike) steeply due ENE.                                       
          Justification   Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, cobalt and
                          manganese mineralisation has been identified in the block area. To infer
                                                                                  
                          the details of the mining feasibility, detailed exploration is required.
                          Hence block area is recommended for grant of Composite Licence.
