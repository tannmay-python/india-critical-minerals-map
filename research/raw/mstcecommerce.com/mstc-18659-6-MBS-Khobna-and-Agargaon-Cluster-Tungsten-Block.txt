        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Kuhi                                                  
          Road           Khobna Block is 47 km south-east from Nagpur by road. A metallic road
                         from Pachegaon to Kuhi emanating from Nagpur-Umrer- Chandrapur
                         State Highway serves as the main line of communication for the area.
                         Agargaon Block is approachable from Nagpur by Nagpur-Umrer Road, a
                         state highway up to 22 Km, then a metalled Road by furcates for Kuhi
                         (27 Km.) and lastly from Kuhi by a fair weather road (17 Km.), leads to
                         Agargaon.                                                
          Airport        Nagpur at 45 kms.
