          Geochemical     Geochemical data of the bedrock samples, soil samples, pitting,
          Exploration     trenching, channel samples proves that the mineralization is within the
                          pegmatite veins. Integration of these geochemical data on geological
                          map again establishes that the sectors X, Y and Z are the richest part of
                          the area in terms of REE mineralization.                
          SEM-EDX/ EPMA/  Important minerals in terms of mineralization, identified in petrographic
          Petrology Analysis study and EPMA study. Petrography shows allanite occurring as pale
                          yellowish- to reddish-brown, pleochroic, euhedral to enhedral crystals
                          with imperfect cleavage. Allanite is the most dominating REE bearing
                          mineral of Kalapathar-Raghudih block. It is a characteristic mineral of
                          REE bearing veins and usually aggregated with epidote, amphiboles,
                          apatite, barite and sulphides. Allanite has often been observed to be in
                          association with sulphides.                             
                          EPMA analytical data of altered allanite shows enhancement of REE and
                                                                                  
                          Y concentration with removal of Si, Al, Ca, Fe. The data shows that Ti has
                          been incorporated within allanite of the study area. Ti is a minor
                          constituent of REE-rich epidote-group minerals.         
          Drilling        A total of 732m drilling has been carried out in 11 nos of boreholes.
          Borehole Density Spacing of the boreholes has been kept 100m in most of the cases.
                          However, due to local as well as ground problems and the surface
                          geochemical data, the spacing of the boreholes ranges from 73.5m to
                          115m.

        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Nearest Railway station is Purulia.                   
                                                                                  
          Road            The place is very well connected with Kolkata by railways as well as
                          road. The Bankura-Raghunathpur and Asansol-Purulia roads connect
                          the area to Kolkata.                                    
          Airport         The nearest major airports to Purulia, West Bengal, are Birsa Munda
                          Airport in Ranchi, Jharkhand, and Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose
                          International Airport in Kolkata.

        8 Climate                                                                 
          Mean Annual Rainfall The monsoon starts by the end of June and continues up to September
                                                                                  
                          with moderate rainfall (~ 1100mm to 1500 mm).           
          Temperature     The summer spans from March to the middle of June with moderate
                          humidity and with a temperature range of 35ºC- 46ºC sometimes
                          reaches up to 48ºC-50ºC. Winter is normally dry, starts from the end of
                          November, and continues till the end of February with a temperature
                          of 7°C-15°C.
