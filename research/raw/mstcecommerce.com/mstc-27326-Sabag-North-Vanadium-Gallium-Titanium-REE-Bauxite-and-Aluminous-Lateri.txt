        5 Mineralised Zones                                                       
          Mineralised Zones Bauxite mineralization was encountered in 11 of these boreholes, with
                           thicknesses varying from 1.00 to 6.10 meters. An aluminous laterite zone
                           was intersected in 14 boreholes, exhibiting thicknesses ranging from 1.00
                           to 11.35 m. Vanadium mineralization was present in all 20 boreholes, with
                           thicknesses spanning from 4.75 to 31.95 m. Gallium was detected in 17
                           boreholes, with thicknesses between 1.00 and 20.05 m. Rare Earth
                           Elements (REEs) were identified in 6 boreholes, with thicknesses ranging
                           from 1.00 to 1.50 m.                                   
                                                                                  
          Trend            No definite strike and dip have been established in the Sabag (North)
          (Dip and Strike) Block. The bauxite–laterite mineralization occurs as a residual; sub-
                           horizontal lateritic profile developed over weathered gabbro.
          Justification    Based on the G3 level of exploration carried out in the area, Vanadium,
                           Gallium, Titanium, REE, Bauxite and Aluminous Laterite mineralisation
                           has been identified in the block area. To infer the details of the mining
                           feasibility, detailed exploration is required. Hence block area is
                           recommended for grant of Composite Licence.            
                                                                                  
        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head The nearest railway station is Ambikapur Railway station which is 100 km
                           SW from the study area.                                
          Road             The area under investigation is situated 500 km north-east of Raipur and
                           it is well connected by NH-130 up to Ambikapur and then connected by
                           the Ambikapur-Balrampur Road. The prospect area is easily accessible by
                                                                                  
                           road from Ambikapur, Kusmi and Balrampur.              
          Airport          The nearest airport is Bilaspur.
