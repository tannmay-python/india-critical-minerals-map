                Notice for Revision in Auction Attempt of Bhimsain Killa Gold Block
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          1. This is with reference to the “Invitation of Bids for grant of Mining Lease for
            Limestone mineral and for grant of Composite Licence for Copper mineral, Gold
            associated with Silver, Lead & Copper, Copper & associated mineral, and Iron
            Ore minerals” dated July 25, 2025, issued by the Government of Maharashtra for
                                                                                  
            nine (9) mineral blocks.                                              
          2. The Bhimsain Killa Gold Block was earlier notified for auction as a second attempt
            in accordance with Rule 9(11) of the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015, for grant of a
            Composite Licence. Subsequently, the Mineral (Auction) Second Amendment
                                                                                  
            Rules, 2025, notified on October 17, 2025, have brought about significant revisions
            to the auction framework and the applicable tender conditions governing the conduct
            of mineral block auctions.                                            
          3. In light of the aforesaid amendments and considering that the Bhimsain Killa Gold
                                                                                  
            Block was notified as a second attempt in the ongoing auction process, it is hereby
            clarified that the said block shall now be treated as a first attempt within the same
            Notice Inviting Tender, in accordance with the provisions of the Mineral (Auction)
            Rules, 2015, as amended.
