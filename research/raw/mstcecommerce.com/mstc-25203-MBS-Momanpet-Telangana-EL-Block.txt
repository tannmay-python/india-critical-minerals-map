        4  Exploration Details                                                    
        i. Regional Geology             The area comprises of Peninsular Gneissic Complex
                                        (gneiss-migmatite- granite complex) with enclaves of
                                        schistose and Supracrustal rocks; overlain by basalt
                                        flows of Deccan Trap and associated Intertrappean beds
                                        and laterite. The gneiss-migmatite-granite complex is an
                                        assemblage of lithotypes comprising amphibolite,
                                        biotite-schist (± hornblende), quartz-diorite, tonalite,
                                        granodiorite, alkali-felspar-granite/quartz syenite and
                                        other migmatitic varieties. The Deccan Trap, along with
                                                                                  
                                        the associated intertrappean beds, overlays the
                                        Archaean gneiss-migmatite- granite complex in a non-
                                        conformable manner. Six basalt flows of Deccan trap
                                        non-conformably overlie the PGC.          
        ii. Geochemical Investigation   Based on the NGCM (FS 2023-24) analysis, ΣREE of
                                        three samples have yielded values between 700-900
                                        ppm and three samples have yielded values between
                                        400-600 ppm within EL block. Minimum and  
                                        maximum value of Al2O3 is recorded as 11.29 and
                                        29.10 wt%, respectively, with median value of 13.65
                                        wt%. One sample has yielded Vanadium value of more
                                        than 1000 ppm, seven samples yielded values between
                                        600-900 ppm. Higher concentration occurs in south-
                                        western and southern part of the proposed EL block.
