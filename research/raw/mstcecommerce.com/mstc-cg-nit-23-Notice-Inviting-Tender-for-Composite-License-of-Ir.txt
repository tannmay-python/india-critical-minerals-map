          make payment of the Tender Document fee through RTGS/ net banking (through the payment gateway of e-auction
          platform). After registration at MSTC website, interested & eligible bidders can purchase the Tender Document. On
          successful registration, eligible bidders will obtain Login ID and password necessary for participation in the e- auction
          process.                                                                  
          Last date for online purchase of Tender Document is Monday, June 20, 2022 and the last date for submission of the
          bid is 15:00 Hours IST on Tuesday, June 21, 2022. Any further communications, amendments etc. shall be available
