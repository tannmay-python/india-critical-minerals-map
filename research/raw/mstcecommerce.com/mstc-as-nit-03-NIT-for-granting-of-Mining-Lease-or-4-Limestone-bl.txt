                                   Notice Inviting Tender                           
                                                                                    
                  “Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone mineral alongwith
                       establishment of greenfield cement plant in the state of Assam”
               In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 10 (B) of the Mines and Minerals (Development
               and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 notified
                                                                                    
               thereunder, as amended from time to time, the Government of Assam has identified:
                  a) 04 (four) mineral blocks of limestone for electronic auction and hereby invites tenders
                    for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.                      
                                                                                    
               Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both
               in digital and physical format from eligible bidders.                
                                                                                    
               Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms and conditions etc. for participating in the electronic
               auction are provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with
               timelines, notifications, updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral
               blocks are available in electronic form only and can be downloaded from the website of MSTC
               Limited: (https://www.mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/mlcln/index.jsp).
                                                                                    
               Interested bidders can register themselves on the above website. On successful registration,
               bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e-auction process.
                                                                                    
               Model Tender Document and Mineral Block Summaries are available free of cost on the
               website of MSTC Limited.                                             
                                                                                    
               Last date for purchase of Tender Document after payment of a tender fee on the website of
               MSTC Limited is 20th February, 2026 and the last date for submission of the bid is 21st
               February, 2026 on or before 17:00 hours (Indian Standard Time).      
               The tender fee is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees five lakh) for each mineral block. The price of Tender
                                                                                    
               Document is exclusive of GST and the GST is payable under Reverse Charge Mechanism.
