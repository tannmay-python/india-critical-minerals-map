        6 Accessibility                                                           
          Nearest Rail Head Narla is the nearest railway station from the block.  
          Road            The graphite block is situated at a distance of about 5 km from State
                          Highway–6A, which lies along the western and northwestern margins of
                          the block. The principal access road connects the block with SH–6A at
                          Balipada, traverses the block area, and rejoins near Patangpadar village
                                                                                  
                          on the northwestern side of the block.                  
          Airport         The nearest air facility is Utkela Airport, located at a distance of about 45
                          km from the block, while Veer Surendra Sai Airport, Jharsuguda is
                          situated approximately 245 km away.
