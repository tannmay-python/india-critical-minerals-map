                            Notice Inviting Tender                    
                                                                      
                                                                      
       "Invitation of bids for grant of Mining Lease for Limestone & Manganese Ore minerals
       and for grant of Composite Licence for Aluminous Laterite, Bauxite and Aluminous
       Laterite, Diamond, Gold, Gold & Base Metal (Lead, Silver), Iron Ore (Magnetite and
       Hematite), Iron Ore & Manganese Ore, Base Metal (Lead, Copper, Silver, Zinc &
       associated minerals) and Limestone minerals."                  
                                                                      
       In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 1O B and Section 11 of the Mines and Minerals
                                                                      
       (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 and in accordance with the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015
       notified thereunder, as amended from time to time, the Government of Madhya Pradesh has identified:
         a) 05 mineral blocks (4 blocks of Limestone and I block of Manganese Ore) for Electronic
            Auction and hereby invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Mining Lease.
         b) 13 mineral blocks ( 1 block of Aluminous Laterite, 1 block of Basemetal (Lead, Copper and
            Associated minerals), l block of Basemetal (Lead, Copper, Silver and associated minerals), 1
            block of Basemetal (Lead, Zinc, Copper, Silver and associated minerals), 3 blocks of Bauxite
            & Aluminous Laterite, l blocks of Diamond, 1 block of Gold, 1 block of Gold & Base metal(
            Lead, Silver) and associated minerals, l block of Iron Ore ((Magnetite and Hematite), 1 block
           of Iron Ore and Manganese Ore, 1 block of Manganese Ore) for electronic auction and hereby
                                                                      
            invites tenders for the purposes of grant of Composite Licence.
       Accordingly, financial bids are invited in digital format only and technical bids are invited both in
                                                                      
       digital and physical format from eligible bidders.             
                                                                      
       Eligibility conditions, date and time, terms, and conditions etc. for participating in the electronic auction
       are provided in the Tender Document. Detailed Tender Documents along with timelines, notifications,
       updates and other details for the e-auction process for the mineral blocks are available in electronic
       form only and can be downloaded from thee-auction portal of MSTC Limited (Mineral Block Auction
       (mstcecommerce.com)).                                          
                                                                      
                                                                      
       Interested and eligible bidders can register themselves on the above e-auction portal. On successful
       registration, eligible bidders will obtain login ID and password necessary for participation in the e
       auction process. Interested and eligible bidders can participate in the bidding only after online purchase
      of the Tender Document on payment of the Tender fee from the above e-auction portal. The Tender Fee
      shall be waived for bidders who have previously purchased the Tender Document for any specific block
      in prior auction attempts. Any revision, clarifications, addendum, con-igendum, time extensions, etc. to
