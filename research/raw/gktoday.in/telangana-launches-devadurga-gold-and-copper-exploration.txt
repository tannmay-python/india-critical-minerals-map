Telangana Launches Devadurga Gold and Copper Exploration
 August 28, 2026 
Current Affairs Today - Latest Current Affairs - 2026-2027
Telangana launched a 
gold
 and 
copper
 mining exploration programme at Lingadahalli village in Devadurga taluk of Raichur district, Karnataka, on 27 August 2026. The project covers 199.13 square kilometres and marks the first entry of Singareni Collieries Company Limited into critical and strategic mineral exploration.
Devadurga Mineral Block
Devadurga is a mineral-bearing area in Raichur district of Karnataka. The exploration block for 
gold
 and 
copper
 spans 199.13 square kilometres and was allotted through an auction conducted by the Union Ministry of Mines.
Singareni Collieries Company Limited
Singareni Collieries Company Limited is a state-owned coal mining enterprise with more than 136 years of history. The company is known for coal mining and has now entered exploration for gold and copper in Karnataka.
Exploration Agreement and Administrative Steps
An exploration agreement for the Devadurga block was signed in Bengaluru on 6 August 2026 between Karnataka Mines and Geology Department Director Rangappa and SCCL Director K. Venkateshwarlu. On 15 August 2026, SCCL Chairman and Managing Director Dr. Buddha Prakash Jyoti announced the exploration licence during Independence Day celebrations.
Important Facts for Exams
Gold and copper are classified as metallic minerals and are used in industrial, electrical and financial applications.
Raichur district is located in Karnataka and is known for mineral and power-related activities.
Critical and strategic minerals are often linked with national security, energy systems and industrial supply chains.
Coal India Limited and Singareni Collieries Company Limited are major public sector mining enterprises in India.
Power Sector Linkages
During the visit, Telangana Deputy Chief Minister Mallu Bhatti Vikramarka met Karnataka Energy Minister K. J. George to discuss a joint venture between SCCL and Karnataka Power Corporation Limited. The proposal was linked to coal availability for thermal power plants.
Operational Arrangements
Officials were directed to set up a dedicated site office and a helipad in Devadurga for field operations. Such arrangements are used in remote mining areas for transport, supervision and logistical support.
Mineral Exploration in India
Mineral exploration in India is regulated through licences and auctions under the Union Ministry of Mines. Exploration blocks are identified for geological survey, resource estimation and future extraction planning.
