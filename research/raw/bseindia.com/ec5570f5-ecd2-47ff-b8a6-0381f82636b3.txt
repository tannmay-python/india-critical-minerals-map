                                   Press Release                                  
             Hindustan Copper appoints Mine Developer cum Operator (MDO)          
                                                                                  
        Re-opening of Rakha Mine in East Singbhum, Jharkhand, after more than 20 years
                                                                                  
                                 Kolkata, 09.01.2025                              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
      Consequent upon successful tendering action, Hindustan Copper Ltd. (HCL) has appointed
                                                                                  
      M/s South West Mining Limited (SWML) as the Mine Developer cum Operator (MDO) for re-
      opening and expansion of Rakha Copper Mine, development of a new underground mine at
                                                                                  
      Chapri and commissioning of a new concentrator plant for a period of 20 years, extendable by
      another 10 years. The mining operation in Rakha Mine was suspended since 2001 on account
                                                                                  
      of unviable operations.                                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       Shri G. Kishan Reddy, Hon’ble Union Minister of Coal and Mines, along with Shri Ghanshyam
                                                                                  
        Sharma, CMD, HCL, Shri Sanjiv Kumar Singh, Director (Mining), HCL, Shri Anil Sood, Whole
        Time Director, SWML, Shri Abhisek Jain, CFO, SWML, Shri Sanjay Singh, AVP (Operations &
                                                                                  
        Projects), SWML, Shri S S Sethi, ED, ICC/HCL, and other senior officials at the signing of the
      Mining Services Agreement between M/s Hindustan Copper Limited and M/s South West Mining
                                                                                  
                                Limited in Ranchi today.

      The Mining Services Agreement was signed today between M/s Hindustan Copper 
                                                                                  
      Limited and M/s South West Mining Limited in Ranchi in the august presence of Shri G.
      Kishan Reddy, Hon’ble Union Minister of Coal and Mines, along with Shri Ghanshyam
      Sharma, CMD, HCL, Shri Sanjiv Kumar Singh, Director (Mining), HCL, Shri Vivek
                                                                                  
      Sharma, Director, MoM, Shri Anil Sood, Whole Time Director, SWML, Shri Abhisek Jain,
      CFO, SWML, Shri Sanjay Singh, AVP (Operations & Projects), SWML, Shri S S Sethi, ED,
                                                                                  
      ICC/HCL, and other senior officials.                                        
                                                                                  
      The Letter of Award (LoA) was issued to M/s South West Mining Limited (SWML) on
                                                                                  
      25.11.2024. The total capital expenditure (CAPEX) for this project is estimated to be
      approximately INR 2,700 crore. The direct and indirect employment will be around 10,000.
