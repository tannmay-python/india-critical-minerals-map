Epsilon Advanced Materials completes acquisition of LFP cathode technology center in Germany

06-Feb-2024 09:29 GMT
News

Amit Panday

Acquiring the CAM technology center from Johnson Matthey solidifies EAM's position as a global EV battery material provider

Mumbai-based lithium-ion battery materials company Epsilon Advanced Materials (EAM) has finalized the acquisition of Johnson Matthey’s lithium-ion phosphate (LFP) cathode active material technology center in Moosburg, Germany, it announced on February 2.
