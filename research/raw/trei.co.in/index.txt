Company
 Profile
 Toyotsu Rare Earths India Pvt. Ltd. (TREI, incorporated in
 October 2009, is a subsidiary of Toyota Tsusho
 Corporation, Japan.) The company commenced commercial
 operations in 2013 and is strategically located in the
 Andhra Pradesh Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) in
 Visakhapatnam, India.
Business 
Activities
 TREI is engaged in the processing and refining of rare
 earth elements (REEs) such as lanthanum, cerium,
 praseodymium, and neodymium, which are essential materials
 in the production of electric vehicles (EVs), wind
 turbines, electronics, defense systems, and various green
 technologies.
 The company plays a critical role in India’s rare earth
 value chain by processing mixed rare earth carbonates.
Parent 
Company
 Toyota Tsusho Corporation is the trading arm of the Toyota
 Group and a global leader in materials, logistics, and
 technology solutions. TREI represents its strategic
 investment in securing a stable supply of rare earths from
 India, one of the world’s emerging sources of critical
 minerals.
Workforce 
& Culture
 TREI employs a highly skilled workforce of over 100
 professionals, maintaining high standards in safety,
 environmental compliance, operational efficiency, and
 employee welfare. It has consistently received high
 ratings for job security and work-life balance on
 platforms like Ambition Box.
