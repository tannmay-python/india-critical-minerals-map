A leading Chemical Powerhouse

built over 8 decades on a foundation of Innovation

Incorporated in January 1939 taking over India's first Soda Ash factories in Dhanghadra, Gujarat, we are now amongst the country’s fastest growing multi-product, multi-location chemical companies.

With a successful record of innovating and pioneering new products and processes, we have a strong presence in the Chlor-Akali, Soda Ash and PVC business segments. Our competitive edge is further enhanced by the diversity of our product portfolio moving towards high value add specialty chemicals.

Founder Chairman Padma Bhushan Sahu Shriyans Prasad Jain 1908 - 1992

Born on November 3rd 1908, Sahu Shriyans Prasad Jain has been the most important source of inspiration to the Company providing it the foundation to build a successful and socialable business. Not only was Mr. S.P. Jain an industrialist far ahead of his time, but also was a leader, a philanthropist, and activist and a visionary whose contribution to the country in the fields of education, healthcare, social welfare and commerce have been far reaching till today. We wish to pay tribute to this truly noble Indian.

our journey

A Story Going Back Decades

1925

Setup India’s first Soda Ash factory at Dhrangadhra, Gujarat in West India.

1936

The plant was taken over and run under the name of Dhrangadhra Chemical Works.

1959

Commissioning of the chlor-alkali plant at Sahupuram in Tamil Nadu.

1968

Founded India’s first
Trichloroethylene plant.

1970

Integrated PVC plant was erected making the Company one of the first in the nascent petrochemicals field.

1970

Set up a plant to manufacture Synthetic Rutile, the first of its kind in Asia, and even today, one of the few of its kind in the world.

1978

First company in India to produce UTOX a buff coloured pigment manufactured by recovering TiO2 particles

1983

First company in South East Asia to use imported merchant VCM to produce PVC

1986

To reflect the expanded activity spectrum, and its emergence as a multi-product and multi-locational company, the corporate name was changed simply to DCW Limited.

1993

Pilot plant to produce Synthetic Iron Oxide Pigment was setup, a result of R&D efforts.

2007

Caustic Soda plant conversion to membrane cell process from mercury process

2008

Commissioned 2X25MW Coal based Power plant.

2016

Commissioned one of Asia's largest scale of Synthetic Iron Oxide Pigment Plant.

2017

Commenced production of C-PVC as the first & only producer in India.

2023

Doubled CPVC capacity to 20,000 Tonnes

Discover Our Products

Synthetic Rutile

Synthetic Rutile is a chemically modified ilmenite sand that has had most of the ferrous, non-titanium components removed and upgraded into Synthetic Rutile which contains ≈ 95wt.%.

Synthetic Iron Oxide Pigment

DCW is one of the largest commercial scale manufacturers of both, Red and Yellow Synthetic Iron Oxide pigments in India.

C-PVC

An important thermoplastic, C-PVC is used across a variety of industries from commercial to residential water piping systems.

Ammonium Bicarbonate

Manufactured using Ammonia Condensate liquid & Carbon Dioxide, its primarily used in Chemical & Pharma manufacturing and the Food industry.

Soda Ash

A non flammable, chemically stable white powder, Soda Ash is used to manufacture Detergents, Petroleum, Cleaning Products, Water Treatment & Paper Manufacturing.

Sodium Bicarbonate

A non flammable, chemically stable white powder, Sodium Bicarbonate is supplied in 50kg HDPE bags.

Caustic Soda

Caustic Soda (Sodium Hydroxide), is a basic product widely used in diverse industrial sectors, either as a raw material or as an auxiliary chemical.

PVC

Used in Agricultural Products, Building Industry - Windows, Profiles, Flooring Tiles  etc.

Hydrochloric Acid

Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) is commonly used in the process of water treatment, leaching, and as an analytical agent.

Liquid Chlorine

Elemental Chlorine is commercially produced from brine by electrolysis, through the chlor-alkali process as a by-product in the production of Caustic Soda.

Trichloroethylene

In metal de-greasing, dry cleaning drying electronic parts, as an extractions solvent for oils fats, waxes, refrigerant and also as a fumigant.

Utox

A pale yellow coloured odourless powder, Utox exhibits chemical resistance to acid with an average particle size of 1 micron.

Ferric Chloride

Produced at our Sahupuram facility its used in Effluent Water, Sewage Treatment, Textiles and Etching industry.

Sodium Hypochlorite

Sodium Hypochlorite is produced by the scrubbing of Chlorine Gas with Caustic Soda Lye.
