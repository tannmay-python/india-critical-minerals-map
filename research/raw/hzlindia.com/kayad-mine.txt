Kayad Mine
Kayad Mine
Overview
Modern mining, powered by precision
The newest addition to Hindustan Zinc’s portfolio, Kayad Mine, is known for its systematic and scientific underground mining. It has evolved from 7.7 Mt R&R in FY 2013–14 to producing 9.9 Mt of ore till FY 2025.
Location
Kayad, Ajmer, Rajasthan, India
Mining type
Underground
Holding capacity
8.1 Mt R&R
Material produced
Zinc concentrate, Lead concentrate
Commissioned
2011
Site significance
Strategically located in Rajasthan, Kayad Mine plays a critical role in supporting Hindustan Zinc’s integrated operations. Ore extracted here is transported to the Rampura Agucha Mill, enabling efficient beneficiation and strengthening supply consistency.

Processes
The making of every metric tonne
Long-hole open stoping
Ore is extracted through precise drilling and blasting, enabling deeper access, controlled breakage, and safer operations.
Cemented paste fill
Mined-out voids are filled with paste tailings to maintain structural integrity and enable safe, continuous extraction sequences.
Underground ore handling
Ore is crushed below ground and conveyed efficiently, reducing surface load and improving material flow for hoisting.
Vertical ore transport
Crushed ore is lifted to the surface via high-capacity shaft systems, ensuring fast transfer with reduced energy loss.
Milling and flotation
Surface plants process ore through grinding and flotation, yielding high-purity zinc and lead concentrate for further refining.
Ventilation, dewatering, automation
Advanced support systems enhance safety, water reuse, and process control, enabling round-the-clock, optimised operations.
