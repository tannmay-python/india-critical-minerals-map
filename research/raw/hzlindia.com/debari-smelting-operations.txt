Debari smelting operations 
Overview
Building excellence in high-grade zinc production
Commissioned in 1967, Debari is Hindustan Zinc’s first smelter, transforming intermediate calcine into pure zinc, generating surplus material that fuels other metallurgical operations across India.
Location
North of Udaipur, Rajasthan
Metal capacities
88 KT annually
Products
High-Grade Zinc (HG)
Power
Site significance
Debari Smelter supports our integrated zinc operations by supplying surplus calcine to other hydrometallurgical smelters, optimising resource utilisation and reinforcing regional industrial linkages.

Processes
The making of every ingot 
Roast–Leach–Electrowinning (RLE)
A hydrometallurgical process that transforms zinc concentrate into ultra-pure zinc through oxidation, leaching, and electrolysis.
Oxidises zinc concentrate; gases sent to acid plant.
Calcine is dissolved to form a zinc sulphate solution.
Removes impurities via cold and hot purification.
Yields high-purity zinc cathodes from zinc solution.
Molten zinc is cast into solid ingots.
