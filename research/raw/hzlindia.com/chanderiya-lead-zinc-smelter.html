Chanderiya smelting operations

Overview

Strengthening India’s leadership in refined metals

Chanderiya Lead-Zinc Smelter (CLZS) stands as one of the world’s largest zinc-lead smelting complexes, enabling large-scale and sustainable metal production.

Location

North of Udaipur, Rajasthan

Metal capacities

Pyro capacity (including Ausmelt): 200 KT
Hydro Capacity: 630 KT
 

Products

SHG regular, SHG Jumbo, Zamak-3, pure lead, PW Zinc, Silver

Power

274.5 MW Captive+ 22MW STG Generation

Site significance

Positioned close to major mining sites, Chanderiya strengthens Hindustan Zinc’s integrated operations while enabling livelihood security, local upskilling, and long-term regional progress.

Quality Management System
Occupational Health & Safety Management System
Environmental Management System
Energy Management System
5S Workplace Management
NABL accredited
ISO 17025:2017 Certified
Chanderiya Lead-Zinc Smelter becomes India’s first Zinc Mark-certified site
USPs

Operational excellence through advanced technologies

Ausmelt™ lead smelter

Top-submerged lance furnace for efficient high-purity lead production.

Silver and cadmium recovery

Extracts minor metals from slag, enhancing overall value.

Captive power

On-site energy via thermal, DG, and waste-heat systems.

Processes

The making of every ingot

Roast–Leach–Electrowinning (RLE)

A hydrometallurgical process that transforms zinc concentrate into ultra-pure zinc through oxidation, leaching, and electrolysis.

Oxidises zinc concentrate; gases sent to acid plant.

Calcine is dissolved to form a zinc sulphate solution.

Removes impurities via cold and hot purification.

Yields high-purity zinc cathodes from zinc solution.

Molten zinc is cast into solid ingots.

Imperial Smelting Process (ISP)

A pyrometallurgical system that simultaneously recovers lead and zinc using sintering, smelting, and multi-stage refining.
