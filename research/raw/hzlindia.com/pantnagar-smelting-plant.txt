Pantnagar smelting plant
Pantnagar smelting plant
Overview
Leading India’s sustainable metal production
Pantnagar Metal Plant is India’s largest and the world’s third-largest silver producer, pioneering fully renewable energy operations. Finished metals are eco-labelled, underscoring our commitment to sustainability.
Location
Pantnagar, Uttarakhand
Metal capacities
Silver - 960 tpa
Zinc - 4,65,000 tpa
Lead - 1,50,000 tpa
Products
SHG & CGG Zinc, Silver Ingot & Powder
Power
Site significance
Pantnagar’s renewable-powered operations cut 31,309 MT CO₂e emissions, advancing HZL’s net-zero pledge while producing world-class silver recognised by the LBMA. Strategically located near Rudrapur city, the plant also benefits regional logistics and industrial linkages.
Quality Management System
Occupational Health & Safety Management System
Environmental Management System
Energy Management System
5S Workplace Management
USPs
Operational excellence through advanced technologies
Renewable energy operations
The entire plant is powered by green electricity, reducing the carbon footprint.
LBMA accreditation
Listed in LBMA’s ‘London Good Delivery’, guaranteeing 99.99% pure silver and global quality standards.
Processes
The making of every ingot 
Silver Refinery Process
Combines Pyro (reduction & oxidation) and Hydro (electrolysis) processes to produce ultra-pure silver powder and 30 kg bars.
Refinery Mud and High-Grade Metal undergo reduction and oxidation to form cupel alloy and BBOC anodes.
BBOC anodes undergo electrolysis, enriching silver and producing powder.
Silver powder is melted in an induction furnace and cast into 30 kg bars.
