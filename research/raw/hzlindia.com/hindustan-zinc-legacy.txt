Legacy And Reach
Hindustan Zinc Legacy
Forging excellence: From ore to global leadership
Our legacy
A story of scale and substance
HZL has led from the front in shaping India's mining and metals sector. Each milestone reflects our deep commitment to operational excellence, sustainable growth, and nation-building. With over five decades of experience and a forward-looking approach, we continue to power industries, communities, and progress, across India and the world. Today, we stand as one of the world’s leading integrated producers of zinc, lead, and silver, driven by strategic foresight and technological innovation.
Ours is a legacy, not just measured in metric tonnes or milestones, but in the people we empower, the ecosystems we sustain, and the future we’re helping build.
Our Journey
A timeline of milestones
2024
Achieved record production of metal and silver
Became the 3rd largest silver producer globally
Ranked #1 globally in S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment 2023
Commissioned India’s first Fumer facility, new alloy plant, and Rajpura Dariba Mill
Incorporated Hindmetal Exploration Services Private Limited
2020
Ranked #1 in Asia Pacific Metals & Mining by Dow Jones Sustainability Index 2019
Became the 6th largest primary silver producer globally and the fastest growing silver company
2018
Full transition of Rampura Agucha from open cast to underground
HZL became the only private Indian company on the London Bullion Market Association list
2016
Declared a special dividend of 1200% — the largest in India’s private sector at the time
2013
Commercial production started at Rampura Agucha UG Mine and Kayad Mine
2012
Pantnagar Silver Refinery commissioned with 500 MT annual capacity
2010
Rajpura Dariba Hydro-III zinc smelter commissioned with 0.2 million MTPA capacity
2009
HZL became the 2nd largest integrated zinc producer globally
2007
Sindesar Khurd Mine commissioned with 0.3 million MTPA capacity
Hydro II smelter commissioned
2005
Hydro I smelter commissioned
HZL became the 3rd largest integrated zinc producer in the world
2002
Vedanta Group (then Sterlite) acquired 26% equity stake in HZL — a  turning point in our growth story
1989
Chanderiya Smelter inaugurated
Production commenced at Rampura Agucha
1984
Rajpura Dariba Mine commissioned
1979
Discovery of Rampura Agucha – now the largest underground zinc mine in the world
1978
Vizag Smelter commissioned
Balaria Mine at Zawar commissioned
1966
Foundation of Debari Smelter laid
