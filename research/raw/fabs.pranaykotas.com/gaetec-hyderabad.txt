GAETEC
Gallium Arsenide Enabling Tech Centre | Compound Semiconductor
Published
October 19, 2026
Tip
Why This Matters
India’s strategic Gallium Arsenide fab — produces radar and electronic warfare chips for defence.
What does this facility make?
Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) Monolithic Microwave Integrated Circuits (MMICs) — specialised chips used in military radar, electronic warfare systems, and satellite communications.
Why does it matter?
GAETEC is a DRDO facility that gives India sovereign capability in GaAs MMICs — chips that cannot be easily procured from abroad due to export controls. It is small-scale and defence-focused, but strategically essential.
