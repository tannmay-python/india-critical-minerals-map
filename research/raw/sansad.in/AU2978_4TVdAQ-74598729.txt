                            CRITICAL MINERALS RESERVES                            
                                                                                  
          2978. SHRI ADITYA YADAV:                                                
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) the details of the concrete domestic exploration outcomes, geographic mappings
                                                                                  
          and estimated reserves discovered for critical and strategic minerals like Lithium,
          Cobalt, Nickel and Graphite across the country;                         
          (b) the details of international joint ventures, asset acquisitions and commercial
          bilateral agreements secured through Khanij Bidesh India Limited (KABIL) to protect
          India's supply security;                                                
                                                                                  
          (c) the details of policy incentives offered to private exploration agencies to accelerate
          the mining of deep-seated critical minerals and the total private investment attracted
          so far; and                                                             
          (d) the roadmap to minimise reliance on aggressive foreign imports particularly from
                                                                                  
          geopolitical monopolists for minerals vital to India's Electric Vehicle (EV) and green
          tech transitions?                                                       
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR COAL AND MINES                    
                            (SHRI SATISH CHANDRA DUBEY)                           
                                                                                  
          (a): The detail of resources augmented for Lithium, Cobalt, Nickel and  
                                                                                  
          Graphite across the country, through exploration projects undertaken by the
          Geological Survey of India (GSI) and funded by National Mineral Exploration and
          Development Trust (NMEDT), are placed at Annexure.                      
                                                                                  
          (b): Khanij Bidesh India Limited (KABIL) has signed an Exploration and Development
          Agreement with CAMYEN, a state-owned enterprise of Catamarca province of
          Argentina, for exploration and mining of 05 Lithium Brine Blocks in Argentina covering
          an area of 15703 Hectares. Further, KABIL has also signed a Memorandum of
                                                                                  
          Understanding with the Critical Mineral Office, Department of Industry, Science,
          Energy and Resources, Government of Australia for co-operation in the field of mining
          & processing of critical and strategic minerals.                        
                                                                                  
          (c): NMEDT has launched the following initiatives to strengthen the private sector
          participation in mineral exploration in the country including critical and strategic
                                                                                  
          minerals:-                                                              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
            (i) 100% funding for mineral exploration projects of Notified Private Exploration
            Agencies.                                                             
                                                                                  
            (ii) The holders of Exploration Licence (EL), granted through auction, can claim up
            to 50% of the direct cost on exploration within a ceiling of ₹20 Crore.
            (iii) The holders of Composite Licenses (CL), granted through auction, can claim
                                                                                  
            up to 50% of direct cost on exploration within a ceiling of ₹8 crore. 
            (iv) Partial reimbursement of up to 50% within a ceiling of ₹300 crore, to CL holders
                                                                                  
            for eligible exploration expenditure on approved offshore exploration activities
            within CL blocks.                                                     
                                                                                  
            (v) Exploration incentive of 25% of the approved projects cost of the G4 items for
            critical, strategic and deep-seated minerals to the exploration agencies if the block
            is successfully auctioned or upgraded to G3 stage.                    
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (d): In order to minimise reliance on foreign imports of minerals vital to India's electric
          vehicle and green tech transitions and to strengthen the supply chain of critical and
          strategic minerals, the following steps have been taken by the government:-
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
            (i) National Critical Mineral Mission (NCMM) has been launched in 2025 with an aim
            to secure a long-term sustainable supply of critical minerals and strengthen
            country’s critical mineral value chains encompassing all stages from mineral
            exploration and mining to beneficiation, processing and recycling through recovery
                                                                                  
            from end-of-life products.                                            
            (ii) Critical and strategic mineral exploration projects across the country have been
                                                                                  
            increasingly taken up by GSI from 195 in field season 2024-25 to 300 in field season
            2026-27. Funding by NMEDT for critical and strategic mineral exploration projects
            also increased from 62 projects in 2024-25 to 84 projects in 2025-26. 
                                                                                  
            (iii) The government has so far successfully auctioned 56 blocks of critical minerals
            and 06 Exploration License (EL) blocks for critical minerals in the country.
                                                                                  
            (iv) The government has launched an Incentive Scheme of ₹1,500 crore in October,
            2025 to develop recycling capacity for the separation and production of critical
            minerals from secondary sources.                                      
                                                                                  
            (v) Guidelines have been issued in November, 2025 for funding Pilot Projects for
            recovery of critical minerals from overburden/ tailings/ fly ash/ Red mud etc.
                                                                                  
            (vi) Under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957
            amended in 2025, the scope of NMEDT has been expanded to support critical
            mineral exploration and mining overseas. The "Overseas Mineral Exploration
            Promotion Guidelines" have been circulated in July 2026 to promote Indian public
            and private sector entities to undertake mineral exploration in overseas
                                                                                  
            jurisdictions.
