                            UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 4271                           
                              ANSWERED  ON 26.03.2025                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                        ROADSHOW  ON OFFSHORE  SAND MINING                        
                                                                                  
          4271. ADV DEAN KURIAKOSE:                                               
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a)  whether the Government has organised a roadshow to seek proposed   
          investment for offshore sand mining in Kerala recently;                 
          (b)  if so, the places where the Roadshows were held and the sites which have
                                                                                  
          been identified for offshore sand mining in Kerala;                     
          (c)  the total amount spent by the Indian Rare Earths Limited (IREL) for the event
          and the break up thereof;                                               
          (d)  whether any Public Sector Unit of the Government of Kerala was involved in
                                                                                  
          the local co-ordination of the event;                                   
          (e)  if so, the details of the funds spent by the Government of Kerala towards the
          local hospitality and arrangement of the event; and                     
          (f) whether any impact study on the livelihood of fishermen community was
          conducted by IREL and if so, the details thereof?                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                                                                                  
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
          (a) & (b) Yes, The Ministry of Mines launched the first tranche of offshore mineral
          auctions on November 28, 2024, offering a total of 13 offshore blocks. Out of these,
          three blocks are off the coast of Kerala are pertaining to construction sand.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          To ensure transparency and encourage wider participation from the potential bidders,
          the Ministry of Mines organized two roadshows. The first roadshow was conducted at
          Porbandar, Gujarat, in December 2024, and the second roadshow was conducted at
          Kochi, Kerala, in January 2025.                                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (c) to (e) As per the information received from Govt. of Kerala, the cost for organizing
          the roadshow, of rupees five lakh, was borne by Indian Rare Earths Limited (IREL).
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (f) No impact study on the livelihoods of the fishermen community was conducted by
          Indian Rare Earths Limited (IREL).
