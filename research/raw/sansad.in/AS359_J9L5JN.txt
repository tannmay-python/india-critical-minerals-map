              RARE EARTH  ELEMENTS (REE) EXPLORATION IN WEST BENGAL               
                                                                                  
          *359. SHRI JYOTIRMAY SINGH MAHATO:                                      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) the current status of mineral exploration at G2 (General Exploration) stage in the
                                                                                  
          blocks at Belma-Malthor, Chirugora, Mednitar and Purdaha in Purulia district, West
          Bengal, including quantum of drilling, sampling and geophysical surveys completed
          and resource estimates established or expected therefrom;               
          (b) whether GSI has formulated any timeline for upgrading these G2 blocks to G1
          (Detailed Exploration) stage and if so, the details thereof;            
                                                                                  
          (c) whether any of these blocks are being considered for auction as Composite
          Licences or Mining Leases upon completion of G2 exploration and if so, the
          expected timeline and modalities thereof; and                           
          (d) the details of the specific steps being taken by the Government to enhance the
                                                                                  
          commercial attractiveness of REE blocks in West Bengal for prospective bidders
          given that the Kalapathar-Raghudih REE block has received no bids under Tranche
          VI?                                                                     
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) to (d): A statement is laid on the table of the House.

          THE STATEMENT   REFERRED  TO  IN REPLY TO  LOK SABHA  STARRED           
          QUESTION   NO.359  REGARDING   'RARE  EARTH   ELEMENTS    (REE)         
          EXPLORATION  IN WEST  BENGAL'  ASKED BY  SHRI JYOTIRMAY  SINGH          
          MAHATO, MEMBER  OF PARLIAMENT FOR REPLY ON 12.08.2026.                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) and (b): The Geological Survey of India (GSI) has completed field work for general
          exploration for Rare Earth Elements (REE) mineralisation in Belma-Malthor block in
          Purulia district, West Bengal under which geophysical surveys of 50 Line Kilometers
          and 2001.95 meters of drilling have been carried out along with collection of 2400 core
                                                                                  
          samples. Further, Chirugora, Mednitar and Purdaha blocks in Purulia district have
          been successfully upgraded from G3 to G2 stage of exploration during field season
          2026-27. So far, any resource of REE has not been established from these blocks.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
               In accordance with the Mines and Mineral (Development and Regulations)
          Amendment Act, 2015 and subsequent amendments in Mineral (Evidence of Minerals
          and Content) Rules, 2015, GSI has been mandated to carry out exploration upto G2
          stage (general exploration).                                            
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (c): The auction of a mineral block depends on the quantum and grade of mineral
          resource established in that block. Blocks are considered for auction based on its
          mineral resources . As no resource of REE has so far been established for these
          blocks these have not been considered for auction.                      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (d): During auction process to enhance commercial attractiveness of blocks including
          REE blocks in the State of West Bengal, roadshows and outreach programmes are
          organised by the government with the objective of engaging prospective bidders and
          other stakeholders from the mining sector. These initiatives are intended to encourage
                                                                                  
          wider participation and provide a comprehensive understanding of the investment
          opportunities.
