                    RARE EARTH  PERMANENT  MANAGEMENT   SCHEME                    
                                                                                  
          460. SHRI N K PREMACHANDRAN:                                            
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of HEAVY INDUSTRIES be pleased to state:              
                                                                                  
          (a) whether the Union Government initiated action to promote the Rare Earth Permanent
          Management Scheme, if so, the details of action taken thereon;          
                                                                                  
          (b) whether the Union Government support the initiative of Government of Kerala for
          development of REPM Scheme in the State of Kerala, if so, the details of action taken thereon;
                                                                                  
          (c)) whether the Union Government initiated action to promote the Sale Linked Sensitive (SLS)
          for the production and marketing of permanent management, if so, the details of action taken
          thereon;                                                                
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (d) whether the Government fixed any time frame for starting the industries for development and
          production of Rare Earth Permanent Management, if so, the initiatives taken to start such
          industries in the State of Kerala; and                                  
                                                                                  
          (e) the details regarding the key features of the proposed Rare Earth Permanent Management
          Production in Kerala?                                                   
                                                                                  
                                      ANSWER                                      
                     THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR HEAVY INDUSTRIES                   
                        (SHRI BHUPATHIRAJU SRINIVASA VARMA)                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) to (e): The Union Cabinet has approved the Scheme to Promote Manufacturing of
          Sintered Rare Earth Permanent Magnet (REPM) with a financial outlay of Rs. 7,280 Crore on
          26th November, 2025. The said scheme was notified on 15th December, 2025. The scheme has
          provision to establish 6,000 metric tonnes per annum (MTPA) of integrated REPM
          manufacturing capacity in the country by 5 beneficiaries over a period of 7 years including 2
          years gestation period. The scheme is not region specific and supports manufacturing of REPM
          on pan India basis.
