                              UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 1474                         
                                 ANSWERED  ON 09.12.2025                          
                                                                                  
                         RARE EARTH MAGNET  INCENTIVE SCHEME                      
                                                                                  
          1474. SHRI SHASHANK MANI:                                               
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of HEAVY INDUSTRIES be pleased to state:              
                                                                                  
          (a) the number of projects sanctioned under the Rare Earth Magnet Incentive Scheme along with
          their locations and investment commitments;                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (b) the estimated employment potential generated through these projects; and
                                                                                  
          (c) whether any proposals from the State of Uttar Pradesh have been approved under the scheme?
                                                                                  
                                       ANSWER                                     
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR HEAVY INDUSTRIES                  
                         (SHRI BHUPATHIRAJU SRINIVASA VARMA)                      
                                                                                  
          (a):  The union Cabinet, Chaired by Hon’ble Prime Minister, approved the “Scheme to Promote
          Manufacturing of Sintered Rare Earth Permanent Magnet” with financial outlay of Rs. 7,280 Crore
          on 26th November, 2025. No project has been sanctioned under the scheme till date.
                                                                                  
          (b) & (c): Does not arise.
