                           UNSTARRED  QUESTION No. †2972

                    DELAY  IN AUCTION AND DECLINE IN PRODUCTION                   
                                                                                  
          †2972. SHRI AZAD KIRTI JHA:                                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) whether it is a fact that the supply of iron ore to various States in the country has
                                                                                  
          sharply declined from the companies over the last several years in Odisha having large
          share of India's iron ore reserves;                                     
          (b) whether this is due to expiry of mining leases, delay in new auctions and decline in
          production;                                                             
          (c) whether it is also a fact that companies in Odisha preferred to make exports
                                                                                  
          oriented due to increase in global prices, thereby affected domestic supply;
          (d) if so, whether the secondary steel industry across the country including West
          Bengal is in serious crisis and many units are on the verge of closure due to reduced
          iron ore supply;                                                        
                                                                                  
          (e) whether the Union Government is formulating any concrete policy to control or ban
          the export of iron ore so that priority can be given to the domestic industry; and
          (f) if so, the details thereof?                                         
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a): The production of iron ore in the country has increased over the years. The
          production of iron ore in the country during the last 5 years is given below:
                                                                                  
           Sl. No.       Year             Production (in Million Tonnes)          
                                                                                  
             1         2020-21                       205                          
             2         2021-22                       254                          
             3         2022-23                       258                          
             4         2023-24                       277                          
                                                                                  
             5         2024-25                       289                          
                                                                                  
          Further, iron ore production in the state of Odisha increased by 13% from 140.93
          million tonnes in 2022–23 to 159.22 million tonnes in 2024–25.          
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (b): So far, a total of 150 iron ore mines have been auctioned in the country out of
          which 40 mines are in production. As per the provisions of Section 8A(4) of the Mines
          and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957, on the expiry of the lease
                                                                                  
          period, the lease shall be put up for auction. Further, Section 8B of the Act provides
          that all valid rights, approvals, clearances, licences and the like granted to a lessee in
          respect of a mine shall continue to be valid even after expiry or termination of lease
          and such rights, approvals, clearances, licences and the like shall be transferred to
          and vested in the successful bidder of the mining lease selected through auction.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          There is no delay in auction of mineral blocks and production of iron ore in the country
          and in Odisha has increased.                                            
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (c) & (d): The production of crude steel in the country during 2024-25 was 112 million
          tonnes with an estimated iron ore requirement of 179 million tonnes. The production
          of iron ore in the country during 2024-25 was 289 million tonnes. Therefore, there is
          sufficient production of iron ore in the country to meet the demand of steel industry.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (e) & (f): In view of above, question does not arise.
