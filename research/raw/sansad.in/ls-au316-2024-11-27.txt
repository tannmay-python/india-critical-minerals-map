                                    LOK SABHA                                     
                            UNSTARRED QUESTION NO – 316                           
                              ANSWERED  ON 27.11.2024                             
                                                                                  
              MINE BLOCKS AUCTIONED  TO PRIVATE AND PUBLIC COMPANIES              
                                                                                  
          †316 SHRI SHYAMKUMAR DAULAT BARVE:                                      
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) the number of mine blocks auctioned in the country especially in Maharashtra since
          2015 till date as a result of mining reforms implemented by the Government;
                                                                                  
          (b) the details of mine blocks allotted to private and public companies since 2015 till
          date, State and company-wise;                                           
          (c) the number of mines that are operational after the mining reforms along with the
          names of investigation agencies notified for private investigation in the country, State-
                                                                                  
          wise especially in Maharashtra; and                                     
          (d) the details of increase or loss of revenue in the State where mines were auctioned
          to private companies, State/mine-wise?                                  
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
          (a) 435 mineral blocks have been auctioned in the country since the introduction of
                                                                                  
          auction regime in 2015. Of which 40 blocks have been auctioned in Maharashtra.
                                                                                  
          (b) State-wise details of mineral blocks allotted to private and public companies,
          through auction, since 2015 are available on the Ministry of Mines’ website.
          (https://mines.gov.in/webportal/content/successful-auction-since-2015). 
                                                                                  
          Apart from above, 24 blocks have been reserved for Central PSUs and State PSUs
          under section 17A of MMDR Act, 1957 since 2015. List at Annexure I.     
                                                                                  
          (c) 50 auctioned mineral blocks auctioned have been operationalised since the
                                                                                  
          introduction of auction regime in 2015. So far, 25 private exploration agencies have
          been notified in the country under section 4(1) of the MMDR Act, 1957. These
          agencies are eligible to take up exploration activities throughout the country including
          Maharashtra. List at Annexure II.                                       
                                                                                  
          (d) The revenue collection to the State Governments which has auctioned and
          operationalised mineral blocks has significantly increased. The details of pre-auction
                                                                                  
          and post-auction revenue collection are given at Annexure-III.
