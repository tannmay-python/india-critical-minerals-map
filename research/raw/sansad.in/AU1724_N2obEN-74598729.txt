                 MINERAL EXPLORATION BY GEOLOGICAL SURVEY  OF INDIA               
                                                                                  
          1724. SHRI ROBERT BRUCE C:                                              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) whether the Geological Survey of India has conducted mineral exploration in
                                                                                  
          Tirunelveli district and if so, the details thereof along with the findings including the
          funds sanctioned or released and the physical progress achieved during each of the
          last three years and if not, the reasons therefor; and                  
          (b) the details of the steps proposed to be taken by the Union Government in this
          regard for Tirunelveli district and the time-frame fixed therefor?      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                      ANSWER                                      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR COAL AND MINES                    
                            (SHRI SATISH CHANDRA DUBEY)                           
                                                                                  
          (a) and (b): The Geological Survey of India (GSI) has not conducted mineral
          exploration in Tirunelveli district during the last three years.        
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
               GSI had carried out mineral exploration programmes for various commodities
          such as Mica, Graphite, Limestone, etc. in Tirunelveli district till field season 2005-
          2006. These exploration projects did not yield significant outcome for any mineral
                                                                                  
          commodity and hence, mineral exploration was not taken up further in this district by
          GSI.
