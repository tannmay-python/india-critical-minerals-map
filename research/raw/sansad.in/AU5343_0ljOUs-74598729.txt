                            UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 5343                           
                              ANSWERED  ON 25.03.2026                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                MINING LEASES HELD BY PUBLIC AND PRIVATE COMPANIES                
                                                                                  
          5343. SHRI BAPI HALDAR:                                                 
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) the number of mining leases held by public sector companies and private
          companies in the country till date, State-wise;                         
          (b) the details of list of lease-holders, mineral mined, place of mining, name of the
                                                                                  
          mine and area along with year of grant, company-wise;                   
          (c) the number of leases that are operational and non-operational along with the
          reasons for non-operation, company-wise; and                            
          (d) the details of royalty and other revenues realised from each company during the
                                                                                  
          last five years, year-wise?                                             
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR COAL AND MINES                    
                                                                                  
                            (SHRI SATISH CHANDRA DUBEY)                           
                                                                                  
          (a) to (c): As per the information provided by Indian Bureau of Mines, State-wise details
          of mining leases of major minerals held by public and private companies are given at
                                                                                  
          Annexure-I. State-wise mineral-wise number of working and non-working mines are
          given at Annexure-II. The mines are currently non-working mainly due to non-
          availability of or non-compliance with statutory clearances, lack of availability of
          suitable grade mineral, uneconomic operations, court matters/ litigation, blocks falling
          under Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZs) or notified wildlife/ tiger corridors, and other factors
                                                                                  
          such as land acquisition and local issues.                              
                                                                                  
          (d): As per the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) (MMDR) Act, 1957
          and the Rules framed thereunder, grant of mineral concessions and collection of
                                                                                  
          revenues from the same fall within the purview of State Governments. As per the
          information provided by Indian Bureau of Mines, State-wise and year-wise details of
          revenue generated in the country during last five years is given at Annexure-III.
