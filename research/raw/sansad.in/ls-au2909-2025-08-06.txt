            EXPLORATION AND AUCTION OF CRITICAL AND STRATEGIC MINERALS            
                                                                                  
          2909. SHRI YADUVEER WADIYAR:                                            
                                                                                  
               SHRI KARAN BHUSHAN SINGH:                                          
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) the manner in which the Government has enhanced the exploration and auction of
          critical and strategic minerals such as lithium, cobalt and rare earth elements during
          the last year;                                                          
          (b) whether the Government has successfully auctioned any blocks under the new
          exploration licence regime; and                                         
                                                                                  
          (c) if so, the details thereof?                                         
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
          (a): The Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) (MMDR) Act, 1957
          amended with effect from 17.08.2023 has empowered the Central Government to
                                                                                  
          exclusively auction critical and strategic minerals like lithium, cobalt, and rare earth
          elements etc. During the last year (2024-25), 24 critical and strategic mineral blocks
          have been successfully auctioned by the Central Government.             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          To enhance exploration of critical and strategic minerals, National Mineral Exploration
          Trust (NMET) provides exploration incentives and also offers partial reimbursement of
          exploration expenses to holders of Exploration and Composite Licenses for critical and
          deep-seated minerals. During last year (2024-25), NMET has sanctioned 72 projects
          of critical and strategic minerals. Further, Geological Survey of India (GSI) has carried
                                                                                  
          out 195 exploration projects for critical and strategic minerals during last year (2024–
          25) in different parts of the country.                                  
                                                                                  
          (b) & (c): The Notice Inviting Tenders (NITs) for the 1st tranche of auction of 13 mineral
                                                                                  
          blocks for grant of Exploration Licence has been issued on 13th March, 2025.
