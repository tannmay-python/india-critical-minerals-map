                            UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 4038                           
                              ANSWERED  ON 12.08.2026                             
                                                                                  
               INDIA'S DEPENDENCE ON IMPORT FOR RARE EARTH MINERALS               
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          4038. MS. S JOTHIMANI:                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) whether the country is currently dependent on China for rare earth minerals and
          magnets used in electric vehicles, electronics and defence equipment and if so, the
          details thereof along with India's existing stock that can last if China tightens supply
          further;                                                                
                                                                                  
          (b) the details of the progress made under the Government's schemes to boost
          domestic mining and manufacturing of rare earth minerals including funds released
          and projects started so far;                                            
          (c) whether India's efforts to source rare earth minerals from other countries have
          started showing results and the manner in which this compares with the quantity still
                                                                                  
          being imported from China; and                                          
          (d) the details of Government's long-term plan to reduce India's dependence on a
          single country for such a critical resource?                            
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR COAL AND MINES                    
                            (SHRI SATISH CHANDRA DUBEY)                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) to (d): In FY 2025–26, China accounted for more than 30% of the total value of
          India’s imports of rare earth elements (REEs). In FY 2025-26, IREL (India) Limited
          under Atomic Mineral Directorate for Exploration & Research has reported 5.47 kilo
          tons production of rare earth compound.                                 
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Atomic Minerals Directorate for Exploration and Research (AMD), a constituent unit of
          the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE), carries out exploration for rare earth
          elements (REEs) along the coastal / inland / riverine placer sands of the country, which
          contain rare earth minerals such as monazite. As on date, AMD has established an
                                                                                  
          estimated resource of REEs as follows:                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
              Approximately 7.31 million tonne (Mt) in-situ Total Rare Earth Oxides
               Equivalent (TREO Eq.) contained in 13.30 Mt monazite resource, occurring in
               the coastal beach and teri, beach sand and inland alluvium in parts of Andhra
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
               Pradesh, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Maharashtra Jharkhand, West Bengal
               and Tamil Nadu.                                                    
                                                                                  
              1.30 Mt in-situ REE resource (TREO Eq.) in hard rock terrains in parts of
               Gujarat and Rajasthan.                                             
                                                                                  
          Additionally, Geological Survey of India (GSI) has also estimated resources of REE in
          hard rock across India.                                                 
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Further, in view of the need to strengthen capabilities and diversify sources of supply,
          enhance domestic processing, and reduce import dependence, the Government has
          taken several initiatives, which inter-alia include:                    
                                                                                  
               i) Approval of the National Critical Mineral Mission (NCMM) on 29 January, 2025
                                                                                  
               with the aim of secure a long-term sustainable supply of critical minerals
               (including REEs) and strengthen India’s critical mineral value chain
               encompassing all stages from mineral exploration and mining to beneficiation,
               processing, and recovery from end-of-life products.                
                                                                                  
               ii) NCMM envisages support for investment for acquisition of critical mineral
               assets to diversify sources of supply. Accordingly, to promote Indian public and
                                                                                  
               private sector entities to undertake mineral exploration in overseas jurisdictions,
               the Ministry of Mines circulated "Overseas Mineral Exploration Promotion
               Guidelines" on 16 July 2026.                                       
                                                                                  
               iii) Further, under NCMM, ₹500 crore has been earmarked to support domestic
               critical mineral processing capabilities by developing mineral processing parks
               using existing infrastructure to strengthen the domestic processing of critical
               minerals within the country and promoting local value addition.    
                                                                                  
               iv) Additionally, the Union Budget for FY 2026–27 has announced support to
                                                                                  
               the mineral-rich States of Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu to
               establish dedicated Rare Earth Corridors to promote mining, processing,
               research and manufacturing.                                        
                                                                                  
               v) The Ministry of Mines has given prior approval for 15 blocks [(Andhra
               Pradesh Mineral Development Corporation (APMDC) - 10, IREL (India) Limited
               - 4)] of Beach Sand Minerals and one block of hard rock REE deposit to Gujarat
               Mineral Development Corporation, Gujarat.                          
                                                                                  
               vi) India is also engaged with resource-rich countries through bilateral and
                                                                                  
               multilateral cooperation to strengthen access to critical minerals, including rare
               earth elements.
