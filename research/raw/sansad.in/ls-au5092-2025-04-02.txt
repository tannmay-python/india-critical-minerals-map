                                AUCTIONING OF MINES                               
                                                                                  
          †5092. SHRI BIDYUT BARAN MAHATO:                                        
               SHRI YOGENDER  CHANDOLIA:                                          
                                                                                  
               SHRI CHHATRAPAL SINGH GANGWAR:                                     
               SHRI DINESHBHAI MAKWANA:                                           
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) the number of mines auctioned in the country since the introduction of the Auction
                                                                                  
          Regime in the year 2015 till February, 2025;                            
          (b) the number of mines auctioned during the Financial Year 2024-25 till February,
          2025; and                                                               
          (c) the number of mines auctioned for mining lease during the financial year 2024-25
                                                                                  
          till February, 2025?                                                    
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) Since the introduction of auction regime in 2015, 457 major mineral blocks have
          auctioned across the country till February, 2025.                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (b) & (c) 103 major mineral blocks have been auctioned during the financial year
                                                                                  
          2024-25 till February, 2025 of which 57 blocks were auctioned for mining lease.
