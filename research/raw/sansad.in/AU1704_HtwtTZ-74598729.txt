             INCENTIVE SCHEME FOR QUICKER OPERATIONALISATION OF MINES             
                                                                                  
          1704. SHRI V K SREEKANDAN:                                              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) whether it is a fact that the Government has instituted an incentive scheme with an
          outlay of Rs.5,000 crore for financial year 2026-27 and if so, the details thereof;
          (b) whether it is also true that the aim of the scheme is for quicker operationalisation
                                                                                  
          of mines, readiness in placing mines for auction, governance and integration with the
          government's central monitoring portal and if so, the details thereof; and
          (c) whether the scheme also aims to incentivise States towards ensuring that mines
          are placed faster for auctions and if so, the details thereof?          
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR COAL AND MINES                    
                            (SHRI SATISH CHANDRA DUBEY)                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) to (c): Yes, an incentive scheme with an outlay of ₹5,000 crore has been
          incorporated under Part-V (Mining Sector Reforms) of the Scheme for Special
          Assistance to States for Capital Investment (SASCI) for financial year 2026–27. The
          Scheme comprises of three components viz. Component-I (Implementation of Mining
          Reforms), Component-II (Mine Operationalization) and Component-III (State Mining
                                                                                  
          Readiness Index-based Reforms).                                         
          The incentive scheme aims to facilitate and expedite faster mine operationalization
                                                                                  
          and improve overall governance of the mining sector by incentivizing States/ UTs (with
          legislature) to undertake reform measures including, inter-alia, State’s integration with
          Unified Mining Portal of Ministry of Mines for mine operationalization; constitution of
          pre-auction committee to resolve land scheduling issues for faster identification and
                                                                                  
          preparation of mineral blocks for auction; publication of annual auction calendar for
          major minerals to facilitate more active & transparent auction by the States; successful
          auctioning of major mineral blocks with pre-embedded clearances; operationalization
          of auctioned major mineral blocks as per prescribed target; and adoption of
          technology-based measures to prevent/                                   
                                                                                  
          detect grade misclassification of mineral ore.
