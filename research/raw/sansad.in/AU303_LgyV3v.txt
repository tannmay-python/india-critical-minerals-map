                  BOOSTING DOMESTIC PRODUCTION  OF RARE EARTH METALS              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
            †303. SHRI BABU SINGH KUSHWAHA:                                       
                                                                                  
            Will the Minister of HEAVY INDUSTRIES be pleased to state:            
                                                                                  
            (a) whether India has outlined a seven-year plan to boost domestic production of rare earth
            metals after China banned their exports;                              
                                                                                  
            (b) if so, the details of the main objectives of the scheme, timeline and implementing agencies;
                                                                                  
            (c) whether the Government proposes to formulate a dedicated policy to ensure indigenous
            supplies in sectors like defence, electronics and clean energy under the said scheme; and
                                                                                  
            (d) if not, whether the Government is considering any alternative strategies to reduce dependence
            on these sectors?                                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                        ANSWER                                    
                       THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR HEAVY INDUSTRIES                 
                          (SHRI BHUPATHIRAJU SRINIVASA VARMA)                     
                                                                                  
            (a) to (c): NITI Aayog & Indian Rare Earth Limited (IREL) under Department of Atomic
            Energy (DAE) has prepared a document on “Incentivisation framework for establishment of
            Downstream industry using Rare Earth Elements”. Further, IREL under DAE has set-up a Rare
            Earth Permanent Magnet (REPM) plant in the country for production of Samarian-Cobalt
            magnets from indigenous resources exclusively for use in defence & atomic energy sector.
                                                                                  
            (d): Does not arise.
