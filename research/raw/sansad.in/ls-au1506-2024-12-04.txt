                           AUCTION OF CRITICAL MINERALS                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          † 1506 SHRI PRADEEP KUMAR SINGH:                                        
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) whether auctions are being conducted for the extraction of important minerals like
          limestone, gold, iron ore, manganese; and                               
          (b) if so, the details thereof?                                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                                                                                  
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
          (a) & (b): Yes, auctions are being conducted since amendment in MMDR Act in 2015
          for major minerals including limestone, gold, iron ore, manganese etc. The Mines and
                                                                                  
          Minerals (Development and Regulation) (MMDR) Act, 1957 was amended through the
          MMDR  Amendment Act, 2015 with effect from 12.01.2015 whereby auction regime
          was introduced for grant of mineral concessions to bring in greater transparency and
          remove discretion. The method of auction also ensures that the State Governments
          get their fair share of revenue accruing from the auction process. Further, the MMDR
                                                                                  
          Amendment Act, 2023 has empowered the Central Government to exclusively auction
          mining leases for minerals specified as "critical and strategic" in Part D of the First
          Schedule of the Act.                                                    
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
               From 2015 to 31st March, 2021, 108 mineral blocks were auctioned. However,
          since 01.04.2021, the number of auctioned mineral blocks has increased more than
          threefold and 333 mineral blocks have been auctioned till date. Since 2015, 441
          mineral blocks have been auctioned across 15 States. The Central Government has
          auctioned 24 critical and strategic mineral blocks since amendment in the Act in
                                                                                  
          August 2023 till date. These auctions have been conducted across various states,
          including Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka,
          Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Telangana, and Uttar Pradesh.
          The auction of mineral blocks has led to significant increase in the revenue accrual to
                                                                                  
          the States.
