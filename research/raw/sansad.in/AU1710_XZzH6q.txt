                              RARE EARTH  CORRIDORS                               
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          1710. SMT. SANJNA JATAV                                                 
               SHRI KRISHNA PRASAD TENNETI                                        
                                                                                  
          Will the PRIME MINISTER be pleased to state:-                           
                                                                                  
          (a) the role assigned to Indian Rare Earths Limited (IREL) in the implementation of the
          proposed Rare Earth Corridor in the country particularly in Andhra Pradesh;
                                                                                  
          (b) the details of the projects undertaken by IREL for the processing and value addition of
          critical minerals during the last three years;                          
                                                                                  
          (c) the details of the investments made and employment generated through these projects; and
                                                                                  
          (d) the expected contribution of these initiatives towards achieving self-reliance in the critical
          minerals supply chain in the country?                                   
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
          THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR PERSONNEL, PUBLIC GRIEVANCES & PENSIONS AND   
                                                                                  
          PRIME MINISTER’S OFFICE (DR. JITENDRA SINGH)                            
                                                                                  
         (a)    The Union Budget 2026-27 made an announcement to support mineral-rich states of
                                                                                  
                Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu to establish dedicated Rare Earth
                corridors to promote mining, processing, research and manufacturing. In line with the
                                                                                  
                announcement, a Joint Working Group (JWG) comprising all stakeholder Ministries of
                Government of India and the four State Governments was formed for formulation of
                                                                                  
                guidelines for the same. IREL (India) Ltd. (IREL), a PSU under Department of Atomic
                Energy is part of the JWG formed.                                 
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
         (b & c) IREL has completed a project - “Rare Earth Permanent Magnet plant, Visakhapatnam”
                for strategic applications during the year 2024. The project cost was Rs. 196.50 crore
                                                                                  
                and the project has generated employment for 65 persons.          
                                                                                  
         (d)    The Projects undertaken by IREL will help in achieving the target set under the Scheme
                                                                                  
                to promote manufacturing of Sintered Rare Earth Permanent Magnets approved by
                Government of India in 2025.
