                   DOMESTIC PRODUCTION  OF RARE EARTH MINERALS                    
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          2922. SHRI VISHALDADA PRAKASHBAPU PATIL                                 
                                                                                  
          Will the PRIME MINISTER be pleased to state:-                           
                                                                                  
          (a) whether the Government is finalizing an incentive scheme worth Rs.3,500 to Rs.5,000 crore
          for boosting domestic production of rare earth minerals and magnets;    
                                                                                  
          (b) if so, the key objectives of the scheme and how it aims to reduce India’s reliance on critical
          imports, particularly from China;                                       
                                                                                  
          (c) the details of sectors that are expected to benefit from increased availability of rare earth
          minerals, such as electric mobility, electronics and renewable energy;  
                                                                                  
          (d) whether the scheme promotes public-private-partnerships and indigenous technology for
          rare earth extraction and magnet manufacturing; and                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (e) if so, the manner in which this initiative aligns with the Government’s broader vision of
          Atmanirbhar Bharat and strategic resource security?                     
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
          THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR PERSONNEL, PUBLIC GRIEVANCES & PENSIONS AND   
                                                                                  
          PRIME MINISTER’S OFFICE (DR. JITENDRA SINGH)                            
                                                                                  
         (a) to (e) As of now, there is no Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for domestic
                 production of rare earth minerals and magnets.
