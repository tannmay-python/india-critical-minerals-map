                RARE EARTH AND CRITICAL MINERALS IN ANDHRA PRADESH                
                                                                                  
          623. SHRI P V MIDHUN REDDY:                                             
                                                                                  
          Will the Minister of MINES be pleased to state:                         
                                                                                  
          (a) whether the Government has identified rare earth and critical mineral deposits in
                                                                                  
          coastal and tribal regions of Andhra Pradesh;                           
          (b) the details of exploration undertaken;                              
          (c) whether Private or PSUs have been granted mining rights, if so, the details thereof;
          and                                                                     
          (d) whether the Government is planning to establish a processing facility in the State,
                                                                                  
          if so, the details thereof?                                             
                                                                                  
                                     ANSWER                                       
                                                                                  
                           THE MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES                         
                               (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          (a) & (b): Yes. Since 2022-23, the Geological Survey of India, an attached office of the
          Ministry of Mines, has taken up 38 mineral exploration projects for critical mineral
          commodities such as molybdenum, graphite, cobalt, lithium, Phosphorite etc. in
          different districts of Andhra Pradesh including Vizianagaram, Nellore, Srikakulam,
                                                                                  
          West Godavari etc. and has augmented resources for critical minerals such as
          graphite, tungsten, cobalt and cadmium in East Godavari, Alluri Sitharama Raju,
          Anantapur, Vizianagaram and YSR (Kadapa) districts. The details are given at
          Annexure.                                                               
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Further, the National Mineral Exploration and Development Trust (NMEDT) has
          approved 17 mineral exploration projects in Andhra Pradesh which includes 03
          projects of critical minerals located in YSR (Kadapa), Prakasam, and Alluri Sitharama
          Raju districts.                                                         
                                                                                  
          (c): The Ministry of Mines has granted previous approval of Central Government
                                                                                  
          under Section 5(1) of the MMDR Act, 1957 for grant of mining lease for Beach Sand
          Minerals (BSM) in Andhra Pradesh for following blocks.
