                                  DEEP OCEAN MISSION                                
                                                                                    
             4032. SHRI BASAVARAJ BOMMAI:                                           
                  SMT. D K ARUNA:                                                   
                                                                                    
                  Will the Minister of EARTH SCIENCES be pleased to state:          
                                                                                    
             (a)  the details of the progress made in the development of indigenous deep-sea mining
                  technologies under the Deep Ocean Mission;                        
             (b)  the status of demonstration of the indigenous deep-sea mining machine VARAHA
                  including the depth and areas where field trials have been conducted;
             (c)  the details of roadmap for deep-sea resource assessment and commercial exploitation
                  of polymetallic nodules in the Indian Ocean; and                  
             (d)  the details of environmental safeguards put in place to ensure sustainable deep-sea
                  exploration and mining activities?                                
                                                                                    
                                       ANSWER                                       
                      THE MINISTER OF STATE (INDEPENDENT CHARGE) FOR                
                          MINISTRY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY                        
                                  AND EARTH SCIENCES                                
                                  (DR. JITENDRA SINGH)                              
                                                                                    
             (a & b) The National Institute of Ocean Technology (NIOT), Chennai, under the Ministry of
                  Earth Sciences (MoES), is entrusted with developing technologies for the reliable and
                  sustainable harvesting of polymetallic nodules from the deep ocean. A seabed mining
                  system has been developed, for which mobility and system-powering trials were
                  successfully undertaken at a depth of 5270 m at the Central Indian Ocean Basin
                  (CIOB) site allocated by the International Seabed Authority (ISA). In addition, NIOT
                  has undertaken the development of nodule collection and crushing components in
                  accordance with ISA guidelines.                                   
                                                                                    
             (c & d) Ministry of Earth Sciences has signed contracts with the International Seabed
                  Authority (ISA) for exploration of seabed minerals, namely Polymetallic Nodules
                  (PMN) and Polymetallic Sulphides (PMS) in the Indian Ocean in the areas beyond
                  National jurisdiction. The Resource Assessment and the Environmental Impact
                  Assessment studies are carried out as per the contractual obligations and guidelines set
                  out by the ISA. Multidisciplinary Oceanographic Studies are conducted in the Indian
                  contract areas under the exploration contract to establish baseline environmental
                  conditions and assess the possible effects of exploration activities on deep sea
                  ecosystems. Presently, only the activities related to exploration of deep seabed
                  minerals in the international seabed are permitted.
