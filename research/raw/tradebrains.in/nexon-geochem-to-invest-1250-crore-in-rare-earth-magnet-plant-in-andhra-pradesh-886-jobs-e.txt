Nexon Geochem to Invest ₹1,250 Crore in Rare-Earth Magnet Plant in Andhra Pradesh: 886 Jobs Expected - Where Is It Coming Up?
Nexon Geochem has proposed a ₹1,250 crore investment in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, for a rare-earth magnet manufacturing project. The proposal could generate 886 jobs and support the state’s advanced manufacturing ambitions.

Andhra Pradesh is looking to strengthen its advanced manufacturing sector with a proposed ₹1,250 crore investment by Nexon Geochem Private Limited in Kakinada. The Hyderabad-based company’s investment proposal was among the agreements announced at the regional conference held in Kakinada on October 12, 2026, ahead of the 31st CII Partnership Summit. The project has the potential to generate 886 jobs.

Nexon Geochem’s ₹1,250 Crore Investment in Kakinada
Nexon Geochem has proposed an investment of ₹1,250 crore in Kakinada, with the potential to create 886 jobs. The proposal was among 15 MoUs signed at the regional conference, which secured investment commitments worth ₹4,256.61 crore across the Godavari region. The proposed project is linked to the development of a rare-earth permanent magnet manufacturing facility at Kakinada Special Economic Zone (KSEZ). Kakinada SEZ Limited has previously stated that Nexon Geochem expressed interest in establishing an integrated neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) rare-earth permanent magnet manufacturing facility at the location. 
Why Is Kakinada Being Considered for the Project?
Kakinada offers access to an established industrial area, port infrastructure and road connectivity. The Kakinada Special Economic Zone (KSEZ), also being developed as Auro Industrial City, is planned as a port-linked industrial hub for manufacturing, logistics and trade. The industrial area has access to power infrastructure, water supply, internal roads and utility facilities. Its location could support companies that need industrial land and access to transport networks for moving raw materials and finished products.
Key Connectivity
Kakinada’s existing transport infrastructure and planned industrial facilities could support manufacturing and logistics operations in the region.

Port:
 Kakinada SEZ is approximately 30 km from the operational Kakinada Deep Water Seaport. The proposed Kakinada Gateway Port is being developed near the industrial zone.
Road:
 The industrial zone connects to NH 16 through the road network, linking the region to the Kolkata–Chennai corridor and other major centres along India’s eastern coast.
Rail:
 Kakinada Town and Kakinada Port stations serve the city, while Samalkot Junction provides access to the main railway network. A dedicated rail link to the proposed Gateway Port is also planned.
Airport:
 Rajahmundry Airport is approximately an hour’s drive from Kakinada SEZ, providing domestic air links to major cities.
These facilities could help manufacturers access suppliers and markets while supporting cargo movement to and from the industrial zone.

Andhra Pradesh’s Rare-Earth Mineral Resources
Andhra Pradesh has monazite-bearing beach sands containing rare-earth elements and thorium. The Department of Atomic Energy has also established a rare-earth permanent magnet facility at Visakhapatnam, with an annual production capacity of three tonnes of samarium-cobalt magnets. This facility is separate from Nexon Geochem’s proposed neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) magnet project at Kakinada. The company’s plans for sourcing raw materials have not been confirmed.
India’s Push for Rare-Earth Magnet Manufacturing
In November 2025, the Union Cabinet approved a ₹7,280 crore scheme to develop 6,000 metric tonnes per annum of integrated rare-earth permanent magnet manufacturing capacity in India. The initiative aims to strengthen domestic production of magnets used in electric vehicles, renewable energy and electronics. Nexon Geochem’s proposed facility could support this broader manufacturing push if implemented. However, its production capacity and participation in the central scheme have not been confirmed.
What Could the Investment Mean for Kakinada?
The proposed ₹1,250 crore investment could expand Kakinada’s industrial base and create new employment opportunities. However, the scale of these benefits will depend on the project progressing from the MoU stage to implementation and commercial operations.
