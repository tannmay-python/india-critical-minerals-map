Synopsis: 
Andhra Pradesh has given approval for two projects of manufacturing rare earth permanent magnets to be established by Proterial (India) Pvt. Ltd. and NaN MagneTech that would cost 
₹3,500 crore
. This will enable the country to build its own manufacturing capacity of rare earth magnets.
Advertisment
India has taken another important step toward becoming self-sufficient in the areas of critical minerals and manufacturing through the State Government of Andhra Pradesh approving two new 'greenfield' projects worth 
₹3,500 crore
 to manufacture rare earth permanent magnets. These projects, being developed by 
Proterial (India) Pvt Ltd and NaN MagneTech (India)
, will provide the country with necessary domestic capabilities for producing high performance magnetic materials that can be used in electric vehicles, renewable energy, defense, and advanced electronics. 
Andhra Pradesh Approves Two Rare Earth Magnet Projects 
The Government of Andhra Pradesh has notified G.O.Ms. No. 136 and G.O.Ms. No. 137 on June 25, 2026, which provides incentives for setting up green field rare earth permanent magnet manufacturing plants through its industrial promotion policy. In combination, these two projects are among the largest ever made in the rare earth magnet manufacturing industry in India, strengthening the reputation of Andhra Pradesh as an ideal location for advanced manufacturing and critical minerals-related investments.

Why Rare Earth Magnets Matter
Rare earth magnets are produced using rare earth materials, including 
neodymium, praseodymium, dysprosium, and terbium
. Of all these types of rare earth magnets, the 
Neodymium Iron Boron (NdFeB)
 is the most powerful commercial permanent magnet due to its high magnetic field strength, small size, and energy efficiency. Their high level of magnetism facilitates the production of light and energy-efficient motors and generators.These magnets are indispensable across several high-growth industries, including: 
Electric vehicle (EV) traction motors 
Wind turbine generators 
Industrial robotics and factory automation
Consumer electronics and smartphones 
Medical equipment
Defence and aerospace systems 
Semiconductor manufacturing equipment
High-efficiency industrial motors 
Advertisment
With demand for electric mobility and renewable energy rising rapidly, rare earth permanent magnets have become one of the world's most strategically important industrial components. 
Strategic Significance for India
The approval of these two projects marks an important step in strengthening India's critical minerals ecosystem and advanced manufacturing capabilities. Both projects are likely to provide several strategic advantages for Indian industrial ecosystem such as: 
Increase domestic production of high performance rare earth permanent magnets.
Decrease reliance on foreign magnets.
Enhance the critical minerals value chain in India.
Promote Make in India initiative and 
Atmanirbhar Bharat.
Generate around 
930 direct and indirect employment opportunities.
Promote the development of electric vehicles, renewable energy, defence, electronics, robotics and semiconductors.
Make Andhra Pradesh a centre of excellence for advanced materials and clean technology production
Future Outlook
As the global race for critical minerals and clean technologies accelerates, these investments will help establish a strong domestic industry of rare earth magnets, support India's strategic manufacturing sector, and propel Andhra Pradesh to become an important node in the global value chain for advanced manufacturing of these essential materials.
