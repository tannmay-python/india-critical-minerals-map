Govt Clears Rs 7,104 Crore Electronics Manufacturing Projects, Including India’s First Rare Earth Magnet Plant
Arjun Brij
Mar 30, 2026 | Updated 03:58 PM GMT+5:30
Electronics Manufacturing (Representative Image) 
The Union government has approved 29 projects worth Rs 7,104 crore under the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS), marking a major push to strengthen domestic electronics manufacturing and reduce reliance on imports. 
Among the approved proposals is India’s first rare earth permanent magnet manufacturing facility, which involves an investment of around Rs 700 crore.
Announcing the approvals on March 30, Union Minister for Electronics and Information Technology Ashwini Vaishnaw said the rare earth magnet project stands out for being built entirely on indigenous technology. “The entire technology of this company is their own… their own IP,” he said.
Rare earth magnets play a crucial role in several modern technologies, including electric vehicles, electronics, renewable energy systems and advanced industrial machinery. 
The government sees the upcoming facility as a strategic step towards building capabilities across the entire refined mineral-to-magnet supply chain, an area currently dominated by a handful of global producers.
The 29 projects cleared under the scheme cover 16 product segments within the electronics component ecosystem. These include lithium-ion battery cells, flexible printed circuit boards (PCBs), connectors and display modules. 
Officials estimate that the investments could generate more than 14,000 jobs while driving total production valued at nearly Rs 84,515 crore in the coming years. The programme is also expected to significantly increase localisation levels in key electronic components.
Government projections suggest domestic manufacturing could eventually meet about 25 per cent of the country’s demand for rare earth magnets. 
The initiative also targets localisation levels of around 50 per cent in printed circuit boards and roughly 61 per cent in lithium-ion battery components.
