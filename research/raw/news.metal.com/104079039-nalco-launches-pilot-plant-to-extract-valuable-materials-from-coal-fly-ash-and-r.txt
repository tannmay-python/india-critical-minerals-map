NALCO Launches Pilot Plant to Extract Valuable Materials from Coal Fly Ash and Red Mud
Published

Aug 26, 2026 09:18 (GMT+8)
Source
: 
nalcoindia.com
Share
Share
Save
[SMM Aluminum Express News] NALCO has inaugurated a 50 kg/day indigenous pilot plant at its Research & Technology Centre in Bhubaneswar to recover value-added materials from bituminous coal fly ash. Developed by CSIR-IMMT and KIIT with support from NALCO, CSIR and India’s Ministry of Mines, the process can recover alumina, calcium silicate, quartz residue and an iron hydroxide residue enriched in rare earth elements including scandium. NALCO also held a ground-breaking ceremony for a separate pilot plant focused on bauxite residue, or red mud, valorisation. The proposed facility will target recovery of ferro-titanium alloy, metallic iron and rare earth elements from red mud, supporting NALCO’s broader effort to turn alumina-refining residues into commercially valuable products and critical minerals.

【SMM Aluminum Flash News】Americas' 5-Series Aluminum FRP Demand Expands, North American Consumption to Reach 2.23Mmt
Demand for 5xxx-series aluminum flat-rolled products (FRP) across the Americas is projected to grow, supported by automotive lightweighting, shipbuilding and infrastructure investment. North American consumption is expected to increase from 1.79 million mt in 2025 to 2.23 million mt by 2032, representing growth of approximately 24.6%, with demand reaching around 2.10 million mt by 2030. Growth will be driven by US automotive and marine applications, alongside Canada's low-carbon aluminum industry and Mexico's expanding automotive manufacturing base.
Meanwhile, South American consumption is forecast to rise from 502,710 mt in 2025 to 602,020 mt by 2032, an increase of approximately 19.8%, with Brazil remaining the region's largest market. Automotive manufacturing, transportation and infrastructure investment are expected to support demand, although trade policies, raw material costs and downstream processing capacity could influence the pace of growth.
