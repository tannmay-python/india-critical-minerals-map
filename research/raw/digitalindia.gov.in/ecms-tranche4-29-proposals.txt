Government approves 29 more proposals under the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS)
In continuation to the approvals of 46 applications for Rs 54,567 crore announced earlier, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology 
(MeitY) has further
 approved 29 proposals
 under the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme 
(ECMS)
 with projected 
investment of Rs 7,104 
crore
, projected 
production of Rs 84,515 crore
. These approvals are expected to generate 
14,246 
direct employment
 opportunities.
