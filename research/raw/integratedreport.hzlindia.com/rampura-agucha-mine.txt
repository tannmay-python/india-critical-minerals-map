Mine Development
Rampura Agucha Mine
 (RAM), an ISO 9001,
 ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001, and
 SA-8000 certified mine, located
 in Bhilwara, Rajasthan, is the
 world’s largest zinc-producing
 mine and is the operational
 cornerstone of Hindustan Zinc’s
 production base. It is continually
 scaling its operations,
 infrastructure, technological
 superiority, sustainability focus,
 and safety credentials to set a
 global benchmark.
Operational and Strategic Developments
Ramping productivity and throughput
Achieved first-ever underground internal waste dumping with c.140 kt waste dumped directly into the open
 stopes, eliminating surface haulage costs
Enhanced safe and efficient working conditions at deeper levels by:
Commissioning a 27 MW refrigeration plant that delivers 400 m
3
/sec of cool air
Developing a 2 km vertical development to strengthen the ventilation network reticulation
Established three major pumping stations (at -355 mRL, -505 mRL, and -580 mRL) to achieve mine
 dewatering capacity up to 26,000 m
3
/day
Commissioned three level-to-level ore passes (from -505 mRL to -530 mRL and -455 mRL to -480 mRL) to
 decongest traffic in active levels
Enhanced skip payload from 20 MT to 22 MT, increasing throughput from 630 TPH to 660 TPH, while
 optimising power consumption
Optimised drill factor from 8.2 MT/m to 8.5 MT/m through stope drill design optimisation
Developments at Stream-1
Enhanced lead cleaner capacity through an indigenously developed flotation cell to increase
 retention time, improving lead and silver recovery by c.1%
Expanded multi-stream analyser (MSA) for enhanced operational monitoring, and refurbished zinc
 filter (PF-980), resulting in additional mined output of 300 MT of lead and 0.5 MT of silver
Technology and digital integration
Deployed computer vision-based rock fragmentation and foreign material detection to enable real-time ore
 quality monitoring, reduce downstream damage risk, and improve crushing efficiency
Installed 86 internet of things (IoT) sensors across stream 1, 2, and 3 mills and blower mills for
 real-time velocity and temperature monitoring
Integrated RIPIK.AI with the distributed control system (DCS) for AI-powered optimisation in consumables
 usage; model fine-tuning is currently in progress
