                              UNSTARRED  QUESTION NO.4612                         
                                                                                  
                                FOR ANSWER ON 22/12/2014                          
                                                                                  
                      JOINT VENTURE IN STEEL INDUSTRY                             
                                                                                  
            4612. SHRI N.K. PREMACHANDRAN:                                        
                                                                                  
                 Will the Minister of STEEL be pleased to state:                  
                                                                                  
            (a)  whether the Government/SAIL proposes to set up a Titanium Sponge Factory
            at Chavara Kollam, Kerala in a joint venture;                         
                                                                                  
            (b)  if so, the details and the status thereof; and                   
                                                                                  
            (c)  the time by which the said factory is likely to be started?      
                                                                                  
                                       ANSWER                                     
                                                                                  
            THE MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF   (SHRI VISHNU DEO SAI)      
            STEEL AND MINES                                                       
                                                                                  
            (a)to(c): Yes, Madam. A Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed on
            04.04.2013 amongst SAIL and Kerala State Industrial Development Corporation Ltd.
            (KSIDC) & Kerala Minerals and Metals Ltd. (KMML) on behalf of Government of
            Kerala (GoK) to jointly explore possibilities of setting up a Titanium Sponge and
            Metal Complex in Kerala in Joint Venture in a phased manner.          
                                                                                  
                 A Joint Committee of SAIL and GoK, constituted to carry out a feasibility study
            of the technical and financial viability of the Titanium Sponge Project (10,000 Metric
            Tonne Per Annum Capacity), has completed the preliminary study based on a
            Techno Economic Feasibility Report (TEFR) prepared by MECON. According to the
            TEFR, the financial viability of the project depends upon the latest cost effective
            technology, which is still not available in India. Efforts to obtain transfer of technology
            from leading global supplies has not been very encouraging and further course of
            action is dependent upon availability of Titanium production technology.
