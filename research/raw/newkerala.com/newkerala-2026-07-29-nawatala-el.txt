Rajasthan Secures India's First Rare Earth Elements Exploration Licence
Rajasthan has executed India's first Rare Earth Elements Exploration Licence, a landmark move for strategic mineral security. The licence, under Chief Minister Bhajan Lal Sharma, covers 207.63 sq km in the Navatala-Devigarh region. Geological investigations indicate presence of key elements like Lanthanum, Cerium, Praseodymium, and Neodymium. This initiative supports the Centre's Atmanirbhar Bharat vision by enhancing domestic critical mineral availability.

Jaipur, July 29
Rajasthan has achieved a major milestone in India's critical minerals sector by executing the country's first Rare Earth Elements Exploration Licence, marking a significant step towards strengthening the nation's strategic mineral security.
"Scientific exploration of these critical minerals would encourage private investment while strengthening India's supply chain for strategic minerals. - Aparna Arora"
The landmark initiative has been undertaken under the leadership of Chief Minister Bhajan Lal Sharma, who also holds the Mines portfolio.
The Department of Mines and Geology executed the country's first exploration licence for Rare Earth Elements and associated minerals, reaffirming Rajasthan's position as a leader in the mining sector.
The state already ranks first in India in the auction of major mineral blocks and has now become the first to execute an REE exploration licence awarded through a transparent auction process.
The licence covers a 207.63-square-kilometre area in the Navatala-Devigarh region, spanning Pachpadra tehsil in Balotra district and Shergarh tehsil in Jodhpur district.
Geological investigations have indicated the presence of key Rare Earth Elements, including Lanthanum, Cerium, Praseodymium and Neodymium, all of which are critical for advanced technology and strategic industries.
To accelerate exploration and mining of critical minerals, the Central government launched auctions for exploration licences in 2025 following policy reforms initiated in 2024.
In the first phase of these auctions, M/s Central Mine Planning and Design Institute Limited (CMPDI) emerged as the successful bidder for the Rajasthan block, after which the state government granted the exploration licence.
The agreement was formally executed by Director (Mines) Mahavir Prasad Meena, Additional Director (Headquarters) Mahesh Mathur, and Vikrant Gupta, Head of Business Development at CMPDI, in the presence of senior officials from the Department of Mines and Geology.
Additional Chief Secretary (Mines) Aparna Arora said the execution of the country's first auctioned REE exploration licence demonstrates Rajasthan's commitment to transparent and investment-friendly mineral governance.
She noted that scientific exploration of these critical minerals would encourage private investment while strengthening India's supply chain for strategic minerals.
Rare Earth Elements are indispensable for industries such as electric vehicles, defence, aerospace, renewable energy, semiconductors, electronics, telecommunications, and other high-tech manufacturing sectors.
The successful execution of the exploration licence is expected to support the Centre's vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat by enhancing domestic availability of critical minerals and reducing dependence on imports.
With this pioneering initiative, Rajasthan has once again reinforced its position at the forefront of India's mining sector and opened a new chapter in the country's exploration of strategically important mineral resources.
— IANS
