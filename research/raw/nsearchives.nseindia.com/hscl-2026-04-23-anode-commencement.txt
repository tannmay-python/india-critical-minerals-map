             Sub: Commencement of first anode material production                   
                                                                                    
             Dear Sir / Madam,                                                      
             This is in continuation of our earlier communication dated 06 December 2023 regarding production of
             of Lithium-ion battery (LiB) components, in phases with total annual production capacity of 200,000
             MT.                                                                    
                                                                                    
             Pursuant to Regulation 30(6) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
             Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), read with Schedule III thereto, we hereby inform that
             the Company has achieved a significant milestone with the commencement of operations at its first
             anode material production facility at Mahistikry, Hooghly, West Bengal, with an initial installed
             capacity 200 MTPA.                                                     
             The facility is the outcome of over 10 years of in-house research and development, covering the entire
             anode technology value chain—from raw material processing to finished anode material. This
             development strengthens the Company’s differentiated capabilities in advanced battery materials,
             positioning it to address the accelerating global demand arising from electric vehicles (EVs) and energy
             storage systems.                                                       
                                                                                    
             The process has been designed to accommodate alternative raw material feeds, ensuring scalability and
             long-term resilience as the market evolves.                            
                                                                                    
             The said capacity addition is expected to further strengthen the Company’s portfolio and contribute to
             its production capabilities and revenue growth going forward.
