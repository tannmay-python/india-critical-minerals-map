Waaree to Set Up 16 GWh Lithium-Ion Battery Gigafactory in Andhra Pradesh
The facility will be set up with an investment of ₹81.75 billion
February 19, 2026
/
Melvin Mathew
/
Energy Storage
, 
Press Releases
Follow
Mercom India on WhatsApp for exclusive updates on clean energy news and insights
Waaree Energies
 has announced plans to set up a 16 GWh integrated lithium-ion battery manufacturing plant in Rambili, Anakapalli, Andhra Pradesh, with an investment of ₹81.75 billion (~$901.26 million).
The integrated greenfield lithium-ion manufacturing plant will cover the entire battery value chain, including cell manufacturing, battery packs, and large-scale battery energy storage systems.
The project has received in-principle approval from the Andhra Pradesh State Investment Promotion Board.
Last year, Waaree had raised over ₹20 billion (~$220.49 million) from investors. The proceeds were intended to be used for battery cell and pack manufacturing, scaling containerized battery energy storage systems, and setting up a lithium-ion advanced-chemistry storage cell manufacturing plant.
In November 2025, Waaree Energy Storage Systems, the battery energy storage arm of Waaree Energies, 
raised
 ₹3.25 billion (~$36.65 million) as part of a ₹10 billion (~$112.78 million) funding round led by investment firm Niveshaay.
The same year, Waaree Energy Storage Solutions also 
secured
 ₹10.03 billion (~$111.16 million) in funding from a group of strategic investors, including family offices, high-net-worth individuals, and institutional backers.
Waaree Energies also 
invested
 ₹3 billion (~$33.79 million) in its wholly owned subsidiary, Waaree Energy Storage Solutions, through a rights issue. The funding was intended to be used for the lithium-ion storage cell plant.
In Q3 FY 2026,  Waaree Energies’ revenue from operations 
rose
 118.8% year-over-year (YoY) to ₹75.65 billion (~$826.77 million) from ₹34.57 billion (~$377.84 million). The company attributed the revenue increase to strong module sales. Its module sales rose 124.9% YoY to ₹69.9 billion (~$763.92 million) from ₹31.08 billion (~$339.71 million).
In January 2025, the Andhra Pradesh government 
greenlighted
 several renewable energy projects totaling ₹158 billion (~$1.75 billion) at the 14th State Investment Promotion Board meeting. The approvals for renewable energy projects were part of the ₹193.91 billion (~$2.15 billion) investment proposals cleared in the meeting.
