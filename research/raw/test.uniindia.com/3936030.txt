Telangana's Singareni begins gold, copper exploration in Karnataka's Devadurga block
Hyderabad, Aug 7 (UNI) State-owned Singareni Collieries Company Limited (SCCL) has formally commenced exploration of gold and copper deposits in Karnataka's Devadurga Block after signing an agreement with the Karnataka Department of Mines and Geology (DMG).
The agreement was signed in Bengaluru by Karnataka DMG Director Rangappa and SCCL Director (Projects & Planning) K. Venkateshwarlu, marking the start of exploration activities in the 199.13 sq. km block spread across Raichur and Yadgir districts, the mining company said in a release here today. 
SCCL had earlier secured the exploration licence for the block through the Government of India's mineral auction process. 
Under the agreement, SCCL will complete exploration of the Devadurga Block within five years. 
Speaking on the occasion, K. Venkateshwarlu said the agreement marks the formal launch of exploration operations after the company secured the licence. He said key officials had already been appointed for the project and the remaining personnel would be deployed shortly. 
He said SCCL aims to complete the G4 stage exploration within the next two years. 
Describing it as a milestone, Venkateshwarlu said this is the first time in SCCL's history that it is undertaking mineral exploration of this scale outside Telangana. Drawing on the company's more than 130 years of geological and exploration experience, he expressed confidence of successfully completing the project. 
He also thanked the Karnataka Government and the Department of Mines and Geology for their support. 
As part of the exploration programme, SCCL will undertake remote sensing, geological mapping and geophysical surveys, followed by scout drilling at selected locations. 
Samples collected during exploration will undergo petrographic, mineralogical and elemental analysis at SCCL's newly established Geo Science Laboratory at Kothagudem. 
Based on the findings, the company will submit a detailed exploration report to the Karnataka Department of Mines and Geology, which will form the basis for advanced stages of mineral exploration in the block. 
Senior officials of SCCL and the Karnataka Department of Mines and Geology were present at the agreement-signing ceremony.
